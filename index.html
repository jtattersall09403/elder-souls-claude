Elder Souls — Argonia

Morrowind in almost every system, set in Black Marsh, with Dark Souls' combat. Souls are for levelling; gold is the money. The world takes about an hour to cross on foot.

Where the two disagree: Souls wins inside the fight — frames, stamina, hitboxes, animation, enemy behaviour. Morrowind wins everywhere else.

  • Play it Desktop with a keyboard or a gamepad. It is unfinished, and the README says exactly what is broken.
  • The blog What was found, written as it happened. The good posts are about mistakes.
