**This is a target, not a verdict.** W1-30K writes down what a frame must land in; W1-30V decides
whether a frame did. The two are separate children on purpose, and no artefact in this directory
may enter a judging pack — `validate-board.mjs` check C6 enforces that.

**This half cites the 2002 reference plates and the negative anchor and nothing else.** Every
plate path behind every number below sits under: `anti-generic/`, `morrowind/REF-A1/`, `morrowind/REF-A10/`, `morrowind/REF-A11/`, `morrowind/REF-A13/`, `morrowind/REF-A16/`, `morrowind/REF-A17/`, `morrowind/REF-A18/`, `morrowind/REF-A19/`, `morrowind/REF-A2/`, `morrowind/REF-A21-regions/`, `morrowind/REF-A22-intraregion/`, `morrowind/REF-A23-ground/`, `morrowind/REF-A3/`, `morrowind/REF-A5/`, `morrowind/REF-A6/`, `morrowind/REF-A8/`, `morrowind/REF-A9/`.
RI-VIS01's CC-3 is the cardinal sin and cross-citation is how it happens, so the split is
structural: this file is generated from rows whose `side` is `ART`, and a row citing across the
line fails check C2 before anyone reads it.

The grant this half operates under is RI-VIS09 §3.3, verbatim in scope: **palette, silhouette,
built form, flora and creature design language** — and never texture resolution, texel density,
anti-aliasing or sharpness. Nothing below measures any of those four.

## 1. The three decisions, made once instead of eleven times

These are the rows worth attacking. The rest of the board is arithmetic in support of them.

### 1.1 The value structure — the sky is the light

| row | what is measured | range | anchor (p50) | n | consumed by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ART-X1-VALUE-SPLIT` | mean CIELAB L* of the top third minus the bottom third of the frame (L* units) | -2.81 … 44.14 | 15.85 | 90 | W1-30A, W1-30B, W1-30D, W1-30F |
| `ART-X4-SKY-FRACTION` | fraction of frame height above the detected skyline, averaged over columns (fraction) | 0.217 … 0.853 | 0.405 | 90 | W1-30B, W1-30E, W1-30F |
| `ART-X5-SKYLINE-RELIEF` | standard deviation of the smoothed per-column skyline height, as a fraction of frame height (fraction of frame height) | 0.085 … 0.274 | 0.175 | 90 | W1-30E, W1-30F |

The reference set puts the top third of the frame **15.85 L\* brighter**
than the bottom third at the median, and never inverts by more than
2.81 L\*. A frame whose ground out-values its sky has
either lit the world from below or lost its sky, and both read instantly.

`ART-X5-SKYLINE-RELIEF` is the cheapest single check in this document and it exists for a reason:
**a dead-flat horizon measures 0.000 and nothing in the reference set is anywhere near 0.000.** The
floor of the measured band is 0.085 of frame height.

### 1.2 The saturation ceiling — the most common way this subject goes wrong

| row | what is measured | range | anchor (p50) | n | consumed by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ART-X2-CHROMA-P95` | 95th-percentile CIELAB chroma C* of the frame (C*) | 7.11 … 34.64 | 18.32 | 165 | W1-30A, W1-30C, W1-30D, W1-30E, W1-30F, W1-30G |
| `ART-X2-CHROMA-FRAC` | fraction of frame pixels with C* > 30 (fraction) | 0 … 0.115 | 0 | 165 | W1-30A, W1-30C, W1-30D, W1-30G |
| `ART-X3-PALETTE-BREADTH` | count of coarse CIELAB cube bins (dL 10, da 20, db 20) holding >= 1% of frame pixels (bins) | 5 … 19 | 12 | 165 | W1-30A, W1-30C, W1-30F |

The measured reference set is far more desaturated than a marsh is usually painted: the 95th
percentile of frame chroma sits at 18.32 C\* at the median, and
the median frame carries **0.0%** of its pixels above C\*=30, and the
90th-percentile frame only 11.5%.
High chroma in this world is a *light source*, not a surface.

> One plate had to be thrown out to get this number, and it is worth naming. `REF-A20` is the
> main-menu and title art — a painted 2D asset rather than a rendered world frame — and it measures
> C\* 29.9–45.8 against 2.3–29.1 for every rendered exterior slot in the set. Including it would
> have roughly doubled the ceiling off four non-world plates. The full exclusion list with reasons
> is `slot_routing.excluded` in `board.json`.

### 1.3 The strangeness budget — distance from the thing we are not

| row | what is measured | range | anchor (p50) | n | consumed by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ART-X6-STRANGENESS-RAW` | Euclidean distance from the centroid of refs/anti-generic/ in the 4x4 mean-CIELAB descriptor (CIELAB descriptor units) | ≥ 59.75 | 91.51 | 165 | W1-30A, W1-30B, W1-30C, W1-30D, W1-30E, W1-30F, W1-30G |
| `ART-X6-STRANGENESS-LAYOUT` | Euclidean distance from the centroid of refs/anti-generic/ in the per-image z-scored 6x4 edge+lightness layout descriptor (layout descriptor units) | ≥ 116.7 | 135.55 | 165 | W1-30A, W1-30B, W1-30C, W1-30D, W1-30E, W1-30F, W1-30G |

RI-VIS07 permits `refs/anti-generic/` only as the thing to measure distance **from**, never as a
target, and these two rows use it exactly that way: a floor with no upper bound. There is no such
thing as too far. The floor is the 10th percentile of the reference set's *own* distance from that
centroid — our frames must be at least as far from generic fantasy as Vvardenfell's are.

The second row is the one that cannot be satisfied by a colour grade, because its descriptor is
z-scored per image: absolute colour, absolute exposure and global contrast are gone before the
distance is taken, and only silhouette, prop density and composition survive.

**How to read the ordering.** The values are quantiles of the measured reference distribution. The
*order* — which region gets the low end and which the high — is read from data the corpus already
owns and is not a taste call made here: lightness and chroma follow the mean CIELAB of the three
`palette_hex` swatches `corpus/50-world/regions.json` already declares per region, and skyline
relief follows `corpus/50-world/landforms.json`'s `measured_relief_range_m` for the shipped tree.
Every row carries its rank, its ordering value and what would overturn it in `board.json`.

**The colour rows are necessary and not sufficient, and the board says so with a measurement.** On
this corpus the reference set **cannot identify its own regions from colour alone**: leave-one-out
region identification came out at
9.1% against
11.1% chance on the mean-CIELAB
descriptor, with a Fisher ratio of 0.964. So
`ART-X7-SEPARATION-RAW` ships **unset**: a separation floor read off a population that fails its
own test is noise wearing a number. The colour-stripped descriptor does better —
27.3% against the same chance —
and that row is the one that ships set:

Each settlement is anchored on one reference slot chosen for **built form** — the use RI-VIS09 §3.3

from one picture — four of the thirty-two settlement rows do.

## 5. Provenance

Every number above is a quantile of a statistic computed by
`docs/art-direction/measure-plates.mjs` over a named list of plate paths, recorded per row in
`board.json` under `source.plates`. Re-derive any of them:

```sh
node docs/art-direction/measure-plates.mjs      # 313 plates -> plate-metrics.json
node docs/art-direction/build-board.mjs         # plate-metrics.json -> board.json
node docs/art-direction/emit-docs.mjs           # board.json -> this file
node docs/art-direction/validate-board.mjs --self-test
```

Population for this half: 165 exterior plates, of which
90 are composition-valid
(`framing == "full frame, native aspect, uncropped" AND manifest sky_visible == true`), plus
5 negative-anchor plates. The composition rule matters: RI-VIS09 §3.3
records that the square crop destroyed original framing on most of the preview set, so those plates
ground palette rows and are excluded from every composition row.

**Caveats that travel with these numbers.** `vanilla_confidence` is `medium` at best on the
preview set and `pixel_metrics_valid` is `false` on all of it; the screenshot population is
skewed toward the picturesque, which the manifest's own `sampling_bias_note` states and which
biases dispersion *upward*, making any floor derived from it harder rather than easier; and no image
in the set is two-host corroborated.
