**This half cites the modern reference set and nothing else.** Every plate path behind every number
below sits under: `modern/character_closeup/`, `modern/combat/`, `modern/exterior_daylight/`, `modern/exterior_lowlight/`, `modern/interior_darkemissive/`, `modern/material_closeup/`. Every one is HUD-free: RI-VIS09
§2 forbids scoring a render against a HUD-bearing frame, so this half never reads one, and the
`ui/` population is layout reference rather than a look target and is excluded too.

**Two rows are on this side that the plan expected on the other.** RI-VIS01 §A routes lighting model
to F03 and atmospheric scattering to F06, so a key-to-fill ratio and a fog density are FIDELITY
properties whatever a look board would like them to be. The art half keeps only the part of them
that is genuinely a grading decision — the tint direction of the far field. This is the one place
where the plan for this piece was wrong about its own bifurcation and the rule won.

**`FID-DYNRANGE-*`** is the statistic RI-VIS09 §4 found separates the two eras with no overlap.
It is on this side for exactly that reason and cannot be set by any plate from the other one.

**`FID-AERIAL-*`** is the checkable consequence of a fog profile: local contrast in the bottom
sixth of the frame divided by local contrast just under the skyline. A renderer with no aerial
perspective returns ≈ 1.0 — foreground and far field carrying identical local contrast — and the
measured daylight band puts the median at
1.269. This is the row a fog profile is aimed at,
because it is the row a critic can check without knowing what the fog density was.

**`FID-SHADOWLIT-*`** is deliberately *not* called a key-to-fill ratio. See §2.

## 2. What this half could not measure

| row | why it is unset | what would fill it |
|---|---|---|
| `FID-KEYFILL` | A key-to-fill ratio is the ratio of two named light sources. A screenshot contains their SUM. Recovering the ratio needs either a known probe object of known albedo in frame or the light rig itself, and refs/modern/ has neither. Every "key-to-fill" number derivable from these plates would be a lit-to-shadow luminance ratio wearing the wrong name, so the honest statistic ships under its own name as FID-SHADOWLIT-* and this row stays empty. | a reference frame containing a grey sphere or chart of known albedo, or the light rig of a reference scene. Neither is acquirable from a screenshot population. |
| `FID-HORIZON-DISTANCE` | Metres are not in a screenshot. No plate in refs/ carries a depth channel, a camera intrinsic or a scale object, so no distance in metres can be read from any of them. The measurable consequence of a fog profile is the contrast falloff between foreground and far field, and that ships as FID-AERIAL-*. | a reference capture with a recorded depth buffer or a known-size object at a known distance. Our own build can supply both, so this row is fillable from OUR frames without any reference at all — W1-30B can set it from the engine and W1-30K should not. |

**Caveats.** These bands are p10–p90 of small populations — the largest is
16 frames — drawn from few titles and few
sessions. They are indicative envelopes, not authorities, and a row with n < 10 should be read as a
direction rather than a bar. Nothing here amends RI-VIS03: its bands remain
`provenance: constructed` and the standing prohibition in RI-VIS09 §4a against publishing a band as
measured on the strength of the nearest-equivalent statistic is not touched by this document.

**The two halves are never combined.** RI-VIS01 CC-5. `ART-X2-CHROMA-P95` and `FID-CHROMA-*` are
the same statistic on two populations and they are two different facts, not one fact measured twice.
