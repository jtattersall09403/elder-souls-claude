## 2. The Fisher 3.01 → 1.005 question, resolved

I was given two readings and told to determine which is true. **Neither is, and the honest answer
is a fourth thing.** All numbers below are from `critic-dispersion-likeforlike.json` and
`critic-structure-only.json`, produced by me over both populations with the same code.

### (1) The runs are not comparable, and I can say exactly why

The 1.005 pools **day, night and worst-weather** frames into one class per region. Doing that
doubles intra-region scatter and collapses inter-centroid distance, because a region's centroid
becomes the average of a bright frame and a dark one.

| population | n | inter | intra | **Fisher** | LOO | chance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ours **pooled** (the 1.005 headline) | 117 | 64.58 | 64.25 | **1.005** | 43.6% | 7.7% |
| ours **day** | 39 | 99.95 | 32.24 | **3.10** | 74.4% | 7.7% |
| ours **worst weather** | 39 | 85.53 | 24.21 | **3.53** | 82.0% | 7.7% |
| ours **night** | 39 | 33.63 | 16.92 | **1.99** | 33.3% | 7.7% |
| Morrowind REF-A21 | 39 | 86.09 | 60.74 | 1.417 | 25.6% | 11.1% |

Against round 1's 3.01 / 66.7% on 39 day frames, **the day world got better, not worse**: 3.10 /
74.4%. Nothing regressed.

### (2) "Not like-for-like" is not a defence, so I built the like-for-like

Morrowind's set is 9 regions × 4.33 images at chance 11.1%; ours is 13 × 9 at chance 7.7%. Those
Fishers were never comparable either. Matched design — **9 regions, 3 images each, 200 random
draws, both populations, identical chance**:

```
  matched ours_day     Fisher 3.087 +/- 0.322    LOO 77.5%   (chance 11.1%)
  matched ours_pooled  Fisher 1.536 +/- 0.312    LOO 35.9%
  matched morrowind    Fisher 1.614 +/- 0.113    LOO 24.2%
```

**A7 passes, decisively, on the like-for-like design.** And note where `ours_pooled` lands:
1.536 ± 0.312 against Morrowind's 1.614 ± 0.113 — *statistically indistinguishable*. The
"we are below Morrowind" reading survives only if our night frames are pooled and theirs are not.
Morrowind's own set is not single-condition (mean L 35.7 ± 14.6, min 5.5), but it is 39
opportunistic screenshots, not a deliberate one-third night sample.

### (3) The bad number is real; it is just not the one on the label

What the pooled Fisher is actually reporting is the **night pass**, shipped for the first time:

> **`ours_night` leave-one-out = 33.3%** (raw descriptor) / **25.6%** (exposure removed)
> against **RI-WLD04 M17 step 6's explicit `night accuracy ≥ 70% required`.**

The item's own sentence is *"a region that is only identifiable in clear daylight is half-built."*
That is the finding. Half of the world had never been measured; it was measured, and it failed by a
factor of two. The `1.005` is the shadow that failure casts on a statistic never designed to be
pooled.

### (4) And the instrument is measuring the wrong thing, which neither reading anticipated

A 4×4 grid of mean CIELAB is satisfied by a colour grade — RI-WLD04's own named way to lose
(*"Palette without material. Getting the hex values right and applying them as a colour grade over
the same ground texture"*). I stripped colour and exposure entirely and kept only silhouette and
composition (6×4 Sobel edge density + 6×4 luminance layout, z-scored per image):

| | with colour | **structure only** | above chance retained |
|---|---|---|---|
| ours_day | LOO 74.4%, Fisher 3.10 | **LOO 30.8%, Fisher 1.77** | 35% |
| Morrowind | LOO 25.6%, Fisher 1.42 | LOO 17.9%, Fisher 0.87 | 47% |

**About two-thirds of our measured region separability is carried by tint alone.** That is why the
machine reads 74.4% and a human taking the same test reads 50% — and it is why the biggest gap in
this verdict is region identity and not the road.

## 4. THE GAP: thirteen regions are one place, painted thirteen colours

`GAP-W1-world-region-identity-is-a-tint` · `world.region.identity` · **blocking**

Every region in `game/data/world/regions.json` carries the **identical prop schema**:

```
region            | canopy  | under | rock | only_here (declared instances)
blackwood         | sphere  | frond | yes  | welkynd_pillar (34)
clay-moor         | dome    | blade | yes  | naga_kiln_dome (26)
crimson-coast     | none    | crust | yes  | open_dye_vat (18)
deep-marshes      | spire   | blade | yes  | voriplasm (40)
eastern-rootlands | sphere  | frond | yes  | swamp_jelly_canopy (48)
hive              | dome    | comb  | yes  | comb_cliff (30)
marauders-coast   | arch    | blade | yes  | beached_hull_house (12)
salt-hills        | cone    | blade | yes  | imperial_milestone (22)
stone-forest      | column  | shelf | yes  | petrified_bole (400)
stone-wastes      | spire   | crust | yes  | glassed_crater (16)
thornmarsh        | cone    | blade | yes  | knife_mark_stem (60)
valus-ridge       | spire   | frond | yes  | rock_flute_spire (14)
western-rootlands | arch    | blade | yes  | root_arch (120)

distinct props key sets across all 13 regions:  ["canopy+rock+under"]   <- one
distinct canopy shapes: 7 for 13 regions.  distinct under shapes: 5 for 13 regions.
```

Three primitives, seven canopy shapes and five ground shapes shared thirteen ways, plus a colour.

**And not one ONLY-HERE element exists.** Those 34 welkynd pillars, 400 petrified boles, 14 rock
flutes, 16 glassed craters and 12 beached hulls are integer literals in
`tools/world/build-regions.mjs` lines 50–218. Nothing places an instance. `RI-WLD04` M19 measures
**0 of 13**.

The whole 14.31 km² province renders with:

```
textureMB 0 · atlasCount 0 · materials 17 · programs 8 · shadowLights 1 · skinnedMeshes 0 · entitiesLive 0
```

There is not a texture in the province. Albedo *cannot* come from different materials, because
there is one flat colour per surface — which is `RI-WLD04`'s "Palette without material" bullet in
its most literal possible form.

And `region-axes.mjs@2`, the rebuilt M18, says the same thing in its own words:

```
  slope_histogram 52/78 · elevation_profile 58/78 · water_regime 62/78 · substrate_mix 64/78
  ground_material_lab 77/78 · flora_density_placed 65/78 · flora_silhouette_placed 78/78
  rendered_palette 73/78 · rendered_vertical_structure 76/78
  fauna DROPPED · architecture DROPPED · audio DROPPED · weather DROPPED · hazard DROPPED
  minimum axes differing = 6   [PASS] M18-DIFFERENTIATION
```

M18 passes at the ≥6 bar with three pairs at exactly 6 — and **five of the item's nine declared
axes are dropped rather than measured**, which is an honest statement that fauna, architecture,
audio, weather and hazard do not exist in the build. Nine axes minus five is not nine.

**Why it matters.** S24 rules that thirteen regions must each read as a *different place*, and that
an implementation where everything is one terrain with a recoloured fog has failed regardless of how
good the terrain is. A player walking the 6.6 km crossing passes through five regions that differ in
ground colour and in nothing they can name.

### Remedy

1. Extend the region prop schema in `tools/world/build-regions.mjs` and `game/src/world/province.js`
   beyond `canopy+under+rock` with a fourth slot, `signature`, carrying its own mesh generator per
   region: a welkynd pillar for Blackwood, a rock-flute spire for Valus Ridge, a whale-bone frame and
   crater rim for the Stone Wastes, a beached hull house and tide pole for Marauder's Coast, a
   drowned xanmeer tooth and corpse lily for the Deep Marshes, a stilt row and tideway marker for the
   Eastern Rootlands.
2. Place them at the counts `regions.json` **already declares**, from the same seeded lattice the
   existing props use, so the number in the table becomes the number in the world.
3. Add `signature_silhouette` and `architecture_placed` axes to `tools/world/region-axes.mjs`,
   computed from **placed instances** rather than from a table row, and un-drop `architecture`.
4. Give the ground more than one material: `textureMB` is 0 today.

**Acceptance.**
- `RI-WLD04` M19 reads **13/13**: each region's ONLY-HERE element is queryable in the running world
  at **≥8 instances inside its own region and 0 in every other**.
- The structure-only re-measurement (`critic-structure-only.json`, colour and exposure removed)
  rises from **30.8% to ≥55%** leave-one-out on the 39 day frames — region identity must survive
  having the tint taken away.
- M18 measures **≥7 axes on all 78 pairs** with `architecture` un-dropped and computed from placed
  instances.
