## 2. Does the race reaction matrix actually fire in the build?

**Partly. The numbers do; the world does not.**

**What fires.** `__HARNESS.getReaction` against the running build reproduces `RI-CHR02` §4a exactly:

| | race | upbringing | disposition | band |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| A | Saxhleel | interior | **74** | friendly |
| B | Saxhleel | lukiul | 48 | neutral |
| C | Imperial | foreign-born | 24 | cold |
| D | Dunmer | foreign-born | **6** | hostile |
| E | Dunmer | lukiul | **0** (clamped) | hostile floor |

68 points, four bands, on identical stats. The matrix itself is **authored, not generated**: full
12 × 10 with no nulls, range −40…+14, σ over the 120 cells = **9.894**, all 12 rows distinct, all 10
columns distinct, and `RG-COURT` genuinely all zeroes. Aggregate Saxhleel − Dunmer = **11.750**.
Prices move live: a 60 g draught quotes 54 / 69 / 76 against the item's 54 / 70 / 76 (max deviation
1.4%), sells 66 / 52 / 47 exactly. Guard terms give 7 distinct law factors and a Naga : Imperial
loiter ratio of **2.511**, inside the required 2.2–2.8.

That is a real matrix and it is the best-built thing in the piece.

**What does not fire.** Every one of those consequences is a **pure oracle function exposed to the
harness**. Nothing in the running world consumes any of them:

- `writ-house` — the piece's own interior — instantiates **0 entities**. There is no NPC whose
  disposition could be derived.
- The three states the methods name by id do not exist: `helstrom-market` (prices),
  `stormhold-street` (guards), `rootlands-well-graph` (The Spilled's respawn). Of the 28 loadable
  states, only `writ-house` and `wld-dres-raid-road` are this piece's.
- **4 greeting lines** ship, in three disposition pools with `faction: null` and no
  `player_race_class` key at all, against `RI-CHR02` §4c's floor of **1,500 across 300 cells**.
- **81 topic records** ship. **0** carry `requires.race`. **0** carry `forbids.race`. The floor is
  90 and 30.
- **0** race-gated rumours (floor 16). **8** slavery-vocabulary hits in all dialogue (floor 60
  lines, of which ≥ 12 addressed to a Dunmer player).
- `getPriceQuote` takes `skill_buy_mult` as a **caller-supplied parameter**; it derives no
  Mercantile or PERSONALITY term. A Dunmer with Mercantile 100 and PERSONALITY 60 quotes
  `buyMult 1.264` — identical to an untrained Dunmer. The par clause holds only when I feed the
  0.80 myself.

`RI-CHR02`'s dialogue-volume axis is therefore at its stated **0** band, and since the item
aggregates min-over-axes, its native score is 0.

### The AR-3 claim: 10/12, not 12/12

I ran the item's literal command for all three races at seed 1337 and then ran **the builder's own

### Escalation: the difference never becomes a fight

Doctrine §2.1 step 4 — not *"is there a difference"* but *"does the difference change how I play"*.

Across all three 1,800-frame runs the player takes **zero damage** and the party never closes:
distance to the lead raider is frozen at **18.20 m from f900 to f1800**. After the Saxhleel AGGRO
the six raiders sit in `REPOSITION` for **1,657 consecutive frames**.

I then stood a Saxhleel **3 m** from the party and stepped 899 frames. All six report AGGRO at f0;
then 899 frames of `REPOSITION`; player HP 620 → 620; **0 `attack_start` events**; the net-throwers
never throw a net. The control — `cmb-duel-infantry` on the same build — produces 21 `attack_start`,
4 `HIT`, 2 `STAGGER` and 1 `DEATH` in 3,600 frames, so the combat system fires; **this encounter's
members simply never act.**

So `capture` and `kill` are strings in a trace event's `net_behaviour` field. There is no capture,
no net, no transport to Archon's holds, and no fight. The `AGGRO` is an annotation on an alert-state
channel. Race changes *what the trace says about* who would attack you; nothing attacks you.

## 7. The single biggest gap

### `GAP-W1-creation-is-an-api-not-a-scene` — `journey.chargen.diegesis`, blocking

**What.** The entire Writ House creation sequence renders nothing. Nineteen census nodes,
twenty byte-identical 1920×1080 PNGs (`sha256 7277a273…`), zero NPC entities, zero characters of
rendered text. `getCensusState()` self-reports `world_visible: true` and `full_screen_panels: 0` —
fields with no rendered counterpart.

**Why it matters.** A player starting this game is never asked anything by anybody: the best-written
material in the piece is delivered to no one, so character creation cannot be experienced at all.

**Remedy.**

1. In `game/data/states/writ-house.json`, place the two NPC entities the scene already names —
   `jeeh-ei` in the barge hold, `warden-scribe-tuleeh-ma` behind the desk — as real entities with
   positions and their `npcs/writ-house.json` records attached.
2. Add a dialogue surface under `game/src/render/` that draws `getCensusState()`'s `line`,
   `input.kind` and `input.options` as a bottom-anchored panel over the live 3D view, occupying
   ≤ 55% of frame area, driven by real input on the closed action set so keyboard, gamepad and touch
   all work.
3. Move the camera to the interior named in `getCensusState().interior` for the scene's duration.
4. Render `readWrit()` as an openable inventory object rather than an API string.

**Acceptance** (re-run `tools/harness/critic-w1-07a.mjs`): the 19 creation-node screenshots yield
**≥ 15 distinct sha256 hashes** rather than 1; `listEntities()` returns ≥ 1 NPC entity at every node
and that entity's id equals `getCensusState().speaker` at the node where the speaker talks; the
`line` appears as rendered text in the frame at every node with opaque non-world UI ≤ 55% of frame
area and 0 frames uniform over ≥ 90%; and the whole sequence completes on keyboard only and on
gamepad only. `RI-JRN01` M5/M6 then become measurable and the item can leave 0.
