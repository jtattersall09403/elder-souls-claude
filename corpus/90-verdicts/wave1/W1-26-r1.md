## 1. `DTR_scene` over all nineteen nodes — the brief's first question, answered

The graph ships **twenty** nodes (`game/data/dialogue/topics/writ-house.json`). Seventeen carry
input and I walked all seventeen, across **all three class routes**, forcing both conditional
nodes; the other three auto-advance and their lines are carried into the next node's `spoken`,
where they are measured. Coverage before this verdict: the builder's probe reached **3** node ids
and said so; the `W1-07-r3` critic's reached **11** (questionnaire route only). Nobody had ever
visited `writ.class-named`, the four `writ.class-custom-*` pick nodes, or `writ.birthsign-second`.

| Denominator | Draw policy | `DTR_scene` | `DTR_q` | Worst node |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| `getCensusModel()` — **the builder's** | scrolled | **0.9585** | **1.0000** | 0.75 @ `writ.stamp` |
| `getCensusState()` + writ | scrolled | **0.8923** | 0.8500 | **0.3333 @ `writ.given-name`** |
| `getCensusState()` + writ | **on arrival** | **0.7744** | 0.8500 | 0.3333 @ `writ.given-name` |

**The builder's headline generalises, and the unreached nodes were not the flattering ones.** On
its own denominator and its own draw policy the all-nodes figure is **0.9585**, slightly *above*
the 0.9500 it reported over three. The custom route — which I expected to be the ugly one, because
`writ.class-custom-primary` offers nineteen skills through a nine-option window — comes back at
**1.0000** on every pick node once the caret is paged, because the caret-resync fix shipped this
round works. That is a real repair and it holds everywhere.

**The disagreement is not coverage, it is the denominator.** Measured against what the *census*
computes rather than what `buildCensusModel()` kept, the same walk reads **0.8923** — below the
0.90 bar — and **0.7744** on arrival, before the player has touched anything. The difference
between the two columns is precisely the strings the model builder drops before the surface ever
sees them, which is the exact layer round 2's defect lived in.

Three races, three race×upbringing-specific lines, all computed, all correct, **none drawn**.
`RI-CHR01` §3 exists for that line — *"a game that permits an odd combination and then never
notices it has permitted nothing"* — and the game notices in the model and says nothing on the
frame.

**On the reading.** `HF1` says *"`DTR` < 0.50 on any **node class**"*, and M1 defines `DTR` at
three granularities. Per node, `writ.given-name` = 0.3333 and HF1 fires. Aggregated by node *kind*,
the `text` class is **0.5000 exactly** and it does not. I take the per-node reading because **it is
the reading the builder itself used to clear HF1** (*"worst node hold.hatch-name at 0.6667 … so HF1
does NOT fire"*), and a threshold cannot be read one way to clear it and another way to clear it

### The pattern, which is the real finding

`hold.wake`'s line — *"You are awake. Good. They do not like waking you at the desk, it makes the

Everything the scribe **asks** is drawn. Everything she **says back** is what goes. `render/ui.js`

response to the player lives. **A scene that asks perfectly and never answers is a form with good
questions on it**, which is the failure `RI-JRN09` was written to name.

## 2. Does the accessor have its sibling's blind spot? — **Yes, and it is structural**

The `W1-07-r3` critic found the equivalent accessor blind to a third surface. At HEAD, on this
build, it still is, and I falsified it rather than inferring it:

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Which of the renderer's three 2D contexts are instrumented | `{dialogue: true, **menus: false**, title: true}` |
| Instrument `menus` at runtime through the register's own public `instrument()`, force a repaint | **0 entries** |
| **Falsification** — same freshly-instrumented context, two draw paths | `glyphs.drawText()` sentinel: **not seen, 0 rows.** `ctx.fillText()` sentinel: **seen, 1 row.** |
| Strings the HUD surface actually draws in the opening | `"5"`, `"0"`, `"LIGHT"`, `"Spark-Dart"`, **`"Take A tithe-gourd, empty"`** |
| M9 as the build ran it | **0 strings searched, 0 hits, reported as a pass** |
| M9 over its real domain | **5 strings searched, 1 hit** |

`render/renderer.js:90` carries the comment *"Every 2D surface this renderer owns is registered
against the one rendered-text register"* and then instruments two of three. The third,
`this.menus = new UISurface(...)`, **is M9's domain** — *"every string rendered outside a
dialogue/journal/book surface"*. And the hole cannot be closed by calling `instrument()`:
`ui/glyphs.js drawText()` renders every glyph as a stroked `Path` (`moveTo` / `lineTo` /
`quadraticCurveTo` / `stroke`) and never calls `fillText` or `strokeText`, which is what the
register wraps.

`reports/journeys/w1-26-opening.json` records
`m9.surfaces_searched: "title (the only non-dialogue drawn surface in the build)"` — **false** —
with `distinct_searched: 0` and `hits: []`, and reports M9 as scoreable for the first time on that
basis.

This is `RI-JRN01` **How-we-lose #15** — *"the instrument is blind and the grep comes back
clean"* — **reproduced one layer down by the fix written to close it**, and it is strictly worse
than what it replaced: an empty accessibility tree announced its own emptiness, while
`getRenderedText()` returns twelve confident strings and a summary block.

Per §0.1(a), **M9 and M15 score `unmeasurable ⇒ 0`.** And M9 fails on the substance too:
`"Take A tithe-gourd, empty"` is an imperative second-person instruction drawn on the HUD after 120
frames of walking in the hold. **HF3 fires. AR-2 fails** on its instruction clause.

## 7. The single biggest gap

**`GAP-W1-rendered-text-accessor-blind-to-the-hud-surface`** · `journey.opening.legibility` ·
**blocking**

> The build's rendered-text accessor is structurally incapable of seeing the one surface
> `RI-JRN01` M9 and M15 are aimed at, and the build's own report records a pass over an empty set
> as a result.

Not a missing feature — **a wrong number that looks right**, on the piece's own declared path, in
the instrument the whole area was gated on. §0.1(a) exists because a canvas-only build returned an
empty accessibility tree and a grep over an empty set read as a clean pass; the fix was to demand a
*named* accessor demonstrated non-empty. This build supplied one, demonstrated it non-empty **on a
different surface**, and bought the same false pass one layer down.

**Remedy (S).** Give `ui/glyphs.js drawText()` a register call of its own — it already holds the
string, `x`, `y`, the face and the size at the top of the function — or route `UISurface.el()`'s
`text` records through a shim the register wraps. Make `getRenderedText()` derive
`surfaces_instrumented` from the contexts actually wrapped instead of a static array, and make
`tools/harness/w1-26-opening.mjs` **exit non-zero when its M9 grep searches zero strings** — a
probe that cannot go red is worse than no probe.

**Acceptance.** On a frame where `UISurface.elements[].text` is non-empty,
`getRenderedText({notSurface:['dialogue']})` returns a set that **contains every one of those
strings** (set equality, not merely non-empty), with clip flags; M9 and M15 then return 0 hits over
it; and the same run falsifies itself by drawing a sentinel through `glyphs.drawText()` and
asserting the register sees it.

## 8. Method, and where I was wrong

Every number here was taken at HEAD `8744ead`, in a browser, by four probes I wrote
(`critic-w1-26-r1a2/b/c/d.mjs`, all in the artifacts directory). **No screenshot is cited anywhere
in this verdict**: the opening is a journey, `ARBITRATION` **S34(b)** forbids a placed capture as
evidence of arrival, and rather than argue the boundary case by case I took every claim from the
harness and the draw-call register. The one figure S34(a) *would* have let me place — M5's
opaque-area pixel readback — is cited from the sibling's independent readback and marked as such.

**Two traps caught my own instrument before it was right, and both will catch the next critic.**

1. `UILayer` repaints only when `dirty`, and `getCensusState()` **and** `censusAnswer()` both call
   `metrics()`, which repaints and clears the flag. A probe that calls `renderedTextClear()` *after*
   reading state measures an empty register and reports `DTR 0.00` for a scene that drew everything
   — my first run did exactly that at eleven nodes. Use a watermark:
   `getRenderedText({}).next_index` taken **before** the repainting call, then `{since: mark}`.
2. `render/ui.js wrap()` splits prose on whitespace, **drops the space**, and hands each fragment to
   its own `fillText`. A delivery test that joins register rows with a space and substring-matches
   therefore fails on every wrapped string and **invents undrawn text**. It understated my
   `DTR_scene` by 0.067 and my `DTR_q` by 0.133, and would have made me report six of the ten
   dilemma questions as undrawn when all ten are drawn. Strip all whitespace from both sides
   instead. **The builder's probe, the `W1-07-r3` critic's probe and my first two drafts all used
   the space-joined test, so every `DTR` figure published before this verdict is an understatement
   of unknown size.**

Both corrections moved the numbers **in the build's favour**, and I would rather say that than have
it found later.

**Conditions.** Load average 17–22 on four cores throughout, above the ~12 `AGENT-PROTOCOL` names as
the distortion threshold. Nothing here is a wall-clock timing, so I do not believe any number is
affected; what load cost was **coverage** — my first DTR probe rendered a WebGL frame per node and
had to be killed at 900 s, and M10 went unmeasured because I ran out of budget.

**Not measured, named rather than assumed:** `RI-JRN01` M10 (inscriptions) — 0, my omission; M5, M6
and the 240-run `NAMED` sweep — cited from `W1-07-r3`; M13's touch leg — not re-run; M1/M2/M3/M12/M14
and M16–M19 — `corpus_debt`, because I did not run `journey-run.mjs` and will not report a number I
did not take.
