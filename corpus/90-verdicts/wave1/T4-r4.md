## The single biggest gap

**`GAP-W1-ui-boxes-were-shrunk-without-re-pitching-what-is-inside-them`.**

**What.** The per-screen boxes landed by round 4 are smaller than the content laid out into them, and
the content was not re-pitched to match, so at 1920×1080 on the shipped `ui-journal` fixture:
**9 of 10 level-up attribute rows** and **all ten character-sheet rows** draw the attribute name, its
value and its gauge into the same pixels; **8 of 19 journal entry blocks** are laid out past the
bottom of their own panel (worst 105 px, onto the world) and **11 of 19** over the foot hint; the
**container** and **spells** foot hints are cut mid-word at the panel edge; two of five quest-index
names and at least one carried item name are ellipsised. `RI-UIX06` **FD4**'s hard fail is *"any
clipping, overlap or overflow at 1920×1080"*.

**Why it matters.** The screen where the player spends souls cannot be read, and the journal — the
one document this game asks the player to actually read — prints its prose over its own instructions
and off the edge of the paper. It also means `RI-UIX09`'s `D2` rose *partly because* ink is stacked
on ink: level-up went 0.0910 → 0.1855 while becoming illegible, so the density metric is not a safe
thing to optimise alone.

**Evidence.**
`corpus/90-verdicts/wave1/artifacts/T4-r4c/crops/levelup-rows-6x.png` ·
`…/crops/sheet-panel-2x.png` · `…/crops/journal-panel-plus-margin-2x.png` ·
`…/crops/container-hint-3x.png` · `…/crops/spells-panel-2x.png` ·
`…/crops/control-levelup-1x.png` (delete-the-fix, legible) ·
`…/reports/t4-r4-overlap.json` · `…/reports/t4-r4-critic.json` ·
`…/reports/frame-liveness-head.json` (8/8 LIVE).

**Remedy — concrete, and it does not undo S56.**

1. **`game/src/ui/screens/progress.js`, `drawLevelUp()` and `drawSheet()`.** Lay the attribute row out
   as three disjoint fractions of its own rect instead of the old fixed offsets: a name column, a
   right-aligned value column, and a gauge that starts at `x ≥ name_w + value_w`. **Done when**
   `node tools/ui/t4-r4-critic-overlap.mjs` reports
   `rows_with_fewer_than_two_separators: 0` on `levelup` **and** `sheet` (currently 9 and 3), and the
   6× crop shows ten readable `NAME  value  ▮▮▮` rows.
2. **`game/src/ui/screens/text.js`, `drawJournal()`.** The paging loop breaks a column only when
   `curH + b.h > colH && cur.length` — so a block taller than `colH` is placed anyway and overflows.
   Either split a block across columns, or reduce the entry's own height to fit (`colH` is
   `ih - 30*s` = 294 units and a wrapped entry at `colW` 140 px is ~397). **Done when** the `JP5`
