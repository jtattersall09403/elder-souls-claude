### The number does not turn on the argument

A reader may reasonably object that scoring the whole piece 0 for a model a *merge* orphaned is
severe. **It changes nothing, and that is deliberate.** Strike `RI-MTH07` out of the table and the
min is **5.0**, set by `RI-QST03`'s rank ceiling; the verdict is FAIL against a 7.0 gate either way.
Both numbers are published so the clamp can be argued with separately from the judgement — the same
courtesy `W1-20-r1` extended over its own cap.

### The null control, which is the plausible wrong answer and not the trivial one

The trivial control for a faction system is "no factions at all", and it proves nothing. The control
run here is **every faction accepts everyone and refuses nobody, with all eight ladders, every rank
name, every favoured-skill set, every exclusivity declaration and every line of refusal prose left
exactly as shipped.** Only the four requirement columns and the world-state column of
`game/data/quests/faction-gates.json` are flattened to zero.

**That build passes the entire static census** — 8 ladders, 8 rows each, complete columns, monotonic
thresholds (0 ≤ 0 ≤ 0), favoured sets intact, 144 quests, every rank band populated, discipline rows
present. A structural census cannot tell it from the shipping build. This is the point of the
control, and it is why *statistics can fail this build and can never pass it.*

### What is genuinely good, and must not be lost in a zero

**Round 1's biggest gap is closed, and closed properly.** Its headline was *"eight eight-rank
ladders; three lines carry 18 quests each and five carry between 1 and 4 … you join the Rootkeepers,
and the Rootkeepers never ask you for anything again."* At `HEAD`: **every one of the eight lines
carries 18 quests across all eight rank bands**, with deceit, dirty work, politics and a succession
quest. Five hard fails became zero. That work landed on 08-10 and nobody has said so until now.

**Round 1's error of fact #1 is fixed.** It found each joining quest paying 20 reputation against a
cited cap of +4..+10, because quest-level and resolution-level awards were summed. At `HEAD` the
quest-level award is gone from all four: `Q-ROOT-00`, `Q-VAKH-00`, `Q-CORT-00` and `Q-DOCK-00`
declare `{}` at quest level and `10` on each resolution. It pays 10.

**Exclusivity is not decorative.** Joining the Wet Ledger locks three factions and **54 quests**;
joining the Assize locks two and 36. Better, the locks exceed what the line *declares* — the Ledger
declares one enemy and locks three — because the earned-rivalry model is live rather than authored.
And `QuestEngine.open()` genuinely refuses: *"The Imperial Assize will not deal with you: you are
The Wet Ledger."*

**The refusals are the best prose in the project.** *"The margin has your name in it 0 times and the
next line wants 10. The Ledger remembers people by the margin. Give it more to remember."* And when
you buy the reputation and nothing else: *"Root-Speech at 25. We ask 70 of anyone who walks where
you are asking to walk, and the ones who went short are still out there."* Eight institutions, eight
voices, every figure filled from `FactionGates.evaluate()` at speak time so the prose cannot drift
from the rule. Round 1 ruled that construction correct; it is still correct, and it now covers eight
lines instead of seven.

**Expulsion and readmission work end to end**, which is rarer than it sounds — `RI-QST03`'s own "How
we lose" predicts *"we implement the expelled flag and then let the player rejoin instantly."* Here:
*"The table takes a bond or it takes nothing. You are 1200 short of one."*
