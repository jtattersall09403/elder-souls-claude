**It stands. It is not void and it needs no amendment. What is stale is not the verdict but the
ledger that still quotes it as the state of W1-20.** This was the cheapest available answer and it
is the one the dates give, so it is the one written here.

The dispatch's premise — *"its live faction instrument and its delivery report had been silently
deleted from the branch before the critic read them"* — **is not what happened, and the correction
matters because it moves the damage somewhere nobody has looked.** The chronology, from the branch:

The round-1 critic could not have read the recovered material because **it did not exist for another

What the deletion actually cost is **round two**. The builder's continuation is a point-by-point
answer to `W1-20-r1` — its row 1 rebuilds the ladders the verdict hard-failed, its row 6 removes the

### And the recovery is two files short, which is the finding of Part 1

`RECOVERY-20260814` restored what a file-level sweep can see: files absent from the tip. `091a6cec`
also **reverted parts of two files that still exist**, and no file-level sweep can see that.

1. **`game/src/engine.js` — the whole world side of the registry.** Three lines added by `9ed28905`
   and absent from `HEAD`: the loader branch `else if (entry.path === 'factions/registry.json') out.factionRegistry = doc;`,
   the install `this.factionRegistry = this.data.factionRegistry || { factions: [] };`, and the
   19-line `Engine.factionAccess()` that reads it. `git log -S factionAccess -- game/src/engine.js`
   returns exactly one commit, `9ed28905`, and the symbol is not at `HEAD`.
2. **`orchestration/status/W1-20.json`** — reverted from the continuation's
   `builder-delivery-complete` record (with ten added `files_touched` entries and a `next_step` of
   *"Fresh independent build critic: execute the NOT_RUN prose/judge and four-bearing reachability
   rows"*) back to the pre-continuation `complete`.

**You do not need git forensics to see the first one. Boot the game and the harness verb calls a
method that is not there:**

```

    at Object.factionAccess (game/src/harness/api.js:2165:46)
```

`harness/api.js`'s one-line wrapper survived; the engine method under it did not. That is the exact
shape of the damage: a merge that took the *other* side's `engine.js` wholesale and kept everything

**Ruling, reversible.** `W1-20-r1` stands as written and is not superseded on its own commit.
`W1-20-r2` — this document — is the current verdict of W1-20. What would overturn the "stands"

### The number does not turn on the argument

A reader may reasonably want to argue that scoring the whole piece 0 for a model a *merge* orphaned
is severe. **It does not matter to the outcome, and that is deliberate**: strike `RI-MTH07` out of
the table entirely and the min is **5.0**, set by `RI-QST03`'s rank ceiling, and the verdict is still
FAIL against a gate of 7.0. Both numbers are published so the cap can be argued with separately from
the judgement — the same courtesy `W1-20-r1` extended over its own cap.

### The null control, which is the plausible wrong answer and not the trivial one

The trivial control for a faction system is "no factions at all", and it proves nothing. The control
run here is **every faction accepts everyone and refuses nobody, with all eight ladders, every rank
name, every favoured-skill set, every exclusivity declaration and every line of refusal prose left
exactly as shipped.** Only the four requirement columns and the world-state column are flattened to
zero, in `game/data/quests/faction-gates.json`.

That build **passes the entire static census** — 8 ladders, 8 rows each, complete columns, monotonic
thresholds (0 ≤ 0 ≤ 0), favoured sets intact, 144 quests, every rank band populated. A structural
census cannot tell it from the shipping build. The live arm can, and the two arms are reported
side by side in `NULL_CONTROL_TABLE`.

The control was built as a hard-linked clone (`HAZARDS` §5a): `game/` and `tools/` linked,
`faction-gates.json` unlinked and rewritten. The repository's own `faction-gates.json` was hashed
before and after and is byte-identical. **`tools/control-clone.mjs` could not build it** — it
refuses a symlink and `tools/node_modules/.bin/` is full of them — so the clone was made by hand
with `cp -al`; that is a real gap in a tool the hazards file recommends, and it is reported rather
than worked around silently.

### What is genuinely good, and must not be lost in a zero

**Round 1's biggest gap is closed, and closed properly.** Its headline was
*"eight eight-rank ladders; three lines carry 18 quests each and five carry between 1 and 4 … you
join the Rootkeepers, and the Rootkeepers never ask you for anything again."* Measured at `HEAD`:
**every one of the eight lines carries 18 quests across all eight rank bands**, with deceit, dirty
work, politics and a succession quest. Five hard fails became zero.

**Round 1's error of fact #1 is fixed.** Its finding was that each joining quest paid 20 reputation
against a cited cap of +4..+10, because quest-level and resolution-level awards were summed. At
`HEAD` the quest-level award is gone from all four: `Q-ROOT-00`, `Q-VAKH-00`, `Q-CORT-00`,
`Q-DOCK-00` each declare `{}` at quest level and `10` on each resolution. It pays 10.

**Exclusivity is not decorative.** Joining the Wet Ledger locks three factions and **54 quests**;
joining the Assize locks two and 36. Five of eight lines close a door on a join. Better, the locks
exceed what the line *declares* — the Wet Ledger declares one enemy and locks three, because the
earned-rivalry model is live.

**The refusals are the best prose in the project.** *"The margin has your name in it 0 times and the
next line wants 10. The Ledger remembers people by the margin. Give it more to remember."* And when
you buy the reputation and nothing else: *"Root-Speech at 25. We ask 70 of anyone who walks where
you are asking to walk, and the ones who went short are still out there."* Eight institutions, eight
voices, every number filled from `FactionGates.evaluate()` at speak time so the prose cannot drift
from the rule. Round 1 ruled that construction correct; it is still correct and it now covers eight
lines instead of seven.

**Expulsion and readmission work end to end**, which is rarer than it sounds — `RI-QST03`'s own
"How we lose" predicts *"we implement the expelled flag and then let the player rejoin instantly."*
Here: *"The table takes a bond or it takes nothing. You are 1200 short of one."*

### Biggest gap

**`GAP-W1-faction-ladders-are-bricked-up-at-rank-5`.** The corridors have been furnished and the top
of four of the eight stairways is bricked up.

`RI-QST03` §B puts a **world-state requirement** on ranks 5, 6 and 7. Four ladders name world states
that **no quest anywhere in `game/data/**` ever raises**:

| line | rank-5/6/7 world states | raised by | quests stranded above the ceiling |
|---|---|---|---:|
| `the_dockhands` | `dockhands_gang_held`, `dockhands_wharf_decided`, `dockhands_harbourmaster_vacant` | **nothing** | 4 |
| `the_drowned_court` | `court_yard_held`, `court_tally_decided`, `court_crown_vacant` | **nothing** | 6 |
| `the_ixtu_vakh` | `sapcutters_well_cut`, `sapcutters_buyer_decided`, `sapcutters_cutter_vacant` | **nothing** | 6 |
| `the_rootkeepers` | `rootkeepers_sapwell_kept`, `rootkeepers_chapter_decided`, `rootkeepers_voice_vacant` | **nothing** | 5 |

`grep -rl dockhands_gang_held game/data/` returns exactly one file: `faction-gates.json`. The other
four lines are clean — `ledger_gate_held`, `assize_seal_held` and the Deep-Kin/Xul-Aneekh states are
all raised by real quests.

**Measured live, not inferred.** A character given every favoured skill at 100, every favoured
attribute at 100 and 400 reputation reaches **rank 4 on all eight lines and stops**, held by the
world state. On the four lines above, nothing in any save can move it. **21 authored quests, and
every succession quest on those four lines, are unreachable in principle** — including the very
`succession` beat `RI-QST01` says the whole ladder exists to arrive at.

**Why it matters more than the volume gap it replaced:** round 1's failure was visible (a line with
one quest). This one is invisible. Every static check passes, every quest exists, the census is
green, and the player simply finds that the Rootkeepers stop promoting them and never says why.

**Remedy, buildable and small.** Twelve `world_flags` entries, on quests that already exist at the
right ranks: give each line's rank-4 or rank-5 quest a resolution that raises its `*_held` flag, its
rank-6 quest one that raises `*_decided`, and its rank-7 quest one that raises `*_vacant`. The
carried lines already show the pattern — copy `faction-wet-ledger.json`'s `ledger_gate_held`.
**Acceptance:** `node tools/quests/critic-w1-20-r2-census.mjs` returns zero `QST03.ceiling.*` hard
fails, and the live arm's `A4` shows a fully-skilled character reaching rank 7 on all eight lines.

### Secondary findings

1. **`deep_kin` is still one body under two ids, and the world still says two contradictory things
   about it.** It has its own 18-quest ladder, its own discipline row, its own registry entry with
   its own seat (`stormhold.faction-deep-kin.r0`) — and when you ask to join, it answers *"That is
   the hollow's own name for itself. It is the same people and the same door."* Its favoured-skill
   set is **byte-identical** to `the_xul_aneekh`'s, which is a stated `RI-QST03` §3 failure ("no two
   factions may share more than 3 favoured skills"). Round 1 measured this exemption false through
   play and did not rebuild it; it is still false, and it now costs a census row as well as a
   sentence. **Fix:** either give Deep Kin a distinct favoured set and a distinct ladder, or delete
   the id and re-point `Q-MAG-07`. Not both halves as they stand.
2. **The exclusivity refusal does not name the exclusion.** When you have joined the Wet Ledger and
   ask the Assize for rank 1, the Assize speaks its ordinary reputation refusal — *"The roll records
   0 against your name and the grade requires 10"* — not the reason it will never say yes. Round 1
   found the exclusivity reason *was* named on the quest-open path; on the rank-request path,
   measured on all eight lines, it is not. **Fix:** `refusal.js` should check `rivalry_locked`
   before the rank terms and speak the lock line first.
3. **Three of eight joins close no door at all** — `deep_kin`, `the_dockhands`, `the_rootkeepers`.
   Two of those three are lines W1-20 opened. `RI-QST03` §C's whole point is that membership is
   scarce; on those lines it is free.
4. **Reputation slack is 2.19×–3.71× on every line** against the item's 1.6× warn ceiling — worse
   than round 1's 1.86×–9.23× on the low end and better on the high end, but still eight warnings.
   *"Too much slack means gating is decorative."*
5. **The round-1 critic's residual fail-open is still live**, at `game/src/ui/system.js:1358`
   (it was `:1167` on 08-08; the line moved, the defect did not):
   `fits: (m.widest_px || 0) <= t.rect[2] + 0.5`. So are the nine `tools/analysis/audio-budget.mjs`
   rows and `tools/quests/critic-faction-r2.mjs:352`. Nothing has been done about a finding the
   branch has carried for six days.
6. **`offences_tolerated` and `on_permanent` are absent from all eight discipline rows.**
   `RI-QST03` §7 names three fields and the build ships one and a half of them. Without
   `offences_tolerated` there is no *second* offence, so Morrowind's "reinstate once, expel
   permanently" tier structure — the thing that makes the Thieves-tier and the Morag-Tong tier
   different institutions — is not expressible.

### Two live findings I was asked to carry, and what they actually do here

Reported plainly because a critic that imports another agent's finding without measuring it is
doing paperwork.

- **"257 of 336 NPC records carry actor names no starting topic is written for."** *Measured at
  `HEAD`, this does not bite W1-20.* Across all 408 NPC records in `game/data/npcs/**`, **7** carry
  an `actor` for which no topic INFO is written (`spellwright` ×4, `enchanter` ×3). All eight faction
  representatives — `harbourmistress-tesh`, `assizer-corvo`, `ee-vashum`, `npc-porter-eeja`,
  `undertaker-vaskh`, `cutter-neeth`, `rootkeeper-jeen` — answer **9 of the 9 starting topics** and
  carry two to four topics of their own. Either the finding used a different definition or the tree
  has moved since; on the definition I can state and reproduce, faction members have plenty to say.
- **`quest-witnesses.json` is absent from `game/data/index.json`, so the engine never loads it.**
  *True, and it does not kill a faction consequence — but it does something subtler and worse.*
  All 16 ids in that file **also exist in `game/data/npcs/mainline.json`, which is loaded**, so no
  reveal is orphaned. The two records are not the same person. The authored one says
  *"The stopper mark was cut before the phial crossed the…"* — the corruption reveal, in a voice.
  The loaded one is a generated shell whose greeting is *"You came about a phial for the blind
  man."*, with the wrong race (`breton fish seller` for a saxhleel witness) and a topic that is the
  quest title. **So the dissenter is in the world and has nothing to say**, which is exactly
  `RI-QST01`'s named "No dissenter" loss mode wearing a disguise. It touches 13 faction quests
  across four lines. **Fix, with a caveat:** adding the index entry is one line, but it creates 16
  duplicate ids against `mainline.json`, so the shells must be removed in the same change. That is
  W1-18's call and this verdict does not make it.
