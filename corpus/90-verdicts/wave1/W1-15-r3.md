## 2. The dark witness is real, is a step, and has one consequence

This is the claim the brief asked me to press hardest, and it half survives.

**It is not a threshold coincidence — the arithmetic is exactly right and I could not shake it.**
Swept `V` continuously from 0.000 to 1.300 in 0.005 steps against `crime/witness.js::isWitness` at
2.0 m with clear LOS:

| V | outcome |
|---|---|
| < 0.100 | not a witness, **0 g** |
| 0.100 – 0.295 | witness, **not identified**, **118 g** |
| ≥ 0.300 | witness, **identified**, **294 g** |

Two edges, three outcomes. `118 = Math.round(294 × 0.4)`, where 0.4 is `bounty.json`'s
`partial_report.multiplier`. The measured dark-corner arm's V of 0.2720 sits below
`identified_at_V` 0.30 and above `V_min` 0.10, exactly as reported. **The control matters here:** a
deliberately continuous grader over the same sweep produces **200** edges, so the instrument can
see a slope and the shipped model genuinely has none. "Seen but not identified" is a real, correct,
authored state and the lamps really do decide which side of it you are on. That is Morrowind-shaped
and it is the best thing in the round.

**Then nothing happens with it.** Classifying every occurrence of `identified` in `game/src` —
16 of them — leaves exactly **one** that changes a decision:

```
game/src/sim/crime/state.js:154   const partial = !witnessRec.identified || witnessRec.kind === 'hearing';
```

(The other two the classifier flags are `system.js:756`, which builds a trace string, and
`system.js:1050`, which *writes* the flag.) So the entire downstream consequence of not being
identified is **a smaller number added to the same ledger**. `stepGuards()` derives its band from
`guardBandNow()`, which reads `this.crime.bounty.imperial` and the player's race and standing — and
nothing else. A guard cannot tell a 294 g identified bounty from two 118 g unidentified ones plus
58 g. Being **suspected** and being **wanted** are the same state, held at different magnitudes.

`justice.json` knows this is not enough, and says so in its own prose:

> `unidentified_effect`: "bounty lands as 'person or persons unknown'; **guards do not approach you
> for it**, but it counts toward the settlement's alarm state and toward RI-STL01 §7's S-4 zone
> memory."

Of those three promises, **none is implemented.** There is no attribution field on the ledger for a
guard to read (the report log writes `attributed: null` and nothing consults it), no settlement
alarm state, and no path from an unattributed report to `ZoneMemory`. This is the piece's own data
file describing behaviour the piece does not have — a `why` string that reads as a specification
and functions as a decoration.

**Grade: the model is live, the consequence is one multiplier.** The 118 g is a number with almost
no consequence, which is what the brief suspected. Not zero — a smaller bounty does eventually
cross a band threshold — but it is magnitude, not kind, and the file promises kind.
