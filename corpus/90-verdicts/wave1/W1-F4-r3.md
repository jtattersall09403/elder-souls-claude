**The overturn condition, verbatim: *"a critic shows the quartile mask holds a real lit/shadow split

The round captured a `key_off` arm in the same process as every `base`, so for every pixel

| pair03 | 0.7693 | 0.0004 | 0.1229 | 0.7395 | 99.88% |
