## 1. What this piece got right, before anything else

The score does not say this, so the prose has to. **Casting is a Souls action and it measures like one.**

- **Cast census exact to the frame at all five weight classes**: 14/3/20, 24/4/28, 36/6/40, 52/8/60, 150/12/48. `Ps` = {15, 25, 37, 53}, which is the number RI-AI03's punish arithmetic consumes.
- **Commitment is real.** A roll injected at every relative frame 1–44 of a `LIGHT` cast never executed and never shortened the cast. The first execution is at **45**, which is `startup(24) + active(4) + ceil(0.60 × 28)` — the formula, to the frame. 56 runs, no exceptions.
- **Zero cast i-frames** on all 56 of those runs.
- **Zero dice.** 60 casts → one damage value (47). `rng.draws` is **0 → 0** across a full cast with no spawn in the scene. No `failure_chance`, `resist_chance` or `proc_chance` token exists anywhere in the shipped magic data, and no spell declares a magnitude range.
- **Ballistics exact**: 22.002 / 16.002 / 10.998 / 7.998 m/s against a table of 22 / 16 / 11 / 8, radii exact, no projectile above the 30 m/s ceiling.
- **Focus never regenerates.** 0 rises across 5,400 frames containing 27 casts, six time-of-day changes and nine `use_item` presses. A HEARTH restores 94.
- **The menu does not pause the fight.** Pressed on frame 6 of a cast with an aggroed enemy: 120 frames advanced.
- **Spellmaking works** — and it is the item's own stated success test. I commissioned five spells that appear nowhere in `spells.json`, at prices the published formula predicts *exactly* (`focus_base` 89 / 176 / 43 / 353 / 2), attuned them, cast them, and found them in `saveState().magic.custom_spells`. A three-effect custom spell cast at a dummy emitted all three `effect_apply` records and dealt **148** damage = 113.94 + 20.07 + 13.90, the exact sum of its magnitudes.
- **The cost model reproduces at 71 of 71 spells**, independently recomputed: 0 `focus_base` mismatches, 0 tier/skill_req mismatches, 0 gold prices off the formula by more than 3%, nuke/balanced ratio **2.920**.

Judged on RI-MAG01 and RI-MAG03's M1 alone, this piece passes the wave-1 gate comfortably.

The whole **Veiling school** — twelve effects, the concealment half of S19's "get in and out unseen" — has zero coupling to the stealth system shipping in the same build.

**Why it matters.** S19 says spell effects are *tools* and quests must be solvable with them. A player who buys **Open** to get past the warded door in *The Warded Ledger* will instead do thirty-one points of damage to the door's guard. RI-MAG04 states that magic supplies "a little over a fifth" of the ≥45% non-combat resolution bar; of the 27 distinct effects the 24 magic resolutions require, **22 do nothing when cast**.

**Remedy** (full text in the JSON): restrict the generic damage accumulator in `game/src/sim/combat-bridge.js` to the five damage effects, and add a per-effect handler registry (`game/src/sim/magic/apply.js`) that writes into the system each effect names — starting with the eight consumers that already exist in this build (equip load, hp/hpMax, a mitigation multiplier, the stealth `V` terms, the S11 buildup meter, `alert_state`/leash, `CombatBody.pos`, and a real levitation locomotion mode).

**Acceptance**: re-run RI-MAG06's census. `EFFECT-FUNCTION ≥ 50/55`, `HP_DAMAGE_ONLY == 0`, `UNREAD_TIMER ≤ 2` — plus five named single-trace assertions that all fail today, including *`still_the_beast` on an aggroed beast ends `COMBAT` with zero `death` events* and *`recall` moves `getPlayerStats().pos` to the mark*.

## 6. Corpus extended — `RI-MAG06`

**`corpus/25-magic/RI-MAG06-effect-behaviour-census.md`** — filed under CORPUS-CONTRACT §5, reason `corpus_hole`. Index regenerated (139 items, 0 holes, 0 problems).

No item in `corpus/25-magic/` contained a check that an effect **does** anything. RI-MAG02 M1 is a diff of two JSON files, M2 is arithmetic, M3 is a grep, and M6 probes ten named effects and no more. A build can ship all 55 records, recompute every cost exactly, pass M1/M2/M3 and the economy axis outright, and have five working effects — which is exactly what happened, and it is why the builder's own static audit returned 10 of 11.

RI-MAG06 supplies the **consuming-system map** for all 55 effects (which system a critic reads, and what delta proves the effect), a closed behaviour-class vocabulary, the **generic-applicator detector** (`HP_DAMAGE_ONLY > 3` is a hard fail, because three is the most a build can plausibly get wrong one effect at a time), and the unread-timer detector. A working reference implementation is committed at `corpus/90-verdicts/wave1/artifacts/W1-14/consolidate.mjs`.

## 7. Which builder claims did not survive

The checkpoint's own findings list is unusually honest — two open gaps (enemy casting, VFX) are declared, two corpus defects are filed against the item rather than hidden, and every `_declared_incomplete` block in the harness names the piece that owns the hole. That discipline is worth keeping. These four claims still did not survive measurement:

1. **"mag-probe.mjs: 19/19 live checks PASS"** — including *"levitation 1.5 Focus/m measured"*. The Focus tax is real. The probe measures the meter and never reads `pos[1]`, horizontal speed against a walk control, or whether `roll`/`block`/`parry` are dropped. Three of RI-MAG02 M4.1's four assertions fail.
2. **"55 effects … mirrored to `game/data/magic/effects.json`"** as evidence the catalogue shipped. The mirror is exact; 50 of the 55 do nothing.
3. **"magic-audit 10/11 PASS (static)"** — true, and the reason it is true is that every instrument in the area is static. That is the corpus hole RI-MAG06 now closes, not a builder failing.
4. **"Magic line lands at 28,132 g against RI-MAG03 M8's 28,000"** — 47 purchasable spells sum to **19,808 g**, 29% below the band. The enchanting services that would close the gap have no priced surface in `game/data/**` for a critic to sum, so the two figures cannot be reconciled from artifacts.

Two further findings the checkpoint does not mention: `spells.json` declares a projectile tracking arc (60 °/s for `LIGHT`, 45 for `HEAVY`) that the trace measures at **0.000 °/s**, which is a declared-vs-observed mismatch under M8; and three shelf spells — `call_the_deep_drowned` (185 Focus), `the_still_water` (594), `the_long_stillness` (156) — cost more than the maximum reachable pool of 124, so `bind_greater` has no castable carrier in the game.

## 8. How this verdict was reached

All live measurement ran in a git worktree pinned to **`75c8e3a`**, the last W1-14 commit. The reason is recorded because it matters: when this critique began the game **did not boot at HEAD** — `api-probe.mjs` exited 12 with `data file missing: dialogue/topics/thorn.json (404)`, because `game/data/index.json` still lists a file W1-18's commit `65275dc` removed. A concurrent agent restored it partway through; the central finding was re-run against HEAD (`d86fc4a`) and is identical (`enemy-effects-HEAD.json`).

Every number in this verdict comes from a trace, a screenshot, a draw-call series or a data recomputation I ran myself. I did not open `reports/runs/MAG-PROBE` or `reports/runs/MAG-AUDIT` at any point. Source was read four times and every read is declared in `source_reads` with its permitted purpose — three of the four were *after* the corresponding finding was already demonstrated from output, to make the remedy name the right file.

**The escalation ladder was used in the unusual direction.** The first sample — RI-MAG01's frame data — was excellent and would have carried a comfortable pass. Rungs 2 and 4 (*change the sample*, *measure the second-order property*) are what produced this verdict: not "does the catalogue have 55 effects" but "does casting one change anything." That is the question no existing item in `corpus/25-magic/` asked, and it is now RI-MAG06.
