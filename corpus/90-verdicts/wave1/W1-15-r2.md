## 2. The parley: a real commitment, and a free win

Both, and the second is what matters.

**It is a real commitment.** Measured through the states, not read off the data: `interact`
begins a **78 f@60** action — `PARLEY_STARTUP` → `PARLEY_ACTIVE` → `PARLEY_RECOVER`,
`actionable_at + 100` — with `iframes: null` and no turn rate. (The brief's "102-frame, 20
stamina" figures are not this build's: `parley.json` says 78 f and `stamina.json` lists parley
under `free_always` at 0.)

**And the grounds genuinely work.** An aggro'd `inf_trash` at 2.5 m **refuses** cold; the same
fight after `learnTopic('the-drowned-ford')` **yields**. That is a Morrowind lore topic learned
outside the fight deciding, at 60 Hz, inside it, whether a hostile can be resolved without a
corpse. It is the best AR-3 crossing in the piece.

**But `guard_legion` — the enemy this piece shipped — surrenders for nothing.** Its parley
declares `gold_price: 0` with `base_disposition: 35`, and `combat/parley.js`'s GOLD ground passes
on `gold >= price && disposition >= 20`. So `0 >= 0` and `35 >= 20`:

| measured | before | after one `interact` |
|---|---:|---:|
| guard HP | 520 / 520 | **520 / 520**, `yielded: true` |
| player gold | 0 | 0 |
| player stamina | 120 | 120 |
| imperial bounty | **3000** | **3000** |
| jail ledger | null | **null** |

And it could not have hurt you anyway: `guard_legion` declares `ai: "hold_ground"`, while
`combat/enemy.js` runs a scripted action only when `stat.ai === 'scripted'`. **An AGGRO
`guard_legion` held at 2.0 m for 900 frames deals 0 damage, emits no `HIT`, and never leaves
`REPOSITION`.** `RI-CRM01` §4's band 3 is the string `attack_on_sight` with no attack behind it,
so the parley's 78-frame exposure is unpunishable by the only enemy that carries the surrender.

The mechanism is right and the datum is wrong — `champion_hist_marked` is `honour_bound` at 900 g
and rank 6, `inf_trash` is 180 g and rank 2, and both gate correctly. This is a one-line data fix
plus a routing fix, and it is the piece's **single biggest gap**.

There is a second, quieter half to it. `ctx.world.gold` is wired to nothing:
`getCombatState().world_knowledge.gold` reads **0** while `getStealthState().gold` reads 400 and
progression reads 405 after a fence sale. There are four independent gold accumulators in this
build against seam **S15**'s "gold is the only currency". So the GOLD parley ground is *unusable
when priced* and *unconditional when priced zero* — both failure modes at once.

## 3. What else is hiding

### S-1's plausible set never runs

`RI-STL01` §7's S-1 is the behaviour the item says makes search frightening: *"it checks WHERE
YOU COULD HAVE GONE, not random points."* Measured `plan: []` and `visited: []` in **4 of 4**
placements — volumes behind the searcher, beyond the LKP, beside the searcher, and in the
peripheral-cone case.

The cause is geometric, not a wiring bug. `plausibleSet()` vetoes any volume whose LKP→volume
segment crosses the searcher's own cone, and the searcher is *by construction* looking at the
LKP, which sits at bearing 0° from it. Every sampled point near the LKP end of every segment is
inside the cone. For a searcher at distance `D` looking at the LKP, a point at angle `φ` and
radius `r` from the LKP subtends `arccos|sin(φ/2)|` — and `crossesCone` samples at `t = 1/8`,
which is always near-zero degrees.

So the search is: walk to the LKP, dwell, sweep radially. The rest of §7 is exact and impressive
— SEARCH entered at frame 360 = **6.000 s** (T25), ran to frame 1080 = **exactly 12.000 s**,
walked at **2.04 m/s** peak over 720 sampled frames, S-3 raised the ally 11.66 m away to alert 45
with `alert_hop: 1` and did not chain, S-4 set the zone baseline to 25 and it is in
`saveState().crime.zones` — which makes the dead leg easy to miss.

This is a **fourth shape** of the `RI-MTH07` failure that §A's table does not carry: not an
orphan model, orphan data or orphan predicate, but a **wiring success with a dead precondition**.
Coupling measured against `plausibleSet`'s output is 1. The behaviour still does not exist. Only
a probe that asserts the output is **non-empty** catches it, and this is worth adding to the item.

## 5. The amendments, assessed on their merits

- **§1, the light exponent — ACCEPT.** This critic derived γ = 1.36 independently from the item's
  own table *before* reading the amendment: `RI-STL01` §2 states `L^0.7` and publishes five rows
  that 0.7 cannot produce, and row 4's "*(clamped)*" annotation is **false at the item's own
  stated exponent** (0.0733 at 0.7, never clamped). That is decisive. It is also a builder making
  the standard *harder*, not easier: γ > 1 makes darkness matter more.
- **§5, `RI-AI01` §B vs `RI-STL01` §2 (100/s vs 75/s at 0.5R) — ACCEPT.** The two items genuinely
  differ by a third at exactly the distance both work an example at. The build picks the one with
  an assertable method behind it, records the choice as *data*, and states the counterfactual
  ("set it the other way and `RI-STL01` method 2's five rows fail instead"). That is the correct
  way to surface an `RI-MTH05` coherence defect; the corpus owner still has to pick.
- **§6, `SNEAK-QUESTS ≥ 22` as a ratio — ACCEPT IN PRINCIPLE, REJECT THE SUPPORTING TABLE.** The
  reasoning is sound and lowers nothing: the item annotates ≥ 22 as "≈12% of ~180", and 22 of 33
  would be 67% of every quest in the game, breaching the very spread cap it was written to
  respect. But the table quotes 13 / 8 / 24.7% and the tree holds 7 / 5 / 27.3%. **Re-file it with
  the tree's actual numbers.**
- **§7, method 6's two S-2 assertions — ACCEPT.** The arithmetic is checkable and correct: 4.0 s
  to the LKP + 2.0 s dwell + 9.0 s of cover volumes + 9.5 s out to 19 m is 24.5 s against a 12.0 s
  window at S17's 2.0 m/s. The band-as-reacquire-radius reading is also the reading §7's own prose
  supports. One irony the amendment does not notice: the S-1 leg it prices into that 24.5 s
  **never actually runs**.
- **NEW, filed by this critic:** `RI-STL01` §4's sound table has a **third** arithmetic defect,
  filed by neither round. Its *left* column reproduces exactly (36.31 / 15.55 / 6.48 against
  36.2 / 15.5 / 6.5). Its *right* column is a flat 0.393–0.400 of `r_base` while
  `E_sound(light) × S_sound(100) × surface(mud) = 0.75 × 0.50 × 0.70 = 0.2625`, giving
  3.675 / 1.575 / 0.656 against the published 5.5 / 2.4 / 1.0. Either the table or one of the
  three constants is wrong. The build implements the formula.

## 8. Working conditions, and two probe traps

The tree booted cleanly throughout: `node tools/harness/smoke.mjs` 6/6, and **0 page errors across
all eight critic probes**. No churn, no retries needed. No indexed data file was deleted.

Two traps cost this critic a wrong measurement each, both recorded in the artifacts rather than
quietly re-run:

1. **`setEntityPos()` silently ignores `opts.yaw`; only `spawn()` honours it.** Every "rear arc"
   measurement taken through `setEntityPos` is really taken at yaw 180 — i.e. facing the player.
   The first pass of `kritik-perception.mjs` reported AGGRO at 1 m behind a stationary enemy and
   was wrong; re-taken through `spawn()`, the result is 0.0000.
2. **`queueInputs()` frame numbers are RELATIVE to the current frame.** An absolute frame number
   silently never fires. That made the first pickpocket run report 0 RNG draws and 0 caught — a
   probe that could not fail, exactly what `AGENT-PROTOCOL.md`'s second named failure mode warns
   about. Re-driven correctly, the die is live at 1 draw per attempt.

Both belong in the protocol's probe section alongside `setRenderRate(0)`.
