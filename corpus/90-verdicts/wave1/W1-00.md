## 0. How this verdict was taken

Everything below was produced by running the game. No score depends on a source-code claim.
The vocabulary is deliberately trace-field and artifact-path vocabulary (`RI-MTH04` F4);
where a function name appears it comes from a **CDP sampling heap profile taken at runtime**
(`critic-alloc-sampling.json` → `top_sites[].site`), which is an artifact I produced, not a
file I read.

**`game/src/**` was never opened.** `source_reads` in the JSON is empty and that is literal.

Six of the eleven instruments under `tools/harness/` and `tools/platform/` were written by
the builder of this piece (`determinism.mjs`, `api-probe.mjs`, `input-probe.mjs`,
`save-audit.mjs`, `perf-probe.mjs`, `lint-determinism.mjs`). Their output is recorded where
useful but **no score rests on it**; every load-bearing number was re-derived by critic-owned
probes driving `window.__HARNESS` directly. That decision paid for itself twice — see §2 and
§3, where the builder's own instruments report PASS on two checks that fail.

## 2. The builder's six candid claims, checked

The brief named six. Here is what each survived.

### 2.1 "0 B per fixed step" — **DOES NOT SURVIVE**

The builder's framing (`performance.memory` quantises, so anything under ~30 B/step reads as
zero) is honest about the instrument but understates what this container can resolve. `A-JRN9`
(`forceGC`/`getHeapUsage`) is **absent from `window.__HARNESS`** — I confirmed by enumerating
all 54 methods — so `RI-PLT01` M4's stated procedure is unrunnable as written. I took it
runner-side over CDP instead (`HeapProfiler.collectGarbage`, `Runtime.getHeapUsage`,
`HeapProfiler.startSampling`), which is 30× finer.

**Result, sampling profiler at a 64-byte interval, 60,000 steps per scenario, render disabled:**

| Scenario | State | B/step | ~samples |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 fen | `swamp_canopy` | **0.98** | 914 |
| F5 dungeon | `dungeon_primary` | **1.73** | 1,620 |
| F4 six-enemy | `arena_flat` + 6 aggroed | **0.78** | 728 |

Named allocation sites, from the profile's own call frames:
`stepFrames @ engine.js:217`, `hypot ← stepPlayer @ player.js:23`,
`latchForStep @ pipeline.js:137`, `stepEntities @ entities.js:67`,
`norm180 @ entities.js:118`, `wallNow @ guards.js:95`.

The honest number is therefore **~1 byte per step, not zero**. `RI-PLT01` P4 requires literally
**0 bytes** in F1/F5, so M4 fails — by about one byte per step. HF4 (> 8 KB/step) is **not**
triggered and there is **no leak**: 30,000 steps between forced collections moved the retained
heap by **0.354 B/step** (`critic-alloc-perf.json → retained_growth`).

Two things fall out that the builder did not claim and that matter more than the byte:

- **With `traceStart()` active the sim step allocates 2,220 B/step**, from
  `enemyRecord @ record.js:101` and `makeRecord @ record.js:31`. `RI-PLT01` §C.3 excludes the
  trace record from P4 *by name*, so this is not a P4 failure — but the same section also says
  the record "is built **outside** the sim step", and the profile's stack is
  `makeRecord ← _step @ engine.js:202 ← stepOnce @ loop.js:134 ← stepFrames`. It is built
  **inside**. Every traced measurement in this project therefore runs with ~2 KB/step of
  garbage the untraced game does not have.
- The instrument the builder used could not have found this. The one CDP call that could was
  never wired into the harness (`A-JRN9`), and `RI-PLT01` calls that check *"the single most
  predictive number for whether the game will stutter on a phone"*.

### 2.2 The R5 failure and amendment `AM-W1-00-01` — **the arithmetic is sound; the conclusion is not yet earned**

The amendment argues R5 is unsatisfiable for any entity with a looping idle, because 60 extra
warm-up frames leave a 48-frame loop at a different phase, and `state_entered_f` is an absolute
index by `HARNESS.md` §5.

**The arithmetic is correct.** (p + 60) mod 48 ≠ p unless the loop length divides the warm-up
delta, so any build with a free-running idle fails R5 as written. I reproduced the failure and
the field census: exactly `enemies[].anim_frame` and `enemies[].state_entered_f` differ, and
`differing_fields_outside_the_enemy_block: []`
(`tool-determinism-ladder.json`; my own re-run in `critic-camera-determinism.json → MTH02.R5_field_census`).

**The conclusion is a rationalisation of a real dependency, and here is why.** The amendment
was filed by a build in which *nothing free-running is happening*: every enemy declares
`ai: "hold_ground"`, `rng.draws` is **0 on every frame of every trace I took**, and the enemy
state histogram over 1800 frames after `aggro()` is `{FACE: 1800}` with
`frames_with_enemy_hit_active: 0` (`critic-ar1.json → AR1.A4_enemy_attacks`). In that build the
two differing fields are necessarily cosmetic — there is nothing for animation phase to feed.
The amendment then generalises from that to a **closed permanent exclusion of `anim_frame`**
from the determinism ladder. The moment animation drives a hurtbox, a hitbox sweep or a
transition frame — which is `RI-CMB01`'s and `RI-AI02`'s whole subject — a genuine
warm-up-dependent divergence will sit inside the excluded field and R5 will not see it.

The amendment also presents a false dichotomy: its options are "delete the idle loop" or
"falsify `anim_frame`". A third exists and is not rebutted — make the **scenario** contract
re-anchor free-running entity animation at the frame the scripted window opens, which changes
the fixture rather than the trace and is not falsification. And a fourth: choose warm-up counts
that are congruent modulo the declared loop lengths.

**Ruling: R5 stays FAIL, the amendment stays unapplied, and it should not be reconsidered until
a build exists with enemy AI that actually runs.** Recorded in `arbitration_questions`.

### 2.4 The determinism trap — **holds against everything I could throw at it**

I tried to get non-determinism in by a route the trap misses. The one that should have worked:
queue an input event whose `press` property is a **getter**, so the read happens from inside
`stepFrames` rather than from my own frame. It fired five times inside the step and threw every
time:

```
["Math.random-threw","DETERMINISM VIOLATION: Math.random() was called inside a fixed
 simulation step. HARNESS.md §8 D1–D3 forbid unseeded randomness and wall-clock reads in
 the sim path; use rng.next() and frame/60 instead."]
```

Outside a step the same calls return real values (`Math.random() = 0.3231…`,
`Date.now() = 1786019545859`) — the wrappers are installed globally and gated on step depth.
`stepFrames` is synchronous, so no microtask or async continuation can land inside it. Three.js
UUID generation routes through `Math.random` and is therefore covered. Resolution invariance
(1920×1080 vs 640×360) and wall-clock invariance (3 s host sleeps between chunks) both hold.

**The trap is not the weak point. The weak point is that nothing draws from the PRNG at all** —
§3.

### 2.5 `stallMainThread` and the 5-step catch-up bound — **run end to end, PASSES**

`RI-PLT01` M8, first execution: `setMode('play')`, one rAF settle, `stallMainThread(400)`
(measured 400.07 ms), then six consecutive rAFs. Sim steps per rAF: **`[5, 0, 0, 1, 1, 1]`**.
Max 5 — exactly R3's bound. `getPerfStats().catchupClamps` incremented to 1, so the clamp is
observable rather than implicit. `t_ms / f = 16.66668` after recovery, i.e. **0 steps of
non-1/60 duration** and no spiral. `getActionSet().catchupCap` reads back `5`.

## 3. The two hard fails

### 3.1 `RI-MTH02` R4 — the seed reaches nothing

`tools/harness/trace.mjs`, `cmb-duel-infantry`, 3600 frames, seeds 1337 and 4242. Field-level
diff over the whole body (`r4-seed-sensitivity.txt`):

```
all differing fields across 3600 frames: {'.rng.seed': 3600}
frames differing in ANY field other than .rng.seed: 0
```

`rng.draws` is **0 on every one of the 3600 frames**, in every scenario I ran, in every state.
The PRNG is reseeded correctly — the four state words `a,b,c,d` are a clean function of the
seed — and then nothing ever draws from it. `body_sha256` differs (`0cb6c2a5…` vs `711f1b2c…`)
and 100% of frames differ **in the trace record echoing back the seed it was given**.

R4's tolerance as literally written is met. Its hard-fail clause is not: *"R4 fails (seed
changes nothing): the AI's randomness is fake."* The seed changes nothing. `RI-MTH02`'s own
`## How we lose` #8 predicts this exact outcome — *"R4's 5% clause passes on a technicality"* —
and R5 already solves the same problem for `rng.draws` by naming a field to drop before
comparing, while R4 does not.

**The build's own instrument reports this rung as passing:** `tools/harness/determinism.mjs`
prints `PASS R4 — seed-sensitive: different seed diverges, >=5% of frames differ /
frames_differing_pct: 100` (`tool-determinism-ladder.json`).

I have filed the sharpened discriminator as
`orchestration/amendments/AM-W1-00-C1-mth02-r4-discriminator.md` (drop `rng.seed` from the
frame-difference count; additionally require `max(rng.draws) > 0`). It changes no threshold.

### 3.2 `RI-JRN05` M1 / HF1 — the save round trip is not hash-stable, and it passes only on the corpus's default seed

`h0 = getStateHash()` → `blob = saveState()` → `loadState(blob)` → `h1 = getStateHash()`.

**5 of 5 scenarios mismatch at seed 4711.** The diff is four keys, identical in every scenario
(`critic-save.json → M1_M2`):

```
rng.a          -339423194   ->  3955544102
rng.b         -1736603526   ->  2558363770
rng.d          -100859197   ->  4194108099
volatile.frame         120  ->           0      (declared volatile — legitimately excluded)
```

`-339423194 + 2³² = 3955544102`. The bits are preserved; the **representation** is not. The live
PRNG holds its state words as signed `int32`; the round-tripped copy holds them as unsigned
`uint32`. The canonical serialiser therefore produces two different documents for the same
state, and `getStateHash()` — which `RI-JRN05` §C.5 defines as *the* round-trip test — differs.

**Then I swept it.** 20 seeds × 2 states = 40 trials
(`critic-roundtrip-seedsweep.json`):

| | |
|---|---|
| First-trip mismatches | **36 / 40 (90%)** |
| Second-trip mismatches | **0 / 40** (once unsigned, it is stable) |
| Seeds that pass | **1337 and 60606 — and only those** |

Those two are exactly the seeds whose four state words `a, b, c, d` are **all positive as
int32**. Every seed with a negative word fails. **1337 is the corpus's default seed and the seed
`tools/harness/determinism.mjs` runs**, which is why the builder's own ladder reports
`PASS R9 — save round trip: state hash equal … round_trip_diff_fields: 0`.

This is the classic single-sample false pass, and it is the reason the doctrine forbids scoring
from someone else's instrument.

**Consequences measured, not inferred:**

- **M1** 0/5 → **HF1**, item capped at 2, `status: FAIL`.
- **M2** the diff is not empty and `rng.*` is **not** on the manifest's closed volatile list
  (`volatilePaths: ["volatile.written_at","volatile.playtime_seconds","volatile.frame",
  "volatile.thumbnail","volatile.ui_focus","volatile.audio_bus"]`). Rule V1 forbids widening
  that list to make the test pass.
- **M3 cold reload** `h0 = 4b2f4077…`, after a real `page.reload()` and `readSave('coldslot')`
  → `h1 = 97a3548c…`. **Same root cause** — the quest state itself comes back intact
  (journal 42, flags 3, topics 5, all correct), so persistence works and the *hash* does not.
- **M14 export → wipe → import** `h0 = 69423209…` → `h1 = 16c7385b…`. Same root cause.

**What does NOT fail, checked because it would have been the harsher story:**

- **M5 post-load divergence: PASSES.** My first pass showed 5/5 diverging; I re-ran it fairly
  (identical 120-frame pre-roll on both sides) and the only difference is the **absolute frame
  index carried inside `events[].f`** — control `f: 180`, loaded `f: 60`, because `loadState`
  resets the frame counter. `first_non_event_divergence: null` over 300 frames
  (`critic-final.json → M5_event_diff`). After the re-basing `RI-MTH02` M5 already mandates,
  the simulation after a load is identical. **R9 therefore passes on its own stated tolerance
  ("the tail matches") even though M1 fails on its.**
- **M17 / HF9 does not trigger.** `readSave(slot)` returns `{ok, gen, degraded}` and *applies*
  the save rather than returning the blob; my first attempt to `loadState()` its return value
  threw, which looked like an offered-slot-that-cannot-be-loaded. It is not: the slot loads and
  restores correctly (`critic-final.json → readSave`, `critic-save2.json → M3_cold_reload`).
  A `read*` verb that mutates state is a naming defect, not a data-loss defect.

## 7. The single biggest gap

Two candidates were real: the round-trip hash instability (§3.2) and the dead PRNG (§3.1). I
promote the second, and the reason is that the first is a **bug** — three integers need a
`>>> 0` at one boundary, it is an S-sized fix, and its blast radius is one hash — whereas the
second is a **hole in the substrate that silently disarms a corpus-wide check**. It is also the
one that made the first one invisible: 1337 is the default seed everywhere, so a defect that
fires on 90% of seeds passed every instrument in the repository.

> **`GAP-W1-platform-prng-never-drawn`** — `platform.determinism.harness`, severity **blocking**.
>
> **What.** The seeded PRNG is reseeded correctly and never drawn from. `rng.draws == 0` on
> every one of 3600 frames in every scenario, state and seed measured. Seeds 1337 and 4242 over
> a 3600-frame duel differ in **exactly one field** — `.rng.seed`, the echo of the input — and in
> **zero** simulation fields. `RI-MTH02` R4's 5%-of-frames clause is satisfied at 100% by that
> echo alone, so the rung, `tools/harness/determinism.mjs`, and any future critic reading either
> will report seed sensitivity on a build that has none.
>
> **Why it matters.** Every "seeded variety" claim downstream — `RI-AI01` M4's inter-COMMIT
> coefficient of variation, rumour variation per town, seeded loot placement — is unfalsifiable
> until the seed reaches something, and the corpus's own detector for that condition currently
> returns PASS.
>
> **Remedy.**
> 1. Route at least one simulation quantity through `rng.next()` inside the fixed step and emit
>    the draw count truthfully: the enemy idle-loop phase offset at spawn is sufficient, cheap,
>    and is exactly the field `AM-W1-00-01` wants excluded from R5 — seeding it turns that
>    argument into a measurement.
> 2. Change `tools/harness/determinism.mjs`'s R4 computation to drop `rng.seed` before counting
>    differing frames, and to fail the rung with reason `prng_never_drawn` when
>    `max(rng.draws) == 0` in either run.
> 3. Adopt `orchestration/amendments/AM-W1-00-C1-mth02-r4-discriminator.md`, which states the
>    sharpened rung. It moves no threshold.
>
> **Targets.** `game/src/sim/entities.js`, `game/src/core/rng.js` (or wherever `rng.next()`
> lives), `tools/harness/determinism.mjs`, `corpus/80-methods/RI-MTH02-determinism-reproducibility.md`.
>
> **Acceptance.** Re-run `node tools/harness/trace.mjs --scenario cmb-duel-infantry` at seeds
> 1337 and 4242 and diff field-by-field: **`max(rng.draws) > 0` in both runs**, and **≥ 5% of
> frames differ in at least one field other than `.rng.seed`**. Both numbers are printed by the
> tool after remedy 2, and the second is the one `r4-seed-sensitivity.txt` currently reports
> as **0.0%**.
>
> **Ref item.** `RI-MTH02` (M3 / rung R4). **Size.** S.

## 8. Secondary observations — explicitly **not** work for the next builder

1. `queueInputs` **silently accepts and no-ops** an event with no recognised action key.
   `{f:0, tap:'light'}` returns `1`, `{f:0, wibble:3}` returns `1`, `{f:0}` returns `1`, and
   none of them do anything. Unknown *buttons* throw correctly; unknown *event shapes* do not.
   This cost me four measurements before I noticed — probes 3, 6, 8 and 10 all ran with their
   attack inputs silently dropped. `HARNESS.md` §4 says the game only ever sees
   `{f, press?, release?, move?, look?}`, so tolerating `tap`/`hold` is not required — but
   returning a count for an event that will never fire is fail-quiet where `RI-MTH01` M6 asks
   for fail-closed. (`critic-ar1.json → sugar`.)
2. `camera({pos:[1,2]})` returns `{pos:[1,2,null], look:[null,1.55,null], …}` instead of
   throwing. `teleport('a','b')` returns `true`.
3. `camera()`'s derived readback fields (`yaw_deg`, `pitch_deg`, `dist_m`) are **stale until a
   sim step runs** — after `camera({pos:[10,2,10], look:[0,1,0]})` the snapshot still reports the
   previous pose's `dist_m` (3.6 or 50.636 depending on history) and only becomes correct
   (`yaw 225°`, `dist 14.1774 m`) after the 24 settle steps `HARNESS.md` §6 mandates. Pixels are
   byte-identical either way (`sha256 df26c96f…` twice), so M7 passes.
4. `listAnchors()` returns nine **`loadState` names**, not the twelve canonical viewpoint ids in
   `tools/harness/viewpoints.json` (`VP01-vista-wide` … `VP12-combat-framing`). `RI-MTH01` A26
   asks for the latter. Three world viewpoints have no anchor at all.
5. `getBuildInfo().commit` and `.builtAt` are both `null`. The trace header carries `git.commit`
   separately, so traceability survives, but `RI-MTH01` A03's stated shape does not.
6. Repeated light attacks translate the player **+0.35 m per swing** with no movement input;
   over 20 swings that is 7 m. It is why only 12 of 50 stationary-target swings connect — the
   player walks through and past the target. Deterministic, zero RNG, and a matter for the
   combat piece. Each attack also emits **two** hitbox windows (1 active frame, then 9), which
   `critic-final.json → swing_geometry` records.
7. `getWorldStats()` reports `pois: 24, interiors: 1` where `tools/analysis/content-stats.mjs`
   counts `pois: 27, interiors: 0` over the same `game/data/**`.
8. `simulateStorageFailure` modes are **cumulative and sticky** — after `ephemeral` then
   `no-idb`, `getStorageInfo().notices` reads `["ephemeral","no-idb","quota"]` and `backend`
   stays `"memory"` even when the mode is set back.
9. The camera's height residual over 900 frames of running is **identically zero**. It passes
   `RI-CAM06` §E's 0.004 m bar by having no vertical camera behaviour at all rather than by
   damping one — no terrain follow, no spring-arm response. Thinness, not a defect, today.
