## 3. Angle 1: look at the picture — the line is live, and it is a line of nothing

This is the one S25 names, so I did not settle for one frame. **Eleven captures**, through the
pooled daemon, at five depths, three tide states and three times of day.

### 3.1 The waterline tracks the real surface. Measured.

The round-1 and round-2 shots **cannot** be a controlled pair — 1280×720 vs 1920×1080, different
camera, different framing, different character pose (weapon drawn vs sheathed). So I built one.

One point, **(2620, 4362)**, Eastern Rootlands, where the shipped field's own phase sweep says the
surface moves **1.178 m** over a cycle, carrying the same square of ground from **W1 (0.104 m)**
through **W3 (0.693 m)** to **W4 (1.282 m)**. One camera, one time of day. Only the tide changed.

| pair | differing pixels | bounding box |
|---|---:|---|
| LOW → MEAN | 8,631 | (424, 458) – (536, 636) |
| MEAN → HIGH | 15,003 | (387, 300) – (527, 518) |
| **LOW → HIGH** | **21,804** | **(387, 300) – (536, 636)** |
| HIGH @ 11h → @ 6h | 809,999 | whole frame |

The line climbs the body monotonically — calf → thigh → waist — and **every one of the 21,804
changed pixels is on the character**. Against the geometry (body 1.8 m over ~520 px) the drawn line
sits within about **0.07 m** of where the field says the surface is. Time of day moves the whole
frame and the tide moves only the body, which separates "the line moved" from "the light moved".
**The waterline is real, live, and correctly coupled.** Round 2's third closure is genuine.

### 3.2 But it is drawn against water that is not there

`game/src/world/province.js:747-773` builds each tile's water mesh on a **12.5 m** lattice
(`TILE_M 300 / WATER_SEG 24`) and drops a quad outright if **any one of its four corners is dry**:

```js
const surf = corners.map(([cx, cz]) => f.waterSurfaceAt(cx, cz));
if (surf.some((s) => s === null)) continue;
```

Western Rootlands is a *paddy-and-channel* region. Its channels are metres wide; the test needs
12.5 m of unbroken water in both axes. Audited over the shipped field with roads applied:

| tile | actually wet (5 m sample) | water quads kept | |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **the round-1/round-2 shot point** (2100, 4200) | **61.3 %** | **32.1 %** | ~half the water is never drawn |
| Eastern Rootlands delta (2400, 4200) | 85.0 % | 62.0 % | |
| open water (2700, 4200) | 89.9 % | 80.9 % | |

And the shot point's **own** quad: corners `[1.650, 1.600, null, null]` → **dropped**. There is no
water drawn on the square metre the player is standing in, in 0.886 m of it.

**This is the mechanism round 1 flagged and nobody ran.** The survey was careful — *"it does not, by
itself, prove the water mesh failed to stream at this exact tile (that would need a separate,
targeted check of the province streamer's per-tile water build, which I did not run this session)"*.
It has now been run. That is why round 1's photograph showed a dry-looking scene at 0.681 m, and
round 2's before-and-after never addressed it: **round 2 fixed the line and not the water.**

### 3.3 And where water *is* drawn, it is frozen

`waterSurfaceAt()` is sampled at **tile-build time** and nothing rebuilds the mesh when the tide
moves. Falsified with two arms that must differ:

- tide moved to HIGH with the tiles already built → **21,804 px change, all on the body**, zero
  ground pixels;
- tide moved to HIGH then the player teleported away and back to force a rebuild →
  **657,561 px change**, and the water visibly rises to the character's chest.

So the body's waterline is live and the world's water surface is stale by up to the full tidal range
(1.20 m Padomaic). At any moment other than the phase a tile happened to be built at, the two
disagree — and S25 rests on reading depth off the silhouette *against the water*.

### 3.4 The 20-second drying window does not exist

`RI-WLD10` §10.3: wet-shading *"persists 20 s after leaving the water and dries visibly."* Matched
pair, same camera, same place, same pose, back to back:

- **A** — a body dry throughout;
- **B** — the same body **one second** after standing in 1.05 m of water (surface 1.623 m),
  teleported onto ground at 2.711 m, well inside the declared window.

**Zero differing pixels. Byte-identical, `bbox=None`.** The cause is in the shader:
`esBand = uWaterY - vEsWaterY` (`render/actor.js:185`) is a **world-space** cutoff, and
`renderer.js:598` stops updating `W.y` once the band is `W0`. Step *up* out of water — the ordinary
way of leaving it — and every vertex is above the frozen plane, so `esWet` is 0 everywhere. The
timer runs, `wetness` is computed and delivered to the uniform, and it changes nothing you can see:
a model that is computed and not consumed, which is the shape `RULES.md` rule 5 exists for.

## 5. The automatic fail: the tide is a global shader

`RI-WLD10`'s automatic-fail list: ***"A tidal amplitude anywhere outside the five tidal regions — the
tide as a global shader."*** M54's bar is `> 0.02 m` in any of the eight non-tidal regions.
Measured over each region's own bounds, 12 phases per point:

| region | declared tidal | measured amplitude |
|---|---|---:|
| **Thornmarsh** | false | **1.2000 m** |
| **The Deep Marshes** | false | **0.6600 m** |
| **Valus Ridge** | false | **0.6600 m** |
| **The Stone Forest** | false | **0.6600 m** |
| **The Salt Hills** | false | **0.6600 m** |
| Blackwood / Clay Moor / The Hive | false | 0.0000 m |

**Five of the eight.** 33× to 60× the bar. The Deep Marshes is the region the item describes as
*"black standing water with **no tide** and no bottom you want to find"*; Valus Ridge's only water is
two tarns; Thornmarsh — sheet water for 90 s after rain, deepest band W2 — gets the full Padomaic
1.20 m.

The cause is one clause, `game/src/world/field.js:556`:

```js
const tidalHere = this.tidal[r] === 1 || !this.isLandAt(x, z);
```

The `|| !this.isLandAt(x, z)` disjunct overrides the region's declared flag for any cell the field
does not call land — which is every standing-water cell. Spot-checked: `deep-marshes` (2503, 3325),
`declared_tidal=false`, `sea=null`, `isLandAt=false`, surface swing 0.216 m. **The comment six lines
below asserts the invariant the code breaks**: *"Amplitude outside a tidal region is exactly 0.00 m
(RI-WLD10 §7, checked by M54)."*

**Rule 4, and it caught me.** My first control zeroed `tideRange` with `for (let i = 0; i <
tideRange.length; i++)` — `tideRange` is an object `{1:…, 2:…}`, `.length` is `undefined`, the loop
never ran, and the control "failed". I did not charge the finding until I understood why. Fixed:
**ranges zeroed → worst non-tidal amplitude 0.0000 m; ranges restored → 1.2000 m.** Two arms that
differ, measuring the tide and nothing else.

## 9. The gap — `GAP-W1-03-water-surface-undrawn-and-frozen`

**`world.water.marsh` / `render.fidelity.water`. Blocking.**

The waterline on the body is live and correct, and the water surface it is a line *of* is (a) not
drawn at all over roughly half the wet area of the marsh regions — 32.1 % of quads kept on a tile
that is 61.3 % wet, including the exact quad both rounds photographed, whose corners are
`[1.650, 1.600, null, null]` — and (b) frozen at tile-build tide phase where it *is* drawn, so it
lags the simulated surface by up to 1.20 m.

**Why it matters:** `RI-WLD10` §10.3 calls the waterline *"the player's only depth readout and it is
not optional"*, and §1 forbids a HUD substitute. A player standing hip-deep sees darkened legs on
green grass, and after a tide swing sees a body wet to the chest with the water at their ankles. S25
made S18's permanent third person load-bearing on this readout; the readout is half-delivered.

**Remedy.** Drop the all-four-corners-wet rule for the water quad: emit the quad when *any* corner
is wet and clamp the dry corners down to the local ground height, so a channel narrower than 12.5 m
still gets a surface. Then mark province tiles water-dirty when `tidePhase` crosses a threshold and
rebuild only the water sub-mesh (the geometry already exists per region per tile, so this is a
vertex-Y rewrite, not a tile rebuild). Separately, clamp `uWaterY` to the body's own foot height
while drying, so §10.3's 20 s persistence has something to shade.

**Acceptance.** (1) At (2226.9, 4376.1) a capture shows a water surface meeting the character's
body at the waterline the shader draws. (2) Kept-quad fraction ≥ 0.90 × wet-area fraction on the
(2100, 4200) and (2400, 4200) tiles. (3) The LOW→HIGH pair at (2620, 4362) changes ground pixels as
well as body pixels without a forced rebuild. (4) The §3.4 matched pair stops being byte-identical.
