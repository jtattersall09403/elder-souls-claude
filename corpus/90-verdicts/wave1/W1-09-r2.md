## 3. Is the fight dangerous, or dialled?

### 3.1 `eatRate`

Every bot profile in `cmb-exemplar.mjs` carries an `eatRate` between 0.18 and 0.30. It is the
per-swing probability that the bot chooses `greed` — *"keeps swinging into the windup and eats the
blow"* — and the generator's own comment, three lines above it, says: *"it is where rows 13, 14
and 21 come from."*

The commit message says *"No damage event is scripted: every hit taken is a mistimed roll or an
unfinished attack."* That is true of the damage **resolution** and false of the **decision**. Rows
13 and 14 — swings taken, damage taken — are set by a tuned constant. It is a better instrument
than round 1's (which rolled perfectly, forever, and could not be wrong), and it is still a knob
whose value was chosen so the rows land in band.

## 4. Why i-frame negation is 0.111 — and it is none of the two obvious answers

Three candidate causes were named. Each is a different defect. Here is which.

**(b) the i-frame window is not where it should be — INNOCENT.** Round 1's boundary probe
(`critic-iframe.json`) re-runs on this commit **byte-identical**. I corroborated it independently
with a probe of my own: from 3.6 m, where a standing player is hit,

```
control, no roll:  HIT
roll pressed f40-42: survives, i-frames already expired  (displacement)
roll pressed f44-70: survives with 2-10 i-frames covering the enemy's active window
roll pressed f72+:   HIT
```

That is a real, learnable, correctly-placed window with a hard edge on both sides.

**(c) negation happens and is not counted — INNOCENT.** `IFRAME_NEGATE` fires 26 times across the
five fights and always where it should.

**(a) the player never rolls into the swing — TRUE, but it is a symptom.** The bot's `hold` band
is **4.0–4.6 m** while the generator's own comment two lines above says *"2.6–3.2 m is the band
where its swing ARRIVES in reach"*. The constant and its rationale disagree and the constant is
what runs.

**(d) — the one nobody named.** In F1 there are **eleven** enemy swings where the player was in
`ROLL_IFRAME` for *every* active frame, at 0.06–0.60 m, and the outcome was `WHIFF`, not
`IFRAME_NEGATE`. Thirty-five such swings across five fights. That is not a telemetry bug. **The
swing genuinely could not connect** — because of §5.

## 5. The single biggest gap: the enemy cannot hit anything standing next to it

`GAP-W1-enemy-attacks-cannot-reach-contact-range`, `combat.hitbox.sweep`, **blocking**.

**Method.** Stationary player, stationary `champion_hist_marked`, `arena_champion`, no input, no
roll, pure geometry. Sweep the player's distance 0.0 → 4.0 m in 0.1 m steps. Run each of the
enemy's attacks. Record whether damage lands.
(`tools/harness/critic-w1-09-r2.mjs --probe reach`)

| enemy attack | hits from | to | **misses at every distance** | declared `root_dz_m` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `chop` | 1.2 m | 3.6 m | **0.0 – 1.1 m** | **1.2** |
| `thrust` | 1.6 m | 4.0 m | **0.0 – 1.5 m** | **1.6** |
| `combo_a` | 1.1 m | 2.9 m | **0.0 – 1.0 m** | **0.9** |

The minimum hit distance **is the attack's declared forward root travel**. The player never moves
(`--probe mech` records the position at every first-active frame: `P[0,0,d]`, unchanged), so this
is not a collision push-out. Setting `root_dz_m` to 0 on all three attacks moves the inner boundary
to 0.6 / 0.8 / 0.8 m and the outer to 2.6 / 2.4 / 2.5 m — the whole annulus travels with the
attacker's root motion. *(Data file restored from backup; `git status` clean.)*

**The player's own weapons have no such hole.** Same sweep, five classes:

```
dagger          hits 0.0 - 1.4 m     inner misses: none
straight-sword  hits 0.0 - 2.1 m     inner misses: none
spear           hits 0.0 - 3.1 m     inner misses: none
greatsword      hits 0.0 - 2.9 m     inner misses: none
halberd         hits 0.0 - 2.8 m     inner misses: none
```

### 5.1 What that is worth to a player

Stand completely still. Never roll, never block, never move. Mash the light attack.

| stand at | enemy killed | frames | **damage taken** | enemy `ATK_ACTIVE` frames | enemy `STAGGER` frames |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.8 m | yes | 1153 | **0 / 620** | 44 | **0** |
| 1.0 m | yes | 1377 | **0 / 620** | 54 | **0** |
| 1.1 m | yes | 1377 | **0 / 620** | 54 | **0** |
| 1.5 m | yes | 1411 | 0 / 620 | 44 | 44 |
| 2.0 m | yes | 1716 | 96 / 620 | 67 | 44 |
| 2.6 m | yes | 1716 | 96 / 620 | 67 | 44 |

The 2876-HP boss-class champion dies in nineteen seconds to a player who does not move, and at
0.8–1.1 m it never staggers — so this is not stunlock, it is **fifty-four frames of live hitbox
that cannot reach a target one metre away**.

**Why it matters:** the correct way to fight this game's boss is to walk inside it and stand still,
which makes rolling, spacing, stamina, and the entire i-frame system that `RI-CMB01` gets exactly
right, decorative.

**Why two rounds of critics missed it:** `RI-CMB04` M2 enumerates *seven player weapon classes ×
24 lateral offsets* against a *stationary* attacker. Nothing in the corpus tests an attacker that
translates during its own active window, and nothing tests an enemy's attack volume at all. Filed
as a corpus hole with the instrument attached.

**Remedy (in the JSON, concretely):** sweep the attacker's **root translation** as well as its
pose — interpolate the socket capsule between last frame's *world* pose and this frame's *world*
pose, root delta included — and re-derive `SUBSTEPS` from `max(per-frame tip travel, per-frame root
travel)`. Acceptance: `min_hit_distance_m ≤ 0.35 m` for all three attacks with `root_dz_m`
unchanged, a motionless mashing player at 0.8–1.5 m taking ≥ 0.4 × max HP, and row 11 reaching
0.40–0.80.

## 11. Corpus extensions filed

1. **`RI-CMB04` — corpus hole.** M2 tests player weapons against a stationary attacker. Proposed
   **M2b**: for every enemy attack with `root_dz_m > 0`, sweep a stationary target from 0.0 m to
   the outer reach in 0.05 m steps and require a contiguous hit band with no interior gap. The
   instrument exists: `tools/harness/critic-w1-09-r2.mjs --probe reach`.
2. **`RI-AI02` — sharper discriminator.** The AP table is one-directional, exactly as B2 was before
   B13. AP9 fails damage with no hitbox; nothing fails a hitbox that is active, telegraphed and
   unable to reach. Proposed **AP10**.
3. **`RI-CMB07` — method gap.** (a) "the 24 rows that carry a band" versus §D's 26 lets an
   implementation pick its own denominator, and one did. Enumerate the banded rows by number.
   (b) M2 requires enemy AI that will not exist until W1-12; declare **Mode B-prime** with its own
   reduced weight so a half-scripted exchange is scoreable without being scoreable *as* Mode B.
4. **`RI-CMB01` / `RI-CMB02` / `RI-CMB06` — degenerate blind pairs**, re-filed from round 1 because
   it bit this round too. A pack whose two sides coincide when the work is correct cannot
   discriminate. The roll's per-frame root-delta curve would discriminate within correctness — and
   would have caught the 2.53 m transition snap.

## 12. Where this leaves the piece

The frame layer is still the best thing this project has shipped, and round 2 verified it rather
than inheriting it: seven of round 1's instrument outputs are byte-identical on this commit. The
remediation did substantial, real work — guard break, staggers, the four dead buttons, the swing
arcs, the substep rationale, `RI-CMB03` M7, and an exemplar that is five deterministic fights
instead of one bloodless one.

What it did not do is look at the enemy. Every fix landed on the player's half of the exchange,
and the half nobody instrumented turns out to contain an attack that cannot hit you at contact
range, a stagger tier that never fires, a hyperarmour control case that never lands, and a parry
with no riposte. The exemplar's three out-of-band rows — whiff rate, i-frame negation, punish
usage — are not three problems. They are one problem, and it is on the other side of the arena.
