## One thing found on the way, filed and not used to pass anything

`RI-UIX09` never says **how** the modal colour is taken. The shipped `d2()` bins colour into 15-bit
buckets, picks the modal *bin* and averages inside it; an exact-colour modal pick is an equally
faithful reading. On the container the two agree to **0.0004** (0.1685 vs 0.1681). On **level-up they
disagree across the hard floor**: `[220,217,204] → 0.1494` (fail) versus `[223,223,216] → 0.1625`
(pass), because level-up's modal bucket holds 7,673 pixels against a runner-up of 5,525. **I scored
level-up FAIL on the shipped instrument** — §1.3 guard 2: an instrument I wrote this evening may be
the reason I fail a piece and never the reason I pass one. Filed as a corpus defect with S65(2)'s own
fix: pin the reference, and write the derivation into the item.
