## 2. The single biggest gap — `GAP-W1-mainquest-topic-bootstrap`

`quests.mainline.acts` · **blocking**

> **The main quest cannot be entered or advanced by playing.**

Booted cold with nothing granted through the harness:

| | |
|---|---:|
| main quests offerable | **0 / 32** |
| main quests refused by `gate.js:153` on `opens_by.topic` | **32 / 32** |
| topics known at a cold start | **0** |

And the supply side, statically:

| | |
|---|---:|
| opening topics supplied **only by the quest that fires the edge** (`Q-MAIN-nn#10` adds the topic `Q-MAIN-nn` demands) | **23 / 32** |
| opening topics with **no supplier at all** | **5 / 32** |
| opening topics supplied by anything else | **4 / 32** |
| opening topics producible in the raw spelling the gate compares | **2 / 32** |
| opening topics that exist as a dialogue topic id | **0 / 32** |
| rumour lines anywhere in `game/data/dialogue/**` naming a main-quest noun | **0** |
| code consumers of `opens_by.overheard_from` | **0** |

A player who boots this build, walks to Soulrest and stands in front of Undersexton Aveline Rell
cannot start the main quest, and no sentence anywhere in the world tells them the words that would
let them.

### Why the green trace does not contradict this

`tools/quests/mainline-trace.mjs` calls `H.learnTopic(step.topic)` with the exact string the gate is
about to ask for, immediately before asking. That is `RI-MTH07`'s hand-feed failure in its purest
form — *"a rule that is only reachable by telling the engine what it should have observed is an
orphan predicate"* — and it is invisible to every tool in the tree, because **every** quest tool
seeds the gate the same way.

The builder shipped a sabotage control, `--sabotage drop-topic`, and reported it as proof the
instrument can go red. **Run against the shipping build it is not a control; it is the
measurement.** It stops at `Q-MAIN-01` with the refusal *"the topic \"the drowned tally\" has not
come up yet"* and writes **0 journal entries**. That is what a player gets.

### An independent witness landed mid-run

`game/src/core/topics.js` appeared **untracked** during this measurement and its header states the
same finding independently — *"74 of the 76 `opens_by.topic` values could not be satisfied by any
AddTopic edge the machine can fire … so the mainline traced green while no quest in the build could
be opened by playing it."* It is imported by `game/src/character/converse.js` and **not** by
`game/src/sim/quest/gate.js` or `game/src/sim/quest/machine.js`, so at `be17753` the fold does not
reach the quest gate. And the fold is only half the fix: it would lift the world-suppliable count
from 2/32 to 27/32 in *spelling* terms while leaving **28 of 32 edges still pointing at themselves
or at nothing**. The bootstrap is a missing authored edge, not a missing string rule.

**Remedy.** (1) Import `topicKey` into `sim/quest/gate.js` and fold both topic comparisons and the
`topicsKnown` set. (2) Re-author the AddTopic graph to point **forward** — every main quest's
opening topic granted by an earlier quest, a book or a rumour, never by its own index 10 — and give
`Q-MAIN-01` a world-side entry that owes nothing to a prior quest: ≥1 rumour line in a settlement
other than Soulrest naming the Drowned Tally, plus content on the three NPCs already named in its
`opens_by.overheard_from`, and a consumer for that field.

**Acceptance.** `mainline-findability.mjs` reports offerable ≥ 1 and blocked-on-topic ≤ 31 at a cold
start, **and** `mainline-trace.mjs` with every `H.learnTopic()` call removed completes 29/29 and
17/17. Equivalently `supplied_only_by_itself = 0` and `no_supplier_at_all ≤ 1`.

`dunmer/interior` finishes and `dunmer/lukiul` does not, so the discriminating field is upbringing
alone — a single-field difference, which is the discipline `RI-CHR02` method 8 asks for. The
province's own two races, Saxhleel and Naga, are the ones locked out of Act IV.

### The design call, judged: the clamp did not preserve the authored intent

The wave-3 note on nine main-spine gates says each was *"clamped to the cold-start standing of the
WORST of the 540 signatures at this giver"*. `tools/quests/mainline-gate-margin.mjs` measures what
that leaves:

> **All nine clamped gates have a worst-signature cold-start margin of exactly 0.** No signature is
> below any gate at a cold start — which is precisely why the offer-gate probe that certified the
> fix passes — and every one of them fails on the first point of erosion.

`tools/quests/mainline-block-attribute.mjs` names the erosion term by term, from the live gate's own
decomposition:

```
saxhleel/interior at salt-factor-neloth-vaun
  15  cold start          race_term -30, faction 0        gate is 15  → margin 0
   3  after Q-MAIN-01 / res_carry (moved -12)             faction 0 → -12
      → Q-MAIN-23, twenty-two quests later, refused for good
```

**The main quest closes itself.** The very first quest of the chain, on the intended non-violent
route, grants the Drowned Court reputation; the derived `faction` term goes negative; the standing
falls under a gate that was set with no room. Nothing the player can do restores it, because the
thing that subtracted it *is* the main quest.

One rule mechanically applied is the right instinct, and no base disposition was altered, which is
also right. What was wrong was the choice of clamp point: the worst **cold-start** standing, on a
build whose own content moves that standing by −12 before the first act is over. The clamp converted
a permanent exclusion into a delayed one and moved it out of the reach of the only instrument that
was watching. The W1-07 r4 builder wrote down this exact shape — *"it can close the MAIN spine on a
route the player chose"* — and declared it out of scope; nobody re-ran the completion trace, because
no completion trace took a character.

**Fix:** clamp to `worst cold-start standing − (largest cumulative negative faction term the
mainline can itself produce at that giver)`, then re-run the sweep to 40/40.

## 9. Answering the two questions the dispatch asked plainly

**Can every race and upbringing finish?** No. **29 of 40** signatures complete both chains. The
eight Saxhleel and Naga signatures die in Act IV and three of four Dunmer die at quest nine of
twenty-nine, and the cause is measured to the term.

**Could a person without the trace find their way?** No. Not to the end, not to the middle, and not
to the first quest. There is no rumour, no topic, no line of dialogue anywhere in the world that
contains the words `Q-MAIN-01` requires, and the one field that was authored to carry them —
`opens_by.overheard_from` — is read by nothing.

The architecture underneath is genuinely at this item's top band, and I could not falsify a single
structural check. It is a very good main quest that no one can currently begin.
