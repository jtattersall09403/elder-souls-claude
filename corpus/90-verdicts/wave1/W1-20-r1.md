## What is genuinely good here, and should not be lost in the number

The **model** is the best faction data in this project and it is not close. Every rank on all eight
ladders gates on reputation *and* an attribute *and* two skills *and* a world state.
`favoured_attributes` and `favoured_skills` are per line. `exclusivity` carries hard groups, enemy
pairs and *earned* rivalries, each with a named escape hatch. `faction-discipline.json` has
expulsion **and readmission** — a gold price, a reputation floor, a reputation cost, an offering
NPC, and a separate line for when you are short. Measured through play: joining the Drowned Court
locks the Assize, and the Assize refuses in its own voice naming the reason. That is Morrowind's
texture, in data, working.

The gap is not imagination. It is that seven-eighths of the ladder has no quests on it.

## The dispatch's premise about the refusal is wrong, and I am ruling against it

My brief says: *"a refusal that states no threshold is worse than one that does — the round added
one and called it a feature; decide whether it is."*

**The refusal does state the threshold.** *"The slate has 0 beside your name and a Leaf has 10."*
The round's phrase *"It states no threshold"* means the **JSON authors no hardcoded number** —
every figure is filled from `FactionGates.evaluate()` at speak time, so the prose cannot drift from
the ladder. That is the correct construction and it is the best single decision in the piece.
The wording invited the misreading; the design is right. **Reversible** if someone shows a spoken
refusal that omits the number a player needs — I checked seven and all seven carry it.

## Biggest gap

**`GAP-W1-faction-ladders-are-doors-onto-empty-corridors`.** Eight eight-rank ladders; three lines
carry 18 quests each and five carry between 1 and 4. Five of eight hard-fail RI-QST01's volume

**Remedy, buildable:** the four new lines need quests at ranks 1–7, not a second quest at rank 1;
`the_dockhands` needs at least one `deceit != null` quest at rank ≤ 3 per RI-QST01 Table B; and
`faction-discipline.json` needs rows for the four new lines. The volume clause is the expensive one
and it is wave-2 work the round already named in ruling S-W1-20-C.

## Two errors of fact in the round's report

1. **One joining quest pays 20 reputation, not 10.** All four quests declare
   `faction_reputation: 10` at quest level *and* again on every resolution; `mergeConsequences()`
   sums them. The report's *"grants exactly the rank-1 demand of 10 and no more"* is false as
   measured, and it cites RI-QST03 §B's +4..+10 cap in the same document its own quests breach 2×.
2. **`deep_kin` is not one body under two ids.** The world tells the player *"It is the same
   people and the same door."* Joining the Xul-Aneekh through play leaves `deep_kin` at **`null`**
   and derived rank 0 while `the_xul_aneekh` reaches reputation 46. The two ladders carry different
   rank names. The exemption rests on a sentence the running world contradicts.

## The instrument finding, which is the one worth reading

The round's best work was catching its own **inert control**: a fit computed against the same
variable the wrapper uses. It repaired it by moving the comparison to `ui/system.js`. **I broke the
wrap on purpose and the repair holds** — `maxW = 99999` turns all seven lines red, 97.7 to 423.4 px
off a 400 px panel, and my independent yardstick (the text register's own per-draw-call advance
width, which `hud.js` does not publish) agrees to within 1 px.

**But the comparison moved and the measurement did not.**

```js
fits: (m.widest_px || 0) <= t.rect[2] + 0.5,     // ui/system.js:1167
```

`m.widest_px` is still published by the thing under test, and `|| 0` defaults it to the passing
side. Drop that one field from `hud.js`'s `meta` — the ordinary outcome of refactoring that block,
which `W1-HUD-TOAST` is doing in a live worktree right now — and `fits` reports **true on all seven
lines while all seven run 203–423 px off the paper**. The round's own probe then **exits 0** with a
summary byte-identical to the clean run, `problems: []` and `refusals_spoken_and_drawn_on_screen: 7`.

A yardstick you compute from the subject is not saved by moving the comparison somewhere else. It
is saved by measuring the subject from outside it — which the text register already does, for free,
in every build.

## A correction to my own predecessor

The round-1 critic's L4 concluded that rank reaches no dialogue consumer, from `93 → 93` after
poking the stored rank 0→1. `Engine._questFactionsView()` folds the derived rank in as
`Math.max(stored, highestQualifying())`, and the derived rank was **already 1** — so the poke moved
no number and every consumer correctly reported no change. **That was an inert control**, the same
failure the round itself was caught by. Driven properly, dialogue disposition moves 44→65, 59→90.5
and 82→100 across ranks 0–7. It is reported here rather than carried forward.
