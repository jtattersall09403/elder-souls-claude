**And the screen says so in writing.** Its own foot hint, photographed in
`screens/critic-journal-stuck-search__1920x1080.png`, reads:

> *"Walk the letters, confirm to add one, **back to remove one**. Results are in the order they

the screen has written a promise for and does not keep — and `backOrSub()`'s first branch is
`if (j.query.length) { j.query = j.query.slice(0, -1); return this.mode; }`, which is that promise,
implemented, unreachable. Second:
**confirm on a weapon row, out of combat, changes nothing you can see.** Sixty frames with the screen
open: equipment unchanged, paper doll unchanged, list unchanged, detail unchanged, frame counter
frozen at 5. It is not dead — close the screen, let ninety frames run, and it lands, with a toast.

morning.** Every screen draws its panel at the same fixed `[200, 160, 1520, 780]` — **57.2% of the

## The single biggest gap

**`GAP-W1-ui-journal-search-view-has-no-exit`.** One press of `confirm` on the journal's chronicle
puts `focus.journal.view` into `'search'`, and **no input in the build's closed action set returns
it** — fifteen tried, plus both axes. `roll` exits the whole screen; walking to a peer and back
returns to the search box with the query intact. The player loses the journal for the session, and
the button that does it is the one the interface tells them to press.

**The remedy is one line, and the code for it is already written.** `game/src/ui/system.js:711`
defines `backOrSub()`, which leaves the search view for the chronicle, then the index for the
chronicle, then the screen — exactly the behaviour required — and `step()` calls `this.back()`
instead. Change that call to `this.backOrSub()`. **Acceptance:** from `mode: 'journal'`,
`focus.journal.view === 'search'`, a real `roll` press leaves `mode === 'journal'` with
`focus.journal.view === 'chronicle'`; a second `roll` leaves the screen; and re-entering the journal
from any peer returns `view: 'chronicle'`. Re-run
`node tools/ui/t4-r2-critic-focus.mjs` and `JS1`/`JS2` must both go green, and the delete-the-fix arm
(restore `this.back()`) must turn them red again.

## Secondary observations — not work for the next builder, but every one has a remedy

1. **The equip control is silent out of combat, and this is the piece's second hard fail.** Not
   listed as the gap only because exactly one is permitted. `_applyUIPending()` sets
   `_equipCommit.at = frame + 30` and `_finishEquipCommit()` waits for `sim.frame >= at` — on a
   paused frame that never arrives, so the screen shows nothing until it is closed. **Remedy:** draw
   the pending equip on the row and on the doll the moment `_queue({kind:'equip'})` fires — a
   "putting on" state — or, out of combat only, resolve the commitment immediately, since P7's 30
   committed frames exist to make you vulnerable *in a fight* and there is no fight. Acceptance:
   confirm on a weapon row out of combat changes at least one declared element within 15 frames.
2. **On touch, no list on any screen can be walked.** The stick registers (`stick.y = -1`) and
   `input.moveX/moveY` stay 0 through four fixed steps. **Remedy:** find the link between
   `TouchInput.tick()`'s `pipe.setMove()` and the UI driver's read of `uiMoveX || moveX` — the
   `menuOpen` path is the first place to look — and add a `--leg menu-stick` arm to
   `tools/touch/critic-fight.mjs` that fails when a drag with a screen open does not move
   `getUIState().focus`.
3. **`hud.place` renders the raw cell id.** The world capture reads **`thorn-hall`**, lowercase and
   hyphenated. `RI-UIX07` W5 asks for *"the name of the cell you just entered"*. **Remedy:** give
   cells a `display_name` in `game/data/world/` and read it here; acceptance is that no
   `place_name` element's text matches `/^[a-z0-9-]+$/`.
4. **The panel is a fixed 57.2% box on every screen and `ARBITRATION` S56 already ruled on it.** Not
   a gap because the ruling is the dispatch. The arithmetic, so the next builder has a target rather
   than an instruction: at the matter each screen already draws, `D2 ≥ 0.35` is reached at
   **24.9%** of frame for the inventory, **21.5%** for the journal, **14.9%** for level-up, **14.6%**
   for the sheet and **11.5%** for spells. Morrowind's inventory window is ~24% of its frame. Sizing
   the panel to its contents clears P4 everywhere **with no new content at all**, and
   `crops/sheet-panel-rect-1x.png` — dense for three-fifths, then nothing — is the picture of why.
5. **`two_hand` (the HUD full/minimal switch) is NOT broken, and finding that out is the most useful
   thing I did for the next critic.** I recorded it as a failure three times and it was my clock
   every time. The fourth pass read the pipeline at four points and the answer is in one field:
   after `stepFrames(20)` with the inventory open, **`getFrame()` is still 5**. The screen pauses the
   world out of combat — correctly, that is M-P1 — and `hold-gate.js`'s `inputNow()` returns
   `frame * STEP_MS` in every mode except `play`, so in a harness **the hold clock is the paused
   clock and a gated hold can never be crossed while a paused screen is open**. `framesHeld` comes
   out 0 against a 12-frame gate; `_holds.KeyG.fired` stays `false`; no edge reaches the latch. A
   real player is unaffected — in mode `play` the gate reads `event.timeStamp`. **This is a general
   trap, not a `two_hand` fact**, and it is now written into `RI-UIX10` §B as I2 so that the next
   critic does not fail a healthy control on it, as I nearly did. To measure a gated hold on a
   paused screen: use mode `play`, or step the world with the screen shut.
6. **The category column is drawn as tab-shaped plates**, which is Morrowind-faithful (its inventory
   has category tabs) and is also the strongest click affordance on a screen with no pointer path.
   Not a failure; worth a decision if a pointer path is ever added.
7. **Two item ids are still defined twice** — `stamped-writ` in `writ.json` and `carried.json`,
   `rotted-hall-key` in `carried.json` and `thorn.json` — with different fields each time, so which
   record wins decides whether the item has a description, and it is why the empty-description count
   reads 4 rather than round 1's 2. Round 1 recorded this and no corpus row covers id uniqueness.
8. **The paper doll is a grey mannequin.** It satisfies `RI-UIX09` P2 — it is a drawn figure and it
   changes with what is equipped — and it is not good. This is the seam `CLAUDE.md`'s character
   directive names: `RI-VIS08` §D covers creature *fidelity* and stops short of humanoid *design*,
   and nothing else covers it. I did not extend the bar there: that is a reference **set**, not a
   row, and §1.3's third guard says do not build a piece of work inside a critique.

## What would make this verdict wrong

**The strongest evidence here is the withdrawal series, the pause block and the D2 cross-check** —
a twenty-frame element census across a boundary I created, raw frame counts, and two independent
implementations of one formula agreeing to four decimals. Those are the least likely to be wrong.

**The three weakest claims, named so they can be attacked:**

1. **`RI-UIX10` OP3 (touch).** I measured the break between `touch.tick()` and the pipeline, not its
   cause, and my drag was two synthetic `PointerEvent`s rather than a finger. If a real handheld
   walks a list, OP3 flips and `RI-UIX10` goes to 6/8 — still capped at 2 by OP5 and OP7.
2. **`RI-UIX06` ART 4.** AD4 is fail-closed on a measurement I did not take and would very likely
   pass on `--blank-world` captures; AD7's cap reading is round 1's, adopted rather than derived. If
   both moved, ART is 7/7 and the item is 6 — and `min(ART, FIDELITY)` is still unevaluated, which
   is the sentence that actually matters.
3. **`RI-UIX07` V1 and V10.** V1 passes on five of six observed with W6 (breath) never made true, and
   V10 passes on the declared half with the `ui-world` sweep still non-existent. Fail-closing either
   takes the item to 9/11, which does not change its ladder 5 — the cap is §G, not the count.

**And the two hard fails are the claims I would most like to be wrong about and am most confident
in**, because both were found by pressing buttons and recorded with the state after every press:
fifteen actions that do not leave a search box, and sixty frames in which a confirm changed nothing
on the screen.
