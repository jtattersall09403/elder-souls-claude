## 1. The owner's question, answered first

> *"are the controls intuitive and do they work — I want them to work both on desktop **and** on
> mobile with a controller attached — I have this one **GameSir X2s Type-C**."*

**On desktop: yes.** Sixteen actions on their normative primary controls, matched on
`KeyboardEvent.code` so AZERTY and QWERTZ work, auto-repeat producing exactly one action, held
actions released within one frame of blur / visibility change / pointer-lock loss with **0.00 m**
of displacement after the committed state ends, look sensitivity identical at 0/15/30/60 Hz render,
and **p100 = 1 frame** of input latency over 200 presses.

**On a pad: very probably yes, and the hard part was done properly.** Both `souls-default` and
`souls-handheld` match the spec exactly with nothing unbound. `pad-quirks.json` carries **both**
GameSir X2s Type-C enumeration modes — the generic-HID entry `3537:1004` with the DualSense-like
face-cluster permutation, hat-axis D-pad and analog triggers on axes 3/4, and the standard-mapping
identity entry — and it labels its own provenance `canonical-recall, confidence medium` rather than
pretending to be a measurement. An id it does not recognise reaches a pad-only calibration that
completed in **6 inputs against a budget of 8**. Disconnect releases everything on the disconnect
frame, 0.00 m of displacement, no modal, sim keeps running for 301 frames.

**On touch alone: no.** Every one of the sixteen actions is reachable, the stick floats, four
pointers register simultaneously, nothing sits in a safe-area inset and every target is ≥ 44 CSS
px — and **none of it is drawn**. There is nothing on the glass to press.

## 3. The single biggest gap

**`GAP-W1-input-discoverability-unmeasured`** — `input.discoverability` — severity **major**.

> RI-JRN03's entire "Discoverability without instruction" block — 14 of its 100 points, and the
> home of hard fail **HF5** — is unmeasured, and the one check in it that reports a result reports
> it from an empty buffer.

`M-K20` searches `getRenderedText()` for instruction tokens and **passed with `frames: 0`**. I drove
that register in seven named states — `arena_flat`, `helstrom-market`, `thorn-hall`, `ui-journal`,
`npc_showcase`, `default`, `settlement_primary_street` — and through `uiOpen('dialogue')`,
`uiOpen('menu')` and `uiOpen('rest')`:

```
entries: 0    distinct_count: 0    summary.surfaces: {}      — in every single one
```

…while `getUIState()` at the same instant reports **three HUD elements carrying drawn text**:
`hud.heal → "5"`, `hud.equipload → "LIGHT"`, `hud.quickslots → "Spark-Dart"`.
`game/src/render/text-register.js` instruments only the `dialogue` and `title` surfaces and neither
ever writes. **The check cannot fail.**

And it is not alone. **Thirteen scored check ids return `0` to `grep -c` over the instrument:**

|---|---|---|
| RI-JRN03 | `M-K8` (HF3), `M-K9` (HF4), `M-K21`, `M-K22` (HF5), `M-N1`, `M-N2`, `M-N3`, `M-N5` (HF10), `M-N7` | **28 / 100** |

## 5. Instruments that pass while their own data shows the problem

The literal `r.frame`-vs-`f` bug does **not** reproduce here. The *shape* appears twice:

- **`M-P21a`** computes `unreached = entries where !v` — *something fired* — and never
  `v === action`. Its own artifact records `reach.roll = "sprint"` and passes. A real misroute
  would be invisible to it. (Here the build is right and only the check is blind.)
- **`M-K20`** passes on an empty buffer, as above. `M-P24` (HF7) is folded into the same
  `record()` call and inherits it.
- **`M-K1`** uses `fired.includes(action)` rather than set equality, so a control firing the right
  action *plus extras* would pass. Here it does not matter — E2 found no double-binds — but the
  predicate is one binding away from hiding the exact defect the builder just fixed.

**The `consumeUI` trap does not reproduce.** `c3` recorded FAIL for the *opposite* reason to the one
expected: the boot sweep and the arena sweep are **identical**, and both show a correct radial
deadzone (0 at 0.10 and 0.14, 0.5844 at 0.60, 1.0 at 0.92, `boot_ui: []`). The builder's warning
appears stale. I checked the second half of the question directly: every pad and touch probe reads
`H.getInputEdges()`, not `held`. **Nothing else in the suite reads the wrong one.**

## 6. No physical pad — adequate, and not a lowered bar

RI-JRN04's own "How we lose" #1 is *"`mapping === 'standard'` is assumed… on the actual GameSir X2s
in its default DualSense-like HID mode the roll button is the menu button."* **That failure mode is
avoided in code, not assumed away.** Both branches are implemented; both of the device's
enumeration modes are in the table; the recall is labelled as recall; and anything the table gets
wrong reaches a pad-only calibration in ≤ 8 inputs with in-fiction prompts (*"Swing."*, *"Take it on
the shield."*) rather than a control legend.

That is the right shape for a device nobody can plug in, and **it is not a request to lower a bar**
— no threshold was softened anywhere. It is also **not a measurement**. Ten real-hardware
behaviours remain unverified, and the verdict carries `unverified_on_hardware:gamesir-x2s-type-c`
in `status_reasons` so the debt is carried forward rather than closed by argument.

## 7. Arbitration

- **AR-1 (Souls leakage): PASS.** No roll-to-hit anywhere in the input path. No pause mid-fight —
  `M-P12` disconnects the pad during play and the sim advances 301 frames with 0 modal surfaces,
  upholding seam S14 on exactly the case the item predicts. No input-driven animation cancel:
  blur releases held actions in 1 frame but records `displacement_after_commitment_m: 0.0000`, so
  the committed state finishes first. **S33 applied as ruled** — `pipeline_drops: 0` over 10 000
  inputs with the 9 487 combat-refused presses kept in a *separate* counter. A press lost in
  transport is zero; a press refused by a window is the combat system working. The two were not
  conflated in either direction.
- **AR-2 (Morrowind leakage): PASS.** No map, no marker (`map_exists: false`, upholding S30). Every
  refusal is in fiction: *"The bindings hold. That hand belongs to the window, not to you."*,
  *"Leave it unbound and you leave it undone."*, *"That one answers to the machine before it
  answers to you."*
- **AR-3 (seam sterility): NOT STERILE, but thin.** One real crossing: the input layer refuses
  presses the combat model rejects and keeps that refusal distinct from transport loss, and `c2`
  shows the desktop `interact` key producing `INPUT_DROPPED:parley` — a keyboard press arriving at
  a Morrowind-side parley window and being refused by it. Recorded as thin rather than inflated.
- **CONSUMPTION (RI-MTH07): FAIL on the touch and rotate models**, coupling **0**, with a stable
  null control and a positive control. §4 above.
- **S32 respected.** The item owns the raw 0.14 deadzone (a device property) and the artifacts make
  no claim about the response curve above it, which belongs to RI-CAM02.

## 9. What is genuinely very good, so the next round does not regress it

Latency **p100 = 1 frame** over 200 inputs on keyboard *and* pad, confirmed by a second independent
sim-side observer. Both pad profiles exact. The roll/sprint 12-frame discriminator correct on pad
**and** on touch. A **genuinely radial** deadzone (max/min ratio `1.0000000000000007` at 0.60), not
the axial one-liner the item predicts. Disconnect handled without a modal. `dvh`, not `vh`.
Rebinding completable on keyboard, pad and touch, naming the previous owner before commit and
refusing to orphan an action — all in fiction. And the heavy attack reaching its normative primary
key again is a real fix to a real defect.

The gap is not that this build is careless. It is that the instrument cannot see the parts of it
that are missing.
