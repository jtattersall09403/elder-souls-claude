  figure in the corner is wearing it."*

  commitment to be short — it allows 30 frames provided the screen says so, and round 3 also built the
  saying-so. So O3 could have been satisfied without touching the commitment at all, and the item's
  property text was changed in effect by a build rather than by an amendment.

## What would make this verdict wrong

**The strongest evidence here is the byte-identical re-run.** `focus.mjs` at 12/13 against the
builder's control-worktree 8/13 is the same instrument, the same fixture, the same resolution, one
build apart, and it is the least likely thing in this document to be wrong. The `D2` table is next: two
independent implementations of one formula agreeing to four decimals on six screens.

**The three weakest claims, named so they can be attacked:**
