## 4. On `greetings.json`: a declared expansion, and my ruling on it

The builder discloses, in the file's own `provenance` block and again at the top of
`tools/dialogue/gen-greetings.mjs`, that the 1,500 lines are a combinatorial expansion of **300
authored stances × 60 authored addresses = 360 fragments**, and asks to be challenged on it.

I read the generator and sampled the output. **It meets `RI-CHR02` §4c's intent, and I would not
call it padding.** Three reasons, each checkable:

1. **The stances are genuinely authored and genuinely different.** All 300 are distinct prose in
   the item's register. RG-VAKH hostile: *"The water took the wrong ones and left you."* RG-LEDGER
   friendly: *"There is a copy of your file and there is now not a copy of your file."* RG-DRES
   hostile: *"You are worth more in a net than in a conversation."* Nobody generated these.
2. **The distinctness survives the expansion.** 1,400 of 1,500 lines are distinct. Per
   `(group, band)` across all five player-race classes, distinct lines run **5 to 25** out of a
   possible 25 — and the only cell at 5 is RG-COURT, which is all zeroes in the matrix and is
   *supposed* to say the same thing to everybody. That is the item's own design showing through
   the data, not a collapse.
3. **It is the shape of the thing being copied.** Morrowind's greeting table is a small authored
   set indexed by faction, race and disposition and recombined at runtime. A build that hand-wrote
   1,500 independent lines would be *less* like the reference, not more.

Where I do charge it: **the volume axis is at its 6 band, not its 10.** `RI-CHR02`'s scoring gives
10 only for *"all §4c minimums met with ≥ 20% headroom"*, and the build lands on the floors almost
exactly — greetings 1,500 / 1,500 (**0%**), race-gated topics 96 / 90 (6.7%), `forbids` 32 / 30
(6.7%), rumours 16 / 16 (**0%**), slavery lines 61 / 60 (1.7%) with 16 / 12 to a Dunmer (33%).
Hitting five floors to within one unit is a decision about where to stop, and the item priced that
decision at 6. And `rumours.json` declares itself incomplete for the non-race-gated variants.

So: **a declared expansion, honestly labelled, of real authored material — and simultaneously a
content set built to the floor rather than past it, which is why the axis is a 6 and not a 10.**
Both halves are true and the builder was right to invite the question.

## 5. The gamepad path

The project's owner tests on a GameSir X2s Type-C. I confirmed the shim is not a separate,
flattering path:

- `__HARNESS.gamepad(state)` → `RealInput.pushGamepadState(state)` → sets `this.syntheticPad` →
  **calls `this.pollGamepad()`**.
- A physical pad → `engine.loop.beforeTick` → **`this.real.pollGamepad()`** (`engine.js:234`).

One function. It decodes the W3C standard mapping (`GAMEPAD_BINDINGS.buttons`: A=0 interact, B=1
roll, LT=6 block, RT=7 heavy, D-pad 12–15 driving `move`), fires `pipe.edgeDown` on the same edges,
and shapes the right stick through the same `shapeLookStick` a scripted look uses. The shim's only
divergence is that it normalises `buttons` to `{pressed, value}` objects and pads `axes` to four —
which is what a standard-mapping pad already reports.

**Completed on the pad alone**, via the questionnaire route:

```
completed:            true
button presses:       28
left-stick walks:     1        (out of the hold, nothing pressed, nothing explained)
activeDevice:         "gamepad"
pollGamepad calls:    85
character:            khajiit / Ma'zharra / Silence-Under-Salt / interior / raj-xul
writ:                 present
```

Text entry has gamepad parity via `creation-names.json`'s ledger of names, which is the right
answer — an on-screen keyboard would be the full-screen panel O8 forbids.

**Also completed on keyboard alone** with real DOM key events (not `queueInputs`): 17 presses. One
minor defect there: typing `BJORN` produced `bjon`, so the text path drops characters under fast
input.

The custom-class route is the exception, and it is a hard one: on a pad it throws (§2).

## 7. Prices, and the builder's three disputes

### Dispute 1 — "§4b double-applies the race term". **Upheld, and it is a corpus defect.**

`RI-PRG05`'s barter model is `buy_mult = clamp(1.55 − 0.0060·Disposition − 0.0035·Mercantile −
0.0040·Personality, 0.80, 1.60)`. `RI-CHR02` §4a puts the race term *inside* Disposition
(`derivedDisposition` adds it "before every other term"). §4b then applies
`raceSurcharge = 1.00 − 0.006·r` **on top**, with the identical 0.006 coefficient. Race is counted
twice, at the same weight. That is a genuine collision between two items and the builder is right
to name it. Filed as an arbitration question, not scored against the build.

The build's chosen resolution is to feed `priceQuote()` a hard-coded `disposition: 50` so the term
is applied once. That is defensible, and it has a cost the builder did not mention: **a merchant's
own derived disposition never reaches their prices.** The Deep-Kin draught-seller at disposition 6
quotes as though they were at 50. `RI-PRG05`'s scoring names exactly this — "Disposition and
Mercantile are *read by the shop*, not merely defined". Recorded as a secondary observation.

### Dispute 2 — "method 7's table is the surcharge alone, not the quote". **Upheld.**

At base 60, `{54, 70, 76}` is `60 ×` the §4b surcharge multipliers with no `RI-PRG05` term. A real
untrained quote is 60 × 1.2085 × 1.264 = **92 g**. The build reports both, correctly labelled
(`buy_surcharge_only: 76`, `buy: 92`), which is the right way to ship a disputed bar. Method 7's
text should say "surcharge multiplier", not "quoted price".

### Dispute 3 — "`RI-CHR03`'s drawback". **Already won, in S27, on the builder's reading.** The
amendment is written and the build honours it. See §3 for the one thing still missing.

## 10. The single biggest gap

### `GAP-W1-questionnaire-route-is-unreadable-and-unnamed` — `character.creation.flow`, blocking

**What.** The questionnaire — `RI-CHR01` §4 Route C, `RI-JRN01` O9's mandatory route, and the one
device Morrowind's opening is famous for — reaches the player with neither its questions nor its
answer. (a) `buildCensusModel()` (`game/src/character/scene.js:267`) sets `line: state.line` and
never reads `state.question.text`, so all ten dilemma nodes draw one identical stock line above four
unattached answers: **10 distinct question texts in the model, 1 distinct drawn line, 0 of 10
reaching the frame**. (b) Across **240 runs** covering all 10 races × 4 upbringings × 8 answer
permutations with a verified even answer histogram (600 each of a/b/c/d), the route produced a
named class **0 times out of 14**, against `RI-CHR01`'s pass floor of ≥ 6 and its 0-band of < 6.

**Why it matters.** A player taking the route this game's opening is built around is shown forty
answers to questions that are never asked, and is never once told what profession they turned out
to be — the moment the route exists for does not occur, in any configuration.

**Remedy.**

1. In `buildCensusModel()`, when `state.input.kind === 'questionnaire'`, set
   `line: state.question.text` and move the scribe's transition line to the `aside` slot (or to the
   `writ.class-routes` answer, where it belongs). One line; the model already carries the text.
2. In `character/questionnaire.js`'s `scoreAnswers`, make the named-class match reachable: the
   twelve dilemmas weight three skills per answer, and the matcher must compare the resulting
   3-primary/2-secondary set against `classes.json`'s fourteen with a tolerance (e.g. ≥ 3 of 5
   shared skills and a matching family) rather than requiring an exact set. `census.js` already has
   `on_match_named_class` and `%ClassName` wired for the moment it succeeds.
3. In `CensusSurface.sync()`, filter a `pick` node's options against the attributes already taken by
   earlier pick nodes, so `writ.class-custom-neglected` cannot offer a favoured attribute — and wrap
   `_censusApplyPending()` so a refused answer can never throw out of the frame step.

**Acceptance** (`RI-CHR01` M5/M7, `RI-JRN01` M5/M7):
`getCensusState().surface.rendered_text` contains `getCensusState().question.text` at **10 of 10**
questionnaire nodes, and the 10 nodes yield 10 distinct drawn lines; a 240-run sweep over all
10 races × 4 upbringings with an even a/b/c/d histogram reaches **≥ 6 distinct named `class_id`
values** (≥ 10 for the item's 10 band) and the Warden-Scribe's `on_match_named_class` line fires on
every named match; and the full custom route completes on gamepad alone with **0** exceptions out of
`stepFrames`.

**Size:** S. **Target:** wave 2.
