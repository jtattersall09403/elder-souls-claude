## 3. How the S60 measurement was made

S60 replaced F4's acceptance with two clauses, **both required**:
**(a)** `key_off ≥ 2× env_off` on the **lit subset** — the pixels the shadow map reports unoccluded;
**(b)** **preservation** — the cast-shadow **area fraction does not fall** in any window.

### 3a. How I got a lit mask, and why it is not circular

A pixel is **shadowed** when switching the sun's shadow map **off** makes it brighter by more than
`tau`. That is the shadow map reporting occlusion, which is what S60 names. It is a *different
experiment* from `key_off` — which zeroes the light itself — so the mask is not derived from the
quantity it restricts. I did **not** need S60's weaker full-frame fallback.

`tau` is not chosen to make an answer come out: every number below is reported as a curve over
`tau ∈ {1, 2, 4, 8, 16}`, and a conclusion that survived at only one `tau` would be reported as not
surviving. None of them depend on `tau`.

### 3b. The delete-the-fix arm is a real one

The builder's `revert_day` is a page-side rig edit, and `sky.js:944` bakes the environment probe with
`sunGain: R.key * max(0.05, day)` — so tripling `noon-marsh.key` also tripled the sun lobe **inside
the probe texture**, and no page-side arm can reach a bake. The builder said so and priced the error
at one window.

I built the before arm as a **hard-linked copy of `game/` with exactly two source lines reverted**
(`noon-marsh` back to `key 1.00 / sky 0.42 / fill 0.30 / env 1.00`; `night-moon` back to
`moon 1.00 / env 0.75`) and served the page from that tree. That revert reaches the bake. The repo's
own `game/` was never touched — verified with `git status` before and after.

**It lands where the true pre-change capture landed.** My reverted tree measures the `pair01` judged
crop at mean luma **59.142**; the builder's genuine pre-change capture from before the edit measures
**59.149**. Two source trees, two browser processes, 0.01% apart.

## 4. `RI-VIS04` §2 — the metric the corpus already had, and what it says

`RI-VIS04` §14 row 2 is *IBL / environment map*, its DETECT is **`M6 hue_offset`**, and its
"Blocks score above" value is **5**. `RI-VIS03` M6 defines it: the angle between the chroma-weighted
circular-mean hue of the lit quartile and of the shadowed quartile of the foreground.

> *"A single white `DirectionalLight` plus a white `AmbientLight` produces `hue_offset ≈ 0`, and this
> metric catches it in one number."* — `RI-VIS03` M6

Thresholds, `exterior_daylight`: `retention ≥ 0.60` (hard fail < 0.30), **`hue_offset ≥ 15°`
(hard fail < 6°)**, `C_shadow ≥ 6`, and `RI-VIS03` M2 `C_local_med ≥ 0.045` (hard fail < 0.025).

**This is precisely the metric that separates "the sun dominates the frame" from "the frame got
brighter", and it is the question §1.3 told me to ask of the bar. The bar could already ask it.**

I implemented M6/M2/M7 from the item's own §0 pseudocode in `tools/visual/f4c-analyse.mjs --vis03`
and **validated it against this repo's own vendored reference population** before pointing it at our
build — see §7.

## 7. Two instrument findings, verified

### 7a. `env_off` as `scene.environment = null` versus `environmentIntensity = 0` — **equivalence PROVEN**

The builder replaced the nulling form after it killed the page, and claimed equivalence at 19.8203
against 19.8203. **That claim is true and I could check it because the tool writes its own config
source into every report.**

| run | at | `env_off` implementation | `pair01` shipped mean | `env_off` delta |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `f4-timing` | 15:28 | `__scene().environment = null` | 59.1417 | **19.8226** |
| `f4-base-p01` | 15:57 | `__scene().environment = null` | 59.1490 | **19.8203** |
| `f4-r1b-p01` | 16:10 | `__envScale(0)` | 59.1490 | **19.8203** |

Two implementations, same window, same pre-change build, identical to four decimals, plus a third
replicate. **This belongs in `HAZARDS.md`.**

**But the page-death half of the story is weaker than stated.** The builder's source comment says
*"measured three times in a row on pair01: … the page is dead by the NEXT arm."* On this box's
surviving evidence the nulling form was run **twice**: `f4-base-p01` aborted with
`Target page, context or browser has been closed`, and **`f4-timing` did not** — it ran `env_off`
(nulling) and then `key_off` successfully. Two more runs were lost with no directory (the builder
records four lost runs), so the three may have existed; they are not reproducible from what is on
disk. The fix is right either way, and the equivalence is proven; the causal claim is one-for-two.

### 7b. `sd_luma` as a "not flatter" metric — **the conclusion holds, the stated rule does not**

| pair01 (`f4-verify-p01`) | 26.7582 → 31.7854 | **raises** +18.8% |
| pair05 (`f4-base-p05`) | 29.1724 → 30.0054 | **raises** +2.9% |

### 7c. The `revert_day` `sd` error, and which conclusions rest on it

Validated by the builder at `pair01`: `revert_day` returns `sd` 32.781 against the true pre-change
29.899, a **+9.6%** bias, while mean and chroma are inside noise. Two conclusions in the status file
quote an `sd` from a `revert_day` row:

- **P6, `pair03` "41.110 → 36.449" (0.887×).** Correcting the before value for the measured +9.6%

- **P6, `pair04` "4.180 → 10.515" (2.5×).** Corrected: ≈3.81 → 10.515 = **2.76×**. The claimed gain
  grows.

**Both corrections move in the build's favour.** No conclusion is overturned against it. My
source-level before arm supersedes the proxy for the windows I re-shot.

### 7d. S52 on the null control — it could have moved, for the reason that matters

`prsv-dusk` came back bit-identical over the judged crop and is offered as the null control. Asking
S52's question — *could it have moved?*:

- **Not from the four numbers.** `dusk-canopy`'s own `key/sky/fill/env` are byte-untouched, so a
  value-level move was impossible. As a control on **the values**, it is trivial.
- **Yes, from the code.** The change also adds `moon: 1.0` to `EXTERIOR_RIG`, scales `moon` in
  `resolveLightingRecipe()`, and multiplies `this.moon.intensity` by `R.moon ?? 1` in `sky.js`. At
  06:00 `night = 0.384`, so the moon is **live and non-zero** in that frame. A missing `?? 1`, a
  wrong default, or an arm that scaled the wrong directional would have moved this window. As a
  control on **the code path**, it is real and it is exercised.

So the control is not trivial, but it is a leak check on the refactor, not evidence about the
rebalance — and the builder presented it as the latter ("a clean null control"). Stated correctly it
still earns its place.

**One qualification the builder did not state: the identity is local.** Over the 512×512 crop the two
`prsv-dusk` captures agree to four decimals (32.838 both). Over the **full 1920×1080 frame** they do
not: 38.3583 against 38.1744, a 0.48% difference. Something outside the crop moved between runs.
"The capture is deterministic at that pose" is true of the crop and false of the frame.

  and `dusk-canopy` uses `0.85`, `overcast-flat` `−0.30`, `night-moon` `−0.80`. The daytime key is
  the same colour as everything else.
- F4 then **cut `sky` 0.42 → 0.315 and `env` 1.00 → 0.35**, which are the terms that put the sky's
  cool hue into the shadow side. Tripling a neutral key while cutting the cool fill moves
  `hue_offset` in the wrong direction by construction.

This is also, independently, exactly what all five Protocol A r2 judges wrote in their own words —
*"every surface returns the same flat matte olive-grey"* — and it is why I believe the metric rather
than doubting my transcription: two instruments, one written by a human judge and one by me, agree.

**`RI-VIS04` §14 row 2 is therefore ABSENT by its own DETECT, before and after F4, and its
"Blocks score above" value is 5.**

`RI-VIS03` M2 `C_local_med` is below the `exterior_daylight` minimum of 0.045 in every one of our
frames and below M2's 0.025 *flat shading* hard fail in two of them (`pair04` crop 0.0113 before,
0.0167 after; `pair03` crop 0.0222 after).

## 8a. A defect in `RI-VIS04` §3's DETECT, found while using it

§3 (cascaded shadow maps) says: *"DETECT: M6 `SHADOW_MASK` fraction < 3% in a daylight exterior = no
shadows."*

**That test cannot detect the absence of shadows.** `RI-VIS03` §0 defines
`SHADOW_MASK = FG_MASK AND Yp < percentile(Yp[FG_MASK], 25)` — the darkest quartile of the foreground,
**by construction**. It is ≈25% of the foreground whether the scene casts a single shadow or none at
all. Measured on our frames it is 0.2329–0.2498 in every case, before and after, shadowed and not.
The `< 3%` clause can only fire when the sky occupies more than ~88% of the frame.

This is the same family as S58, S59 and S60: a metric evaluated over a domain that has nothing to do
with the thing it names. It is filed as an ADD in §11 with a test that does work — the one I used for
S60 clause (b), which is derived from the shadow map rather than from a luminance percentile.

## 11. Corpus extension, ADDed and bounded

`RI-VIS04` §3 gains **`§3-D2`** (a cast-shadow DETECT that can actually fire — the shadow-map
ablation mask, reported as a curve over `tau`) and **`§3-D3`** (a preservation clause: `M6 retention`
and `C_shadow` may not fall below their `RI-VIS03` profile minima in a window whose cast-shadow area
rose). Nothing was removed and **no threshold was lowered**.

**Bound, and it matters:** both clauses are DETECT and preservation only. They add **no route to a
higher §14 mark and no route to a higher score for any build** — a critic may cite them to withhold
a mark and never to grant one. This verdict's status is FAIL and neither added clause contributes to
it: the FAIL is set by S60 clause (a), which predates me, and by `RI-VIS04` §2's own pre-existing
DETECT. `§3-D2` is the one clause on which the build **passes**, and it lifts nothing, because the
score is a `min`.

**Overturned if** a shadow-map ablation proves unavailable to a harness, in which case §3 falls back
to the penumbra and cascade-ring tests and records the area clause `UNVERIFIED`, which counts as
absent.
