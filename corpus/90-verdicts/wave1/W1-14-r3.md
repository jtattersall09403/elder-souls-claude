# W1-14 round 3 — Magic (seam S19)

**FAIL — 4.6 / 10 against a wave-1 gate of 7.0.** Round 1: 2.7. Round 2: 4.4.

Round 3 is the first round of this piece where the headline claim survived an independently
written instrument. `DISTINCT-VERBS 53/55` is real. So is `AP-M3`. So is the skill register.
The score barely moves because two things were measured properly for the first time and both
came back zero, and because one new defect — found by obeying the round's own new protocol rule
in a place the builder did not apply it — takes the offensive half of the piece out of the game.

## 1. What I measured and what it says

### 1.1 `DISTINCT-VERBS` survives my own instrument — at skill 100, and only there

I wrote `tools/harness/critic-w1-14-r3-census.mjs` from `RI-MAG06` §D and never ran the
builder's. Three things it does that neither the builder's nor my predecessor's does:

* **A repeat.** Every treatment runs twice from an identically-seeded arena. A leaf path that
  differs between two identical treatment runs is noise, and noise is the obvious way to inflate
  this metric: 55 chaotic diff-sets are 55 distinct signatures.
* **An empty-empty control.** Two null runs diffed against each other, in an empty room and in an
  aggroed one, before anything is cast.
* **`--practice N`**, so §E/M8's re-run is one flag rather than a rewrite.

| | all leaf paths | world-side only | notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **Builder's claim** | 53/55 | 45/55 | plus 43/55 under the r2 flattener |
| **Mine, `--practice 700`** | **53/55** | **43/55** | `moved_anything` 55/55, `not_delivered` 0 |
| **Mine, `--practice 0`** | **30/55** | **25/55** | **25 of 55 effects NOT CASTABLE** |

Control integrity: **null-vs-null drift 0 paths** in both rooms, and **zero effects had an
unstable path across the repeat.** The number is not noise. 53/55 stands.

Exactly two collision groups survive, both named by the builder: `[bind_lesser, bind_greater]`

### 1.2 The arena audit (§E / M8) — the builder replaced one gate with another

**This is a method failure, not a fraud**, and the reason is §1.3.

### 1.4 The camera override is **not** broken

The builder's one open blocker is that `H.camera({pos,look,fov})` "is NOT taking effect".
It is false, and it matters beyond magic, so it was tested from four sides
(`camera.json`, `camera/*.png`):

* readback exact immediately and after 24 steps;
* the override **survives a full spell cast** (pose identical before and after);
* `loadState` clears it, which is correct;
* **the pixels**: builder's own pose vs the default rig **96.94%** changed; a 40 m-high
  fov-20 pose **96.08%**; looking the other way **99.999%**. `camera({})` as a read does not
  install an override, and unknown keys still throw.

Nothing in `game/src/sim/camera.js` or `game/src/render/renderer.js` ignores it: `stepCamera`
short-circuits to `applyOverride` and `renderer.render` reads `sim.camera.pos/pivot/fov`
directly. **No regression to escalate.** RI-MAG05 was therefore judgeable and is judged.

## 3. The single biggest gap

**`GAP-W1-magic-cannot-hit-a-moving-target`** — `magic.casting.geometry`.

A spell does not connect with anything that moves. A LIGHT bolt fired at a body 18 m away deals
**80 damage when the body is still and 0 damage the moment it moves at 1.5 m/s** — slower than
the walk speed measured in the same arena (3.17 m/s) — and 0 at 3, 5 and 7 m/s. `spell_impact`
fires in every case; `effect_apply` fires only at 0 m/s.

This is not a tracking-fence problem: `AP-M3` passes cleanly and the arc is real (60 °/s on
LIGHT, 45 on HEAVY, 21.95° and 24.16° of turn before the fence). The bolt steers and still
misses, because it steers toward where the target was acquired rather than where it is, and the
`0.35 × journey` fence closes before the error is paid off.

**Why it matters more than anything else in this verdict.** Every enemy in this build
repositions; the arena's own bodies sit in `REPOSITION` for the whole of a 900-frame
observation. So the offensive half of the catalogue — every `projectile` and `target`-range
effect, which is where the five damage effects and most of the status verbs live — does not work
against a live opponent at all. It also explains a structural fact about every census this piece
has ever run, including mine: they all pin a body at 1.4 m with `touch` range, because that is
the only geometry that connects. `DISTINCT-VERBS 53/55` is a true statement about a build in
which the thing the player actually does with a spell in a fight does not land.

**Remedy, buildable:** re-acquire the target position each frame the bolt is inside its tracking
window rather than latching it at release (`sim/magic/system.js`, the projectile step), and set
the fence from the *predicted* intercept rather than the acquisition-time journey. Acceptance:
a LIGHT bolt fired at a body 18 m away strafing at 1.5, 3 and 5 m/s deals its full deterministic
damage in ≥ 2 of the 3 rows, with `AP-M3` still reporting 0 violating frames past
`0.35 × travel_f` and `--break=nocutoff` still going red.
