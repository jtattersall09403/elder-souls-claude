## The one gap

**`GAP-W1-legibility-overlap-leg-is-defeated-by-the-gauge-bar`** — blocking, `ui.style.diegesis`.

The S58 legibility pass — without which *no density figure counts* — reports six findings that are
not legibility defects, and reports the same rows clean at the other viewport for an equally
irrelevant reason.

**Remedy.** Profile the row's **text band**, not the whole rect — derive it from the row's declared
rect and the face metrics `drawText` uses, the way `descLineCap()` already does. Then give the leg a
self-test in the shape the COLLISION leg already has: a round-4-style collapsed row that must be
**RED**, a clean row with a full-width gauge that must be **GREEN** (the current leg fails this), and
the same clean row at 720p that must give the **same answer**. Do **not** just rescale `GAP` — the
threshold is not the defect; projecting a gauge and a text line onto one axis is.

**Acceptance.** 0 overlap findings on level-up and the sheet at both viewports, with the round-4
fixture still RED and the two viewports agreeing on every fixture — proven red on an actual capture
of a build whose rows really do collide before it is trusted green.
