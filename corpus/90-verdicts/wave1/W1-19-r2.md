## 2. The single biggest gap — `GAP-W1-quest-givers-not-in-the-world`

`quests.mainline.acts` · **blocking**

> **Nine of the ninety-four quest givers in this build exist anywhere a player can stand. None of
> the thirty-five quests behind the "10 of 35" headline has a giver in the world at all.**

`tools/quests/critic-giver-presence.mjs` (written for this verdict, declared in §8) boots each of
the four town states and asks the running world two questions about every quest that names a giver:
is `quest.giver.npc_id` in `H.listNPCs()`, and does `H.talkTo()` open a conversation with them.

| | |
|---|---:|
| quests with a named giver | **94** |
| givers **talkable** in *any* bootable state | **9** |
| — of 32 **main** quests | **9** (all at `soulrest-quay`) |
| — of 25 **side** quests | **0** |
| — of 36 **faction** quests | **0** |
| — of the **35** quests in the round-2 findability headline | **0** |
| NPCs in the world: `helstrom-market` / `stormhold-street` / `soulrest-quay` / `thorn-hall` | 4 / 3 / 5 / **0** |

The whole inhabited world is **twelve NPCs across three states**. The nine reachable quests are
`Q-MAIN-01`–`05`, `11`, `12`, `16`, `24`, and every one of them is Undersexton Aveline Rell.

### Why the headline does not contradict this

`QuestEngine.open()` has **no presence term and no proximity term** — it never asks whether the
person it names is anywhere. Both round-2 headline instruments decide findability from its answer.
`mainline-chain-floor.mjs` makes the assumption explicit at line 198: when a gate refuses on
standing, it **conjures the giver**

```js
try { H.spawnNPC({ from_record: giver, pos: [0, 0, 2] }); } catch (e) { /* already there */ }
```

with its own comment conceding *"which `questOpen` already assumes, since the gate has never had a
proximity term."*

This is round 1's finding moved one layer out, and it is worth naming precisely. Round 1: *"a
machine walking the graph is not a player finding it"* — the player could not learn the words.
Round 2 fixed that, properly and with a red control. **The player can now learn the words and has
nobody to say them to.** A rumour in Helstrom teaches you *"the upper shelf"*; Rootkeeper Jeen, who
wants it said, is in no state file in the build.

The instrument is sound because its control is: `--sabotage assume-present` reports presence for
all 94 without asking the world, and **`talkable` stays at 9** — because `talkTo()` is attempted for
all 94 by record id and only nine succeed. The number does not depend on my field matching.

Two smaller things sit inside this. Aveline Rell offers the topics **"the road to Blackrose"** and
**"the road to Gideon"**; neither Blackrose nor Gideon has a bootable state. And the rumour supply
is keyed to **nine settlements** of which **four** have a state and **three** have anybody in them:
**16 of the 35** subject quests have their keyword rumoured *only* in Gideon (9), Lilmoth (5) or
Blackrose (3), which no player can reach. `Q-MAG-18` ("the voriplasm") has **no supplier anywhere**,
one-hop or by closure.

**Remedy.** Place the givers. A quest is findable when the sentence that names it and the person who
answers to it are in the same reachable world. Then make the gate say so: give `QuestEngine.open()`
a presence term, so that a quest whose giver is nowhere reports `blocked: giver absent` instead of
`ok`, and every existing instrument inherits the truth for free.

**Acceptance.** `critic-giver-presence.mjs` reports `giver_talkable_in_some_state ≥ 60/94`, with
side ≥ 20/25 and every one of the 10 quests in the findability headline among them; and
`utility-findability.mjs` counts a quest as opened only after `H.talkTo(giver)` has succeeded.

## 6. The attribute rescale — right diagnosis, and two of three demands now clear every sheet

The diagnosis is correct and well evidenced: quest attribute demands were authored on Morrowind's
0–100 scale while `derive.js` grants +1 per multiple of 15 in a governing skill, so a demand of
40–45 is a 100-scale number on a 31-scale sheet. `--sheet max` now reports 0 mainline endings out of
reach. That is a real fix.

Two qualifications.

**The ceiling is read from paperwork.** `resolution-reachability.mjs` takes its ceilings from
`reports/faction-signature-sweep.json`, a cached report carrying **no `measured_at`**, rather than
from a live sheet. The numbers are right today; nothing makes them stay right.

**The rescale moved two of the three demands out of the discriminating range.** Against the p10
sheet's ceilings:

| ending | was | now | p10 ceiling | still discriminates? |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `Q-MAIN-23 res_turn_the_writ` | personality 45 | **20** | 18 | **yes** |
| `Q-MAIN-08 res_lean_on_cuiro` | personality 40 | **17** | 18 | no |
| `Q-MAIN-13 res_make_him_say_it` | willpower 35 | **20** | 22 | no |

A demand nobody can meet and a demand every sheet can meet are both failures. One of the three is a
choice; two are now inside the reach of the tenth-percentile character.

## 8. The prose carries the machine's fingerprint

The writing is good. The *numbers in it* are one number.

Across all round-2 dialogue — `55-utility.json` (102 infos), `50-mainline.json` (135), and the 55
rumour lines — **8,626 words**, measured against `corpus/40-dialogue/data/morrowind-dialogue.csv.gz`
(69,876 rows, **1,866,989 words**):

| | new prose, per 10k words | Morrowind, per 10k words | ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **"eleven"** | **35.94** (31 hits) | **0.21** (39 hits) | **171×** |
| "four hundred" | 15.07 | 0.01 | ~1500× |
| "has stopped" | 6.96 | 0.01 | ~700× |
| "that is not a…" | 3.48 | 0.04 | 87× |

Only **3** of the 31 are the proper noun *"the four hundred and eleven"*. The other **26** are
free-standing, in **20 distinct unrelated records**, in different towns, about different things:

> eleven gold a week · eleven years · eleven thousand names · eleven boxes · eleven sextons ·
> eleven names short every morning · eleven still drink · eleven families off the hiring list ·
> eleven entries in four hundred years

**"eleven" is the second most commonly used number-word in the new prose after "one"** — ahead of
"two" (29) and "three" (20). Independent facts about unrelated things, all landing on the same
number. This is the fingerprint the prose sweep is chasing, present at full strength in material
written this round.

The structure carries a second fingerprint. All **34** topics have **exactly 3 infos** (102 / 34 =
3.00, histogram `{3: 34}`), and **0 of 102 infos carries any condition at all** — no `speaker`, no
`race`, no `faction`, no `rank`, no `disposition`, no `requires`. Every person in the world says the
identical three lines in the identical order. `RI-DLG01`'s topic graph and Morrowind's whole
dialogue model are *filtered* infos; this uses none of it. The builder's own `50-mainline.json` does
filter, which shows the team knows how — the 34 fast-written bodies simply did not.
