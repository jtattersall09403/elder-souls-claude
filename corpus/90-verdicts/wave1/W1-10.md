these items are gates, not dimensions. Five hard fails across three items. AR-1 pass, AR-2 pass.

## The single biggest gap

**`GAP-W1-weapon-movesets-not-wired-to-the-runtime`** · `weapon.moveset.slots` · **blocking**

**What.** The 87 authored movesets are unreachable from the running game. `setLoadout()` rejects
all 87; eight of fifteen classes have no live representation; what a player can press is a
three-slot spine file, so every weapon in the game plays exactly four animation ids.
`CFS_live = 0.0000`, `distinct_anim = 4` against a floor of 18, M6 agreement 0.5357 with the clip
id matching on 0 of 14 rows.

**Why it matters.** Every weapon in the game is the same weapon: four attacks, and rolling,
backstepping, sprinting, jumping and guard-countering all play the standing light attack — which
is exactly the failure the user named when they asked for this piece.

**Remedy.** Make `MovesetLibrary` the runtime's move source instead of a harness-only reader.
(1) In `game/src/combat/system.js` `createPlayer()`, build the move table from
`game/data/combat/movesets/*.json` via `MovesetLibrary` rather than from
`game/data/combat/spine/*.json`. (2) In `game/src/combat/player.js`, dispatch the attack buttons
through `MovesetLibrary.resolveSlot(weaponId, button, ctx)` with `ctx` carrying `state`,
`state_frame`, `roll_tier`, `stance`, `offhand`, `descending` and `fall_height_m`, and **delete
the path that falls through to the standing light attack when no window matches** — a missed
window must produce nothing (`RI-WPN04` §B rule 2). (3) Add `player.anim_slot`,
`player.hitstop_f`, `player.weapon_tip` and a `block_success` event to the trace. (4) Restore the
seven spine classes' R2 active/recovery split to `game/data/combat/frames.json`. (5) Repair the 82
weapons whose `chains_to` points at a slot they do not declare.

**Acceptance.** `critic-w1-10a.mjs` reports `equipped_ok 87/87` and
`classes_with_zero_equippable []`; `critic-w1-10c.mjs` reports `CFS_live == 1.0000` with a
`contextual` band exactly on `RI-WPN04` §B's windows for all four equip-load tiers;
`critic-w1-10f.mjs` reports M6 agreement `1.0000` including the anim id; observed `distinct_anim`
per weapon ≥ 18. Estimated size **L**.

## Bar pressure

Full text in the verdict JSON's `notes`. In summary:

1. **Is a passing score the thing the user asked for?** Yes. Taken at random,
   `ssw_kothringi_bronze`'s deviation is a rising `r1.3` at 95° instead of a horizontal, which
   catches a rolling enemy the baseline whiffs — a difference a player would notice, and the
   item's own test. The bar is right; the build has not reached it.
2. **Which threshold does no work?** **`ARI`.** `SHARE(c) ≤ 4` forces `ARI ≥ 0.3185` on any
   conforming roster, so both the 0.26 pass bar and the 0.19 hard-fail bar are *unreachable from
   below*. `ARI` cannot fail. Amendment filed with replacement bands (pass 0.42, hard fail 0.32).
3. **Which threshold is gameable, and how cheaply?** **`TDV`.** Its divergence clause is satisfied
   by a different clip id plus a ≥ 25° arc delta, and arc is a number in a JSON file — a `sed`
   one-liner adding 26 to every `2h.*` arc moves any roster to 1.00. The declared 0.8333 already
   leans on 50 weapons whose `2h.r2` chains into a `2h.r2.follow` they do not declare. The
   companion check `TDV` needs is `RI-WPN03` M2's: confirm the 2h clip *track* differs on the rig,
   not the declared number.
4. **The tenth-hour version.** Nothing measures the *sequence*. Both fingerprints are built from
   `r1.1` and per-weapon scalars; the only dimension looking past the first hit is a single
   integer, and 12 of 15 classes share its value. Two weapons whose entire chain after hit one is
   identical are invisible to every number here.
5. **Is the roster the right size?** Yes — and unusually clearly. 1 133 clips, 0 forged pairs, 331
   distinct normalised shapes, both blind tests passed. The remedy is **not** "fewer weapons,
   better authored"; `RI-AI05` §13's delete-nine-archetypes precedent does not apply. It is "the
   same weapons, connected to the game."

**Amendment filed:** `corpus/12-weapons/AMENDMENT-W1-10-CRITIC-01.md` — replacement `ARI` bands,
and a new `RI-WPN03` §D.3 **chain rhythm distance** `Rh`, computable from the declared data with
no harness extension, whose within-class `Rh_min < 0.03` is a hard fail. It is the only proposed
measure here that would fail a build whose every other number passes.
