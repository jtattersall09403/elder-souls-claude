## 3. Claim 2 — the one-ulp bug, and the hunt for its siblings

The fix (`mag = 0.55 - 1e-9`) is real and it is load-bearing: the histogram shows 33,925 of 33,944
samples inside [1.99, 2.01] and **1** above 3.0 m/s. Without it, 12% of frames would jog.

I looked for siblings on the same pattern — a float comparison against a band edge that a `hypot()`
or a division can land one ulp the wrong side of.

- **Walk-band interior:** half stick (0.275) → **0.9973 m/s**, exactly half of 2.0. The band is
  linear in stick magnitude with no other boundary inside it.
- **Deadzone:** quarter stick (0.1375) → **0.0000 m/s**, state `IDLE`. Clean.
- **Jog:** 1.0 → **3.1913 m/s** on land, **3.1943** in water. One band edge, one behaviour.
- **Equip-load tier edges** (30 / 70 in `RI-CMB01`): swept 10 / 24 / 45 / 69 / 79 / 95 / 105%.
  Roll class steps LIGHT → LIGHT → MEDIUM → MEDIUM → HEAVY → HEAVY → OVERLOADED. Discrete, no
  interpolation. No ulp fragility.

**No sibling found in locomotion.** But the same *class* of error — a boundary that decides
behaviour and is never asserted — is present one layer up, and it is the gap in §7: nothing
anywhere asserts that a **road point is above the water surface**. `scale-audit.mjs` has a
`TIDEWAY-INVERSION` check that measures water depth on exactly **one** of the ten legs. The other
nine were never asked.

the builder's own number is a flood fill wearing a walk's clothes.

## 7. Claim 6 — road relief, and whether the rejected remedy was right

`M36-ROAD-RELIEF` measures **26.7%** of 500 m road windows changing ≥15 m against a ≥30% target,
with a fail floor of 12%. Reproduced exactly.

**The declaration is honest.** It is reported as a FAIL in the tool's own output, in
`reports/scale-audit.json`, and in the builder's status file, with the number and both thresholds.
Nothing was rounded, re-windowed or re-bounded to reach 30. That is the correct behaviour and it
should be said plainly.

**The rejected remedy was correctly rejected.** The builder tried lowering the A* slope penalty so
roads would climb more, found it *"did not move it and broke the settlement pads (75 deg carve
cliffs)"*. My own measurements corroborate both halves. The relief is not missing because the roads
are lazy — the terrain they cross is genuinely flat: area-weighted mean slope is 8.63°, and the
regions the crossing runs through are the flattest in the province (Deep Marshes 2.92°, Clay Moor
3.11°, Crimson Coast 4.17°). Meanwhile the province has real vertical range where it should:
−41.8 to +424.2 m, Valus at 424.2, Salt Hills at 265.3, 19.4% of land above +100 m. Making marsh
trails climb 15 m per 500 m would have produced switchbacks in a swamp — a worse world scoring a
better number. **The right call, correctly declared rather than bent.**

The remedy this actually wants is *routing*, not *carving*: run more of the trunk network through
the regions that already have relief. That is a wave-2 job and it is not the biggest gap.

## 8. The gap: THE CROSSING WALKS 502 m ALONG THE FLOOR OF A 65-METRE LAKE

This is the thing nobody stated, and it sits on the number the whole corpus is denominated in.

I sampled `getWaterAt()` at every point of all ten built road legs, at both tide phases, through
the harness — the same instrument and the same tide state (`LOW`) that `crossing.mjs` uses.

```
node <critic probe>  ->  artifacts/W1-01/critic-road-water-harness.json

TIDE LOW : road 25,221 m; over-knee 1,364 m (5.4%); over-chest 574 m (2.3%)
   stormhold-helstrom   max  65.64 m   W3+ 538 m   W5 502 m     <-- leg 1 of THE CROSSING
   helstrom-gideon      max   6.93 m   W3+  84 m   W5  72 m
   lilmoth-archon       max   0.85 m   W3+ 742 m   W5   0 m     <-- the declared tideway, correct
   (the other seven legs: max depth 0.000 m)
```

At `(2182.52, 2214.63)`, inside The Stone Forest, on the trunk road:

```
ground_y 69.66 m      water surface_y 135.30 m      depth 65.640 m      band W5
player state IDLE     hp 620/620     stamina 120/120     walk speed 2.0 m/s
```

`artifacts/W1-01/shot-drowned-road-stormhold-helstrom.png` (1280×720, third-person orbital,
yaw 90°, pitch −5°, arm 4.0 m, 12:00, tide LOW) is a **uniformly black-green frame**. That is what
the player sees standing on the Rootway: sixty-five metres of water volume.

**502 m is 7.4% of the 6,788 m crossing — about 4.2 minutes of the "hour".**

This is not a water-system complaint, which would belong to W1-03. It is a **road-routing and
terrain-form defect**, and both paths are W1-01's own. Three consequences, all measured:

1. **The 56.574-minute figure is conditional on water having no effect.** When `RI-WLD10`'s bands
   land, W5 is *swim*, and 502 m at the declared swim speed of 1.1 m/s is 7.6 minutes underwater
   against a 40–60 s breath clock. The canonical crossing stops being walkable and starts being
   lethal — so the traversal minute that `RI-WLD02`, `RI-WLD03` and every density figure in the
   corpus is denominated in gets re-derived, in wave 2, from a route that no longer exists.
2. **`M3-SPEED-HONESTY` passes for the wrong reason.** 0.012% of samples fall below 1.6 m/s
   *because water is inert*. The check was built to catch an hour manufactured out of friction; it
   cannot see an hour manufactured out of a road that should not be traversable at all.
3. **Nothing in the instrument suite asks the question.** `scale-audit.mjs` measures water on the
   one leg RI-WLD01 flags as tidal and on no other. The defect is precisely where the instrument
   is not looking — the same shape as the one-ulp bug the builder itself found, one layer up.

The proximate cause is visible in the builder's own note: *"a water cell ≥90 m from land is ocean;
anything narrower is an incision in the local surface. Without this every river became a 300 m
gorge through Valus Ridge."* The incision rule put a river surface at 135.3 m over a valley floor
at 69.7 m — a 65 m gorge that filled — and `build-roads.mjs` then routed the Rootway along its bed
because A* costs slope, not depth.

**Remedy** — concrete, in the piece's own files:

- Add depth to the A* cost in `tools/world/build-roads.mjs`: make any cell whose water depth
  exceeds **0.60 m (W2)** impassable to trunk routing, except on the leg declared `tideway` in
  `RI-WLD01` §4, and re-run the build chain `build-terrain → build-water-hazards → build-roads`.
- Where a leg genuinely must cross a channel, emit a **bridge or causeway deck** into
  `game/data/world/roads.json` as a raised polyline with its own surface height, so the road is
  *over* the water rather than *under* it.
- Add the check that does not exist to `tools/world/scale-audit.mjs`, as `M2-ROAD-ABOVE-WATER`,
  covering **all ten legs at all four tide phases** — not one leg at one phase.

**Acceptance:** `scale-audit.mjs` reports 0 m of trunk road above W2 on all legs except
`lilmoth-archon`, at RISING/HIGH/FALLING/LOW; `lilmoth-archon` keeps its declared ≤0.85 m at LOW
and ≥1.40 m at HIGH; and `crossing.mjs` re-measures the crossing inside **52–65 min / 6,300–7,600 m**
on the re-routed network.

## 11. Corpus defects filed

   forced to choose between schema-valid and doctrine-valid. Carried in `justification` strings and
   in §10 above as a workaround. Reason: `method_gap`.
2. **`RI-WLD01` has no clause requiring a road to be above water.** Its "How we lose" list catches
   friction-cheating, sinuosity-forgetting and ocean bloat, and cannot see §8. Proposed check
   `M2b — ROAD ABOVE WATER`. Reason: `corpus_hole`.

   different words in thirteen table rows, which is why a build with `audioMB: 0` scores 78/78 on
   the audio axis. Proposed: each axis names its source, and ≥5 of 9 must be measured off built
   geometry or rendered pixels. Reason: `sharper_discriminator`.
4. **`RI-WLD04` M17 has no anti-ordering clause.** "Shuffle the 39 images" has no recorded seed and
   no assertion, so a pack can ship sorted and score 39/39. Proposed: `ANSWERS.json` must carry a
   `shuffle_seed`, and the critic must assert the frame order is not a permutation of the region
   order before reporting any M17 number. Reason: `method_gap`.

**Arbitration question raised, not answered.** AR-2 **B13** fires on any piece built before W1-05
ships the transport network, which is all twenty-eight wave-1 pieces. B13's fail condition is
absence, and absence is a property of the *build*, not of this piece. Souls side: the check must
stay two-directional or the S7 inversion returns. Morrowind side: a per-piece gate that fails
pieces for another piece's unbuilt work destroys the signal it was meant to carry. Proposed
resolution for the dimension owner: B13 becomes a **wave gate**, run once per wave by the coherence
agent, rather than a per-piece AR-2 probe. I have not applied it; I have recorded B13 as failing.
