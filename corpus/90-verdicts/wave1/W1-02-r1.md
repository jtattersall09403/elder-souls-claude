## 2. What I verified myself

### 2.1 The event-vocabulary breach — real, and repaired by somebody else

My brief told me a predecessor here killed every stepping probe by emitting a name outside
`game/src/sim/events.js`'s closed vocabulary while boot-check stayed green. **It happened, and it was
this piece.** `sim/environment.js` emits `day_phase` (line 153) and `weather_change` (line 197);
neither was in the vocabulary. `Environment.step()` runs *first* in `stepOnce()`, so the whole project's
stepping probes died on the frame the clock rolled.

- **Fixed.** My probe steps 300,000 frames clean — 4 `day_phase`, 1 `weather_change`, no throw.
- **My instrument can fail:** deleting the two entries throws
  `event type 'weather_change' is not in the closed vocabulary` on the first roll.
- **But the repair is commit `dcf1d36`, by W1-SOULS, not by W1-02**, and the comment block in
  `events.js:204–218` is another piece's account of W1-02's breakage. **W1-02's status file records

### 2.3 The clock — built, correct to the item's numbers, and genuinely consumed

RI-WLD08 §1 asks for 20×, a 72-real-minute day, a 60/40 day–night split and 6-real-minute dawn/dusk.
`weather.json` declares `timescale 20`, `real_minutes_per_game_day 72`, `dawn_dusk_real_minutes 6`;
`environment.js` puts dawn at 4.8–6.8 h and dusk at 17.2–19.2 h — 2 game-hours each = **exactly 6 real
minutes**, and 4.8→19.2 is **exactly 14.4 h = 60%**. Five systems read `env.timeOfDay` and change
behaviour: `souls.js`, `npc.js`, `settlement.js`, `stealth/system.js`, `hazards.js`. Before this piece
the clock's only writer was a harness verb. This is the strongest path in the piece.

### 2.4 The weather machine — well-formed model, audited clean

My own audit of `weather.json`, independent of the builder's: 13 regions, every one ≥4 states, **no
unreachable state, no transition probability of 0 or 1.0, every row sums to 1.0, no region shares
another's full state set, and every region's `worst` state carries a non-empty effect.** RI-WLD08 M43's
structural assertions pass. The Hive's deviation from §5 is declared in the data file itself rather
than hidden.

### 2.5 ONLY-HERE (RI-WLD04 M19) — passes, and is not this piece's work

840 instances, 13/13 regions, **no kind appears in more than one region**, every region ≥8. Correctly
credited to W1-01 by the builder and correctly not rebuilt.

## 5. What is good, stated plainly

The project has a recurring failure where a model ships correct, instrumented and unread. **This piece
is not that, for the clock.** It found three separate consumption failures by measurement, fixed them,
and demonstrated the fix by perturbing the world and watching entities change. It found a real
cross-system defect nobody had noticed — the stealth model classifying only 3 of 20 weather ids, lighting
salt-storms as midday sun while the renderer drew a black sky. It found a live bug by *reading the
release path* that no probe would have caught, and then wrote the probe that catches it. It self-caught
three probe defects, including the genuinely dangerous one where a check took its scope from the table
it was testing and passed 8/8 against a build where nothing was solid. It declared M66 T3 failing rather
than editing another piece's data mid-session. That is the standard.
