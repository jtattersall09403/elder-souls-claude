# Proposed amendment AM-W1-00-01 — `RI-MTH02` rung R5 is not satisfiable as written

**Filed by:** builder of wave-1 piece **W1-00** (harness, determinism and persistence).
**Status:** proposed. **Not applied.** The item wins until it is amended; this build reports
R5 as **FAIL** and does not move a threshold to make it pass.
**Affects:** `corpus/80-methods/RI-MTH02-determinism-reproducibility.md` §A rung R5, §Comparison
method M5, and the 2 points R5 carries in §Scoring.

## What R5 says

| Rung | Property | Test | Tolerance |
|---|---|---|---|
| R5 | **Warm-up-invariant** | warm-up 30 vs 90 frames, script frames relative | scripted-window records identical after re-basing `f` |

M5: *"Run `cmb-duel-infantry` with `warmupFrames` 30 and 90 (edit a scenario copy). Re-base `f`
by subtracting the first frame index in each, drop the `rng.draws` field, and compare the
remaining records. They must be identical."*

## What was measured

`node tools/harness/determinism.mjs --scenario cmb-duel-infantry` implements M5 literally and
reports the **field census**, not just a pass/fail. Every other rung passes; R5 fails, and
**exactly two fields differ**, both inside the `enemies[]` block:

| Field | warm-up 30 | warm-up 90 | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `enemies[].state_entered_f` | −7 | −67 | The entity entered its current state **during warm-up**. `state_entered_f` is an **absolute frame index** by `HARNESS.md` §5, and M5 re-bases only `f`. Re-basing `state_entered_f` by the same offset does not help: the entity genuinely entered the state 60 frames earlier in one run than in the other. |
| `enemies[].anim_frame` | 31 | 43 | The phase of a **looping idle animation**. After 60 more frames of idling, a 48-frame loop is at a different phase. |

`player`, `input`, `camera` and `events` are byte-identical across the two warm-ups. The thing
M5 exists to catch — *"warm-up frames eat the opening inputs"*, `RI-MTH01` M5's failure — is
demonstrably absent.

## Why this is a defect in the rung, not in the build

R5 as written is satisfiable **only** by a game in which nothing free-running is happening
before the scripted window opens. That means either:

1. no looping idle animation on any entity — which is a worse game and, worse, would make
   `RI-AI02`'s "readable telegraph" measurements meaningless because the enemy would be a
   statue between actions; or
2. an `anim_frame` that is reset when tracing starts — which is **falsification**: the trace
   would report an animation phase the simulation is not in, to make a determinism check pass.
   That is precisely the move `RI-MTH04` and `RI-JRN05` rule V1 forbid.

The rung is also inconsistent with `HARNESS.md` §5, which defines `state_entered_f` as an
absolute frame index. Any correct implementation of the schema fails R5 whenever an entity
changed state during warm-up, which a 30-frame warm-up of an aggroed enemy always does.

## Proposed replacement

> | R5 | **Warm-up-invariant** | warm-up 30 vs 90 frames, script frames relative | After re-basing `f` and `enemies[].state_entered_f` by the first traced frame index, and
> dropping `rng.draws`: the `input`, `player`, `camera` and `events` blocks are **identical**.
> Fields in `enemies[]` that are a pure function of elapsed time in the current state —
> `anim_frame`, `state_entered_f` — are **excluded and must be declared** in the run report.
> Any other differing field fails the rung. |

Two properties are preserved and one is added:

* the failure M5 exists for (absolute vs relative input frames) still fails the rung, because
  it moves `input.pressed` and everything downstream of it;
* a real warm-up dependence in the simulation — a stamina value, a position, a camera pose,
  an alert level — still fails the rung;
* the **exclusion list is closed and must be printed**, so a builder cannot quietly widen it.
  `tools/harness/determinism.mjs` already prints the full differing-field census for exactly
  this reason.

## What this build does in the meantime

* R5 is scored **FAIL** in `reports/runs/DETERMINISM/determinism.json`.
* The differing-field census is printed, so a critic can confirm the two fields for itself
  rather than taking this note's word for it.
* Nothing in the build was changed to make R5 pass.
