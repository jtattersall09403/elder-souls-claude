# W1-30A — the frame pipeline: antialiasing, bloom, occlusion, grade

Plan-State: satisfied
Parent: [W1-30](W1-30.md) · Contract: [Builder execution contract](BUILDER-EXECUTION-CONTRACT.md)
Evidence: [W1-30-EVIDENCE.md](W1-30-EVIDENCE.md) · Reuse: [W1-30-LIBRARY.md](W1-30-LIBRARY.md)

**The highest ratio of visible improvement to risk in the whole tree.** Nothing here changes a single
model, texture or piece of world content: it changes how the frame is resolved and graded. That means
it improves *every* shot at once and cannot collide with anyone.

Dispatch the moment W1-30S lands.

## Owned paths (exclusive write)

`game/src/render/renderer.js`, `game/src/render/post/**`, `game/vendor/three/addons/**` (additive
vendoring of same-version r180 official addons, with licences).

Read-only: `render/lighting.js` (B's, consumed for fog/aerial/exposure params).

## Where we are, precisely

| thing | today | why it fails a person |
|---|---|---|
| antialiasing | **none.** `renderer.js:64` sets `antialias:true` on the context, but the world renders into `worldTarget` (`renderer.js:253`) whose `samples` is never set. Context MSAA applies only to the default framebuffer. | jagged, crawling silhouettes in motion — the most reliable amateur tell there is |
| the AA substitute | a depth-edge detector blending 4 neighbours at `edge*0.38` | blind to colour edges at constant depth, blind to alpha-test edges, saturates against sky and smears |
| bloom | 4 taps at 2 px, threshold 0.72, added at 0.075 | emissives do not read as light sources |
| ambient occlusion | 4 depth taps at 3 px, clamped to 0.12 max | invisible; nothing looks grounded |
| grade | saturation ×1.035, contrast ×1.015, vignette 0.075 | no tonal identity at all; Black Marsh looks like anywhere |
| output | 8-bit sRGB, no dither | sky and fog gradients band |

## The work

1. **MSAA, properly.** Set `samples` on `worldTarget` (WebGL2; 4× floor, 8× where the device
   reports it) and keep the resolve inside the HDR chain. This is a one-line fix for the single
   largest defect in the frame and should ship first, alone, so its effect is measurable in isolation.
2. **SMAA on top**, vendored from r180 addons, for the edges MSAA misses (alpha-test foliage,
   specular aliasing). TAA is explicitly **out of scope for this child** — it needs history
   invalidation for cuts, teleports, resize, cell changes and animated foliage, and getting that
   wrong produces ghosting that looks worse than aliasing. Named as the alternative, deliberately not
   taken; revisit only if SMAA+MSAA measurably fails the motion gate.
3. **A real bloom chain.** Multi-resolution downsample/upsample (5 mips), soft knee threshold tied to
   the exposure target from `lighting.js`, energy-conserving upsample. Emissive windows, hearths,
   sapwells and spells must read as light sources without the frame going milky.
4. **Real ambient occlusion.** GTAO (preferred) or SSAO from the r180 addons, applied before
   transparencies, on a half-resolution depth+normal prepass with a bilateral upsample. This needs a
   normal buffer, which needs a depth-normal prepass — budget it explicitly.
5. **A grade that is a grade.** A per-region, per-weather, per-time-of-day tone curve and colour
   transform, driven by `lighting.js`'s parameters, applied after ACES: lift/gamma/gain plus a
   channel-mixing matrix, with named recipes registered in `lighting-recipes.js` (B's registry — A
   consumes, does not own). Blackwood at dusk and Salt Hills at noon must not share a grade.
6. **Dither before output.** Ordered or blue-noise, ±0.5 LSB, applied after the colour-space
   conversion. One line; removes visible banding in every sky.
7. **A quality ladder.** `high` / `medium` / `low` presets that turn passes off in a defined order,
   with an observable direct-render fallback for devices without WebGL2 or float targets. Every pass
   keeps its `setVisualFeature` sabotage switch.

## The bar

| gate | acceptance, with units | null control |
|---|---|---|
| **edge quality, in motion** | On the 12 Deck motion sequences, the count of pixels whose luma changes by > 24/255 between consecutive frames **while the camera and subject are both static in world space** drops by ≥ 60% against the pre-A commit. This measures crawl, which is what aliasing actually costs. | disable MSAA + SMAA: the figure must return to within 10% of the pre-A baseline |
| **edge quality, still** | Silhouette gradient width against sky, averaged over 48 Deck vistas, ≥ 1.8 px (from ~1.0 px hard-edged today) | disable both AA paths; must return to ≤ 1.2 px |
| **bloom reads** | On the 4 interior and 2 night Deck shots, ≥ 3 distinct emissive sources per frame show a measurable halo extending ≥ 12 px at half-intensity | threshold to 10.0 (nothing passes); halo count must go to 0 |
| **occlusion reads** | Mean luminance in a 0.5 m contact band around ground-meeting geometry is ≥ 12% below the surrounding surface, over ≥ 200 sampled contacts | disable AO; delta must fall below 3% |
| **grade separates places** | For all 78 region×time×weather still combinations, the mean-hue + mean-value pair is distinguishable between any two regions at the same time/weather by ≥ the corpus-defined swatch distance | swap every region to one grade recipe; the distinguishability must collapse |
| **no banding** | On the 26 sky-dominant Deck shots, the longest run of identical 8-bit values along a vertical gradient scan is ≤ 6 px | disable dither; must exceed 20 px |
| **budget** | Frame time on the RunPod T4 at 1920×1080 ≤ 16.6 ms at `high` in the worst Deck vista; ≤ 8 ms at `medium` | — |
| **human read** | Deck primary judgement (`W1-30-EVIDENCE.md` §2): Craft ≥ pre-A + 1.5 on the **minimum** over shots | — |
| determinism | same seed → identical frame hash, all 288 stills | change seed; hashes must change |

**Hard fails:** any pass that cannot be switched off; a pass whose off-switch does not change pixels;
double tone-mapping; the UI pass entering bloom or being colour-converted twice; a `high` preset that
exceeds the frame budget; regression in the M1–M12 tripwire battery.

## Reuse rows

**Consumed:** `lighting.js` frame params (B), `lighting-recipes.js` grade recipes (B), the existing
`setVisualFeature` sabotage surface, `worldTarget`'s HDR-linear discipline. **Published:**
`render/post/*` passes as independently switchable modules, each consumed by both the world composite
and (for grade) the Deck's reference-plate renderer; the quality ladder consumed by the settings
screen (W1-21) and the capture harness (V). **Variants:** grade recipes are variant specs over a base
curve, one per region×weather — never 78 separate transforms.

## Dependencies and stop conditions

Depends on **W1-30S** only. Consumes B's `lighting.js` but does not block on B: until B ships, A
reads the current values through the shim S creates.

Stop before shipping GTAO if the depth-normal prepass pushes the worst Deck vista past 16.6 ms on the
T4 — fall back to a half-res SSAO with bilateral upsample and record the substitution. Stop the grade
work if `lighting.js` cannot supply region/weather identity, and file that back to B rather than
reading `sky.js` internals.

## Falsification audit

The strongest attack: **A can make every metric better and the game still look bad**, because good
light on capsule characters and box houses is still capsule characters and box houses. That is real
and it is why the Deck's minimum-over-shots includes a mandatory character close-up and a mandatory
street shot. A should expect its human read to be capped by D and E, and should not chase the cap.

The second: a bloom chain and a vignette can flatter a bad frame into a better *score* without a
better *game* — the classic Instagram-filter pass. The guard is the census sample (12 unseen shots
per run) and the requirement that Craft improve on the **minimum**, not the mean.

Least certain: whether 4× MSAA plus SMAA plus GTAO plus a 5-mip bloom fits 16.6 ms on a T4 at 1080p
in a scene with instanced vegetation. It probably does; it has not been measured. The first builder
action is to measure it, and the quality ladder exists so the answer "no" is a configuration, not a
crisis.
