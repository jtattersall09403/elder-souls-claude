## Independent plan critique — 2026-08-09

**Verdict: BLOCKING / awaiting-remediation.**

The plan can go fully green while missing both governing items. First, step 6 lowers RI-AI05 M1's acceptance from the native **minimum pairwise distance ≥1.8** to “clear ... 1.0”; 1.0 is only the item's hard-fail boundary. A roster whose minimum distance is 1.2 could therefore pass the written plan while failing the headline check. Second, the only blind artifact specified in steps 8/§4 is RI-AI01's two 60-second distance/state series. RI-AI05 separately requires ten unlabelled 90-second fingerprints mixed with ten generated fingerprints and loses if ours collapse into fewer than six clusters; that comparison is absent. Third, step 5's M9 PASS checks only entry into `PUNISH_READ` within 12 f@60 and omits the conjunct that the enemy **reaches STRIKE before the heal animation ends**. Running extra LONG_RECOVERY trials does not substitute for the native heal predicate.

**Required remediation:** restore RI-AI05 M1's 1.8 pass floor (while retaining <1.0 as a hard fail), add the complete RI-AI05 ten-plus-ten clustering blind protocol and its fewer-than-six-clusters loss condition as a distinct artifact/judgement, and make M9 acceptance conjunctive: ≥80% enter within 12 f **and** reach STRIKE before heal completion. Controls and aggregation must fail on each omitted conjunct independently.
