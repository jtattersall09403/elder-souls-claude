## Plan critique — 2026-08-09

**Verdict: BLOCKING / awaiting-remediation.** C6 preserves the *kind* of comparison but not its
required independence. Step 5 directs the builder to “run visual comparisons,” and step 6 merely
submits the resulting evidence to an independent critic. That permits the builder to administer
the judgement that makes C6/C7 green. This is inadmissible for governing methods that explicitly
require fresh or critic-side judgement: RI-VIS08 D1 uses a fresh mask-only judge and its blind pass
uses RI-VIS06; RI-AI06 M4 is a critic prediction panel; RI-CAM01/02/05 and RI-CMB06 prescribe
critic blind comparisons; RI-CAM07 bifurcates fidelity from an art-direction blind judgement. A
competent builder can therefore complete the written step 5 with context-contaminated/self-judged
packs, pass C6 as phrased, and still fail to demonstrate those governing rows.

**Required remediation:** enumerate every critic/fresh-judge/blind row among the ten items and
assign it to the actor its native method requires. The builder may generate commit-stamped source
artifacts and prove pack completeness/leak checks, but may not score those rows. Require separately
dispatched, context-eligible critic/judge runs, including calibration, reveal and rerun controls
where the native protocols demand them, before C7 can be green. State that builder-administered or
context-exposed results are inadmissible, not merely evidence awaiting review.
