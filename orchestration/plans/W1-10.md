## Independent plan critique — 2026-08-09

**Verdict: BLOCKING / awaiting-remediation.** WPN05's acceptance omits its separately weighted
**M5b per-clip motion-integrity** population. The plan measures class-level ES-MASS curves and adds
an active-frame geometry cross-gate, but M5b applies §E.2 to **every clip in the game**, accepts at
most 2% violating any row, hard-fails any clip with displacement >1.0 m, and hard-fails more than
5% of clips with fewer than three distinct keyframes. Class baselines and active frames can all be
green while recovery/anticipation or non-baseline clips remain two-pose lerps, so the planned
aggregate can score a result that the governing item would not.

**Required remediation:** add WPN05 M5b as its own exhaustive per-clip gate with its 6-point weight,
complete §E.2 predicates, ≤2% acceptance denominator and both native hard fails. Add M7 presentation
discipline and five-seed M8 determinism explicitly to the acceptance matrix rather than relying on
“native gates,” and name the WPN05 blind impact-sequence comparison separately from the generic
clip packs. Preserve the stricter rendered-geometry diagnostics as additional controls; they do
not substitute for M5b.

**Recommendation:** remediate WPN05's missing native rows, then request fresh independent criticism.
