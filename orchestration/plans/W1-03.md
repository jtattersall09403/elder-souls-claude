## Plan critique — 2026-08-09

**Recorded blocker (resolved in this remediation; fresh review required):** W2 and the hard-fail list weaken RI-WLD10's native
automatic fail. The item distinguishes M54's observed-fit tolerance (`> 0.02 m` fails the fitted
amplitude probe) from the governing rule: *any tidal amplitude anywhere outside the five tidal
regions* is an automatic fail. The plan converts that rule into “non-tidal amplitude never exceeds
0.02 m” and lists only amplitude “above 0.02 m” as a hard fail. A build can therefore deliberately
author and consume a 0.01–0.02 m global tide in all eight non-tidal regions, pass W2 exactly as
written and pass the M54 tolerance, yet violate the item-native automatic fail and the exact-five
population predicate.

**Remediation applied:** W2, the hard-fail list, the controls, and build ordering now separate declaration/consumed behaviour from M54 measurement tolerance. Require the
tidal-region set to equal exactly the five named regions; require every other region's authored
tide amplitude and tide-driven world-field/mesh/body response to be exactly zero; retain
`> 0.02 m` only as M54's observational fit-failure threshold, not as permission for a small
non-tidal tide. Add a red seed with a consumed 0.01 m tide in one non-tidal region and require the
hard-fail/exact-set check to fail even though the fitted-amplitude tolerance arm would not.
