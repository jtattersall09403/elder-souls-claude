## Hard-fail/control policy and decisive test

Any governing hard fail/lose condition, missing actual audio, stale PCM sidecar, nondeterminism at fixed
seed, inert positive arm, no-op mute passing, listener arm identical to no-listener, duplicated blind trial
counted as independent, opened reveal, or unmeasurable required method is a hard stop. **A §QUAL that
was not run, or that came out inert, is also a hard stop for closure** — the identity rows may all be
green and the piece still does not close, which is the whole point of adding it. Delete the relevant
fix on a copy: emitter scheduling must return to bit-identical +40 dB arms; gain scaling must return to
non-scalar behavior; ToD removal must return day/night equality; bed deletion/shared L1 must fail B4;
event deletion must fail onset and blind signature checks. Restore and rerun the combined suite.

Dependencies: W1-02 supplies region identity/area and other M18 axes; W1-04 supplies settlements/interiors;
W1-01 supplies weather/world; W1-11 supplies impact headroom; W1-05 supplies wayfinding. Cite their
current-HEAD evidence or mark the governing aggregation red—not waived.

**Decisive test:** A builder cannot get this plan green and fail a governing bar because green requires

hard-fail/lose clauses, actual-audio blind methods and live consumption controls. No proxy is allowed to
