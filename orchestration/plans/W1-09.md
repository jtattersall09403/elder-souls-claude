### The 55-weight denominator: I read `RI-CMB07`, and the audit's arithmetic is not right

`orchestration/audits/PLAN-AUDIT-2026-08-14.md` §3 reads this plan as forbidding a `not_run` row
from being renormalised away and then renormalising the quality read away. Having read the item
rather than the plan's transcription of it, that is **not what happens**, and saying so plainly
matters more than accepting a convenient finding:

- The 55 runnable weight is **native `RI-CMB07` text**, not a plan invention. Its scoring section
  says outright: *"Bands apply to the renormalised score while M1 is `corpus_debt` (§0.1) and
  M2-LIVE is a seam debt … **Runnable weight in wave 1 is therefore 55** — M0 15, M2-CTRL 25, M3 10,
  M4 5"*, and §0.1 fixes the same rule for M1. The plan is transcribing the bar faithfully.
- The two rows removed from the denominator are **M1 Mode-A replay** (a determinism/replay check
  against an `INVALIDATED` corpus exemplar) and **M2-LIVE** (a `W1-09`↔`W1-12` seam debt). **Neither
  is a human quality read.** The renormalisation removes an instrument row and a dependency row.
- The blind pair carries **zero scoring weight in `RI-CMB07` in either direction** — it is not one
  of the six weighted checks. So it cannot be, and was not, "renormalised away". It is separately
  `not_possible` under the item's own wave-1 amendment because the exemplar is `INVALIDATED` and
  synthesising a counterpart from the item's published tables is explicitly `VOID`.
- And the renormalisation is not generous: 55 runnable means M0 15 + M2-CTRL 25 + M3 10 + M4 5, so
  the item's ≥90 band needs ≈50 of 55. Nobody should "repair" this by widening it.

**One thing the plan genuinely does need to say, and now does.** Build-order step 3's blanket ban —
*"a cross-piece dependency may make a row `not_run`; it may not turn it green, be renormalised away,
or permit an aggregate that natively consumes that row to pass"* — is right for every row **except**
the two `RI-CMB07` rows the item itself declares debt-renormalised. That exception is the item's, is
narrow, and must not spread: it applies to `RI-CMB07` M1 and M2-LIVE only, both are reported as debt
with their reason, **raw and runnable scores are reported on the same line** as §0.1 requires, and
the W1-09 ≥7.0/10 aggregate is reported **both ways**. Any other `not_run` row still fails closed.
