## Where we are, precisely

- Terrain uses vertex colours on a mud family (`province.js:347`: `worldMaterial('mud',
  {vertexColors:true, roughness:.97, bumpScale:.08, lod:'far'})`). One family, one tint, no blending
  between materials by slope, height or region.
- LOD bands exist (`province.js:343`) with near/far geometry overlap and one-tile hysteresis. Good
  structure; there is no **fade or dither** across the band, so the swap pops.
- Vegetation is instanced with per-region scale/lean parameters from `world-art.js`, and there is
  genuine thought in `province.js:195` about crowns having "a core, broken perimeter and holes
  between lobes". But **foliage is not alpha-tested** — `visual-foundation.js:159` defaults
  `alphaTest: 0` and no consumer overrides it — so crowns are opaque geometry rather than cut-out
  layers.
- No wind. No detail normals on the ground. No scattered rocks, roots, litter or decals to break the
  ground plane. No contact transitions where a rock meets soil.
- `world-art.js` gives each region one terrain word, three flora scalars, a lean and a depth phrase.
  **That is a vocabulary, not a topology.** It cannot express "this valley has a different landform
  from that ridge", which is precisely the richness the owner is asking about.

### What the frames show, ahead of the code reading

The VISUAL-TRUTH-AUDIT's captures (`docs/shots/2026-08-14-visual-truth/`) add three defects that the
code reading above understates:

- **Tree canopies render with no trunk.** Discs float in the sky at the top of the frame with nothing
  holding them up. Whether this is a culling bug, an LOD band that drops the trunk, or a placement
  error, it is the most immediately broken-looking thing in the whole capture set and it is item 0.
- **The terrain is visibly a grid.** A flat green plane with ~1 m quad seams and hard tile edges,
  running to the horizon. No relief, no scatter, no colour break. This is the picture of directive
  §6's "samey landscape".
- **Roads and boardwalks sit *on* the ground, not *in* it.** Flat hard-edged quads with no blend, no
  contact darkening and visible overlap between planks. The blend and contact work in items 1 and 2
  must explicitly cover road and path surfaces, not only natural ground.

## The work

**0. Fix the floating canopies** before anything else. It is a bug, it is the first thing a player
sees, and it makes every other improvement in this child look pointless.

1. **Multi-layer terrain blending.** Four material layers per region (e.g. mud / reed-mat / rock /
   salt-crust), blended by height, slope, curvature and an authored region mask, with C's detail
   normals at close range and a macro colour/roughness breakup at distance. Height-blend rather than
   linear-blend so transitions interlock instead of dissolving.
2. **Break the ground plane.** Deterministic scatter of rocks, roots, litter, fallen trunks, puddles
   and decals, densities per region, with contact darkening where they meet ground and small
   micro-relief so the terrain silhouette is not a smooth mathematical surface.
3. **Alpha-cut foliage.** Consume C's alpha foliage path: mipmapped, alpha-coverage-preserving
   atlases, `alphaTest` set, layered canopies with light passing between leaves. This is the single
   change that turns "green lumps" into "trees".
4. **Vegetation LOD with fade.** Near geometry → simplified mid mesh/card → far cluster → cull, with
   dither or cross-fade across each band and hysteresis, so M11 sees no pop ring.
5. **Wind.** Stable seeded shader wind, driven from simulation frame, with per-instance phase, scaled
   by species stiffness. Reeds move a lot, trunks barely. Static vegetation in a marsh reads as dead.
6. **Region character, made real.** Each region gets a landform profile — not just a colour — driving
   the height field: hummocks, kiln shelves, tidal ridges, drowned hollows, basalt steps, salt tusks.
   Plus a **traversal-scale variety rule**: over any 5-minute walk the player must cross at least
   three distinguishable terrain situations. This is the anti-dullness mechanism and it is measurable.
7. **Consume the new region corpus — do not write a second one.** This plan originally scoped
   authoring new corpus items for region topology and landform. **While it was being written, another
   agent landed exactly that**: `corpus/50-world/RI-WLD15-within-region-variety.md` (the six-minute
   walk and the patch-size ceiling), `corpus/50-world/RI-WLD16-landform-and-geology.md` (the terrain
   grammar each region is built from), `corpus/50-world/landforms.json`,
   `corpus/50-world/data/morrowind-region-census.json`, and the methods
   `corpus/80-methods/m-wld15-monotony.mjs` and `m-wld16-landform-census.mjs`. They are better than
   what this plan would have produced and they are the bar now.

   **F's job is to make the game satisfy them**, and to run `m-wld15-monotony.mjs` and
   `m-wld16-landform-census.mjs` as gates. If F finds a genuine gap in those items it reports it to
   their owner and escalates through `ARBITRATION.md`; it does not author a parallel set. This is
   rule 10 applied to the corpus — two implementations of one bar is how this project ends up with a
   good measurement and a broken one at the same time.

## The bar

| gate | acceptance, with units | null control |
|---|---|---|
| **no floating canopies** | Across the 26 region shots and all 12 motion sequences, zero vegetation crowns render without their trunk; checked geometrically (every crown instance has a visible supporting instance within its footprint) and confirmed in frames | cull trunks on a copy; the check must go red |
| **no visible grid** | On the 13 eye-level shots, no periodic luminance structure at the terrain quad frequency exceeds 4/255 amplitude in a Fourier scan of the ground region | restore the flat single-family ground; the amplitude must exceed the threshold |
| **paths sit in the ground** | Every road, boardwalk and path surface shows a blended edge ≥ 0.3 m wide and contact darkening where it meets terrain; zero hard rectangular seams against terrain | disable path blending; hard seams must return |
| **terrain has layers** | On all 13 region eye-level shots, ≥ 3 distinguishable material layers are present and correlate with slope/height as declared (correlation ≥ 0.5) | force one layer; the count and correlation must collapse |
| **the ground is broken** | ≥ 40 scattered elements per 100 m² in vegetated regions, ≥ 8 in sparse ones; every one shows contact darkening | disable scatter; both must go to zero and the human read must fall |
| **foliage is cut, not solid** | ≥ 60% of canopy silhouette pixels show alpha-cut edges (sky visible through the crown within 2 px of an opaque leaf) | set `alphaTest: 0`; the figure must collapse |
| **no LOD pop** | Across a 300-frame forward walk into and out of each LOD band, no frame shows more than 0.4% of pixels changing by > 40/255 attributable to a band swap | disable fade; the pop must appear and exceed the threshold |
| **wind is alive and deterministic** | Over 180 frames with a static camera, ≥ 15% of vegetation pixels change by > 8/255 between consecutive frames; and the same seed gives identical frame hashes | freeze wind; the motion figure must go to ~0. Switch wind to wall clock; hashes must diverge |
| **regions are distinguishable** | Naive judges shown unlabelled eye-level shots correctly group 13 regions into their declared families ≥ 60% of the time, and correctly answer "are these two shots from the same region?" ≥ 75% | apply one landform and one palette to all; both must fall to chance |
| **the walk is not dull** | `node corpus/80-methods/m-wld15-monotony.mjs` passes at its native thresholds — this is RI-WLD15's own instrument and F does not invent a substitute; plus naive judges watching the 6 five-minute walk contact sheets rate "was this interesting to walk through?" ≥ 6.0 | flatten to one landform; the method must go red and the judge score must fall |
| **regions have a geology** | `node corpus/80-methods/m-wld16-landform-census.mjs` passes: every region resolves to a landform grammar in `corpus/50-world/landforms.json` and expresses it in shipped terrain | remove a landform from a region; the census must go red |
| **budget** | ≤ 1400 draw calls and ≤ 2.5 M triangles in the worst vegetated vista; instancing preserved | — |
| **human read** | Deck Craft ≥ pre-F + 1.5 on the minimum over the 26 region shots | — |

**Hard fails:** wall-clock-driven wind; a region without a landform profile; foliage without
mipmaps; LOD swap without fade; loss of the province height/collision source of truth; a second
implementation of a corpus method F should have consumed.

## Falsification audit

The strongest attack: **variety can be added as noise rather than as design.** Three distinguishable
terrain situations per walk is satisfiable by randomising, and randomised landscape is *also* dull —
it is dull in a different way, because nothing means anything. The guard is the human read on the
walk contact sheets, which asks whether it was interesting, not whether it varied.

The second: region distinguishability can be won on palette alone. Recolouring 13 identical
landscapes would pass a hue-distance check. That is why the grouping test is by *family* and why the
landform profile has a separate corpus-resolution gate.

Least certain: whether RI-WLD15's and RI-WLD16's thresholds are reachable by a procedural terrain
generator at all, or whether "never dull" requires hand-placed landform set-pieces the way Morrowind
actually did it. If `m-wld15-monotony.mjs` proves unreachable procedurally, the honest answer is a
hand-authored landform pass over the worst corridors, not a threshold negotiation — and F should say
so and cost it rather than tuning noise amplitudes until the metric moves.
