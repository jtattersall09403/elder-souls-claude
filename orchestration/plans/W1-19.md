## 3. Sequential execution and exact acceptance

### Gate A — honest chain population

Freeze the 40 character signatures and both endings/backpath at the tested commit. For every step
emit: quest/resolution, loaded state/cell, giver id and distance, topics/reveals learned and their
world sources, gate before/after, action invoked, journal index, durable consequences, clock and
marker count. A step is complete only when the shipped `QuestEngine` applies it after player-facing
actions. Hard fail on `questOpen`, `questReveal`, `questNote`, `questSetFlag`, `questResolve`,
`learnTopic`, `spawnNPC`, direct state/file reads used to advance, or an absent person/object.

Acceptance: **40/40 × 2 chains** complete; **0** violent resolutions; **0** bought gate openings in
the no-purse arm; **0/31** arrived-short with all 31 measured; every giver used is talkable; every
required reveal has a routed world action; marker count **0**. Report median and worst steps/time,
but counts—not timing—decide pass.

### Gate B — non-vacuous choices and crossing

For every disposition/attribute/knowledge/faction gate run one failing and one passing character.
For every exclusive choice play both siblings from the same pre-choice save and compare the
reference item’s state vector. Require ≥3 distinct durable flags, ≥1 reward and ≥1 entity delta and
route-specific journal prose. The Q-MAIN-23 clause arm must still produce roster 6→4 through the
real consequence pipeline; its sibling remains 6. A hand-set flag or file-side declaration fails.

### Gate C — last-hour and aftermath

Use matched pre-PONR saves. Run intended ending, alternative and backpath at least twice each.
Acceptance is the conjunction of every RI-EXP05 metric, with special hard floors: PONR ≥.94 of the
chain, ≥3 independent signals, ≥4 acknowledged permanent closures, 0 confirmation modal, ≥12
antagonist topics/≥4 knowledge-gated, ≥2 verified claims, 0 authorial refutation, ≥25 durable world
flag delta, and the item’s exact NPC-reaction/specificity/aftermath/blind thresholds. The 40-NPC
population must be 40 distinct reachable NPCs sampled by a predeclared settlement/faction strata;
missing speakers are failures, not skips.

### Gate D — lore and opacity

Run complete RI-LOR01/02/05 and RI-WLD09 tools over the final tree and played transcripts. Every
row must print denominator and source. Zero empty populations, silent skips, unresolved ids or
undeclared deviations. Then run boot/data/quest/content/corpus gates and all six governing methods
together.
