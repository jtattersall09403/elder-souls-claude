## 5. Independent plan criticism (2026-08-09)

**Verdict: BLOCKING / awaiting-remediation.** The acceptance contract never binds RI-WPN05 M2
(all 18 material-multiplier cells and full enemy-material census), M3 (deterministic deflection,
bypasses, and exactly +16 f@60), M7 (presentation discipline), or M8 (M1/M2/M4 at five seeds).
Those are not optional supporting checks: M2 and M3 carry 25 native points, M7 gates the item, and
M8 is the required determinism comparison. A build can green every named plan row—panner,
hitstop/whiff, motion, camera/ILS, audio blind and budget—while material damage is wrong,
deflection is probabilistic, multiple decals spawn, or the result changes by seed. The closing
instruction to run four items “together” does not state these predicates or make their evidence
mandatory.

The camera row also needs seam-safe wording: CAM06 permits damage shake only when the **player
takes damage** and forbids it on block, parry, enemy death and landing, whereas RI-WPN05's ILS
requests a shake component across its impact cells but expressly defers camera authority to
CAM06. “Every eligible impact cell fires real combat shake” is undefined and could green an ILS
by violating CAM06's hard constraints.

**Required remediation:** add WPN05 M2, M3, M7 and M8 verbatim to the acceptance manifest with
their exact populations, tolerances, five-seed comparison, hard fails, and red controls. Resolve
the WPN05/CAM06 shake seam before build (as already required for AQ-02), explicitly define which
cells may have nonzero rendered shake, require zero in CAM06-forbidden cells, and define how ILS is
scored without authorizing forbidden shake. The aggregate must fail when any of these artifacts is
absent.

No CARRIED finding is appropriate: these are known native rows and a known governing seam.
