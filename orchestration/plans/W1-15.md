S1–S3 bind every STL01 axis and populations; T1 re-proves all STL02 axes rather than inheriting 7; C1 requires damage/consequence from a natural witness chain; C2 requires the entire CRM02 lifecycle; Q1 binds every numeric hard fail and subsystem dependency; Q2 plays ten real quests; M1 makes the formerly inert vacuity guard fail on purpose. Cross-piece dependencies may be assigned but cannot lower the result. Successful execution therefore establishes, rather than approximates, every governing bar.

## Independent plan critique — BLOCKING / awaiting-remediation (2026-08-09)

The falsification proposition does **not** hold. The contract is broad, but broad phrases such as
“every threshold” do not require several governing comparison methods, binary hard fails, or their
native populations. A builder can satisfy S1–G1 exactly as written and still miss the bar.

**Biggest actionable gap:** add a one-to-one acceptance matrix for every method/axis/hard fail in
RI-STL01, RI-STL02, RI-CRM01, RI-CRM02, and RI-QST05, using the amended arithmetic where governing.
At minimum the remediation must close these concrete green-but-wrong routes:

- S1–S3 omit RI-STL01's 100,000-case formula sweep and clamp assertions, OPENER-PURITY's byte-level
  Sneak 20/100 replay plus Sneak-19 gate, the NO-METER pixel comparison, the civilian four-state
  model/dialogue off-ramp, the all-eight-loop dark/snuffable audit, and the ≥22 sneak-resolution
  quest load. “All prescribed axes” and a general moving fixture do not execute those methods or
  enforce their binary automatic failures. Restore their exact denominators, tolerances, trace
  fields, and controls.
- T1 collapses RI-STL02's distinct methods into a whole-object census and unspecified “bands.” It
  must expressly require the deterministic lock trace and zero RNG draws, non-uniform authored
  lock-angle χ² test, tier/on-ramp and alternate-route populations, 200-NPC equipped-item exclusion
  sample, trespass/pickpocket cases, fence price arithmetic under the accepted amendment, and each
  item hard fail. Otherwise a natural-action demo plus persistence census can pass while the lock
  or pickpocket bar fails.
- C1 stops at witness→guard→a consequential surrender. RI-CRM01 separately governs the three arrest
  answers, confiscation, deterministic highest-skill jail loss, faction consequences, blood-price,
  and persistence across death. Those are neither populations nor acceptance predicates in C1;
  save/load is not death persistence. Add the item-native bounty cases, report timing/attribution,
  arrest/jail/faction/blood-price/death methods and red arms.
- C2's “writ lifecycle” is too generic to compel CRM02's full authority/membership matrix,
  exclusivity maximum, all authored writ counts/shapes, sanctioned-target dialogue/parley hard fail,
  guard-behaviour disposition arithmetic, report-chain rules, and player-as-target cases. Enumerate
  those methods and denominators rather than treating all qualifying crimes/factions as sufficient.
- Q2 says “deterministic random eligible quests” but must use RI-QST05's native once-per-wave random
  sample frame and preserve the predeclared selection without allowing eligibility filtering or
  substitution to choose ten easy `pacifist_all` records. Q1 must retain the exact jq-derived
  denominators and all hard fails, including the subsystem-presence conditions, while Q2 proves the
  sampled routes in the live engine.

Each restored instrument needs its own deliberate failing mutation, consumption/producer cut where
it computes a model, and aggregate run at the same commit. These omissions are definitional and
resolvable before build; none qualifies as CARRIED.
