**Verdict: BLOCKING / awaiting-remediation.**

The plan does not preserve every governing comparison method. It specifies WLD04 M17's shuffled 117-frame judgement, but step 8 reduces RI-AI05/RI-PRG06 to a census and “native methods,” and the blind work actually required by RI-AI05 is nowhere in the acceptance or artifact list: ten unlabelled 90-second fingerprints from the build must be mixed with ten generated from the reference bands and independently clustered, with fewer than six clusters a loss. Add tell and wrong-population controls for each and require their results in the final aggregate.
