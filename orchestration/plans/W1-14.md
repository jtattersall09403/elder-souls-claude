## 5. Independent plan criticism (2026-08-09)

**Verdict: BLOCKING / awaiting-remediation.** The projectile acceptance invokes a nonexistent
“MAG01 native threshold” for hit rate in every moving-target stratum and requires a
pure-pursuit/delete-lead arm to go red. RI-MAG01 does not set a moving-target hit-rate floor. Its
governing comparisons instead require M4's aim-latch residual (median at least 25°, fail below
10°), yaw-rate/commitment limits; M5's 40 moving plus 40 stationary outcomes explained 40/40 by
recomputed swept geometry with zero PRNG draws; and M6's speed, lifetime, radius, post-cutoff
turn-rate and dodge-window predicates. A builder could define an accommodating local hit-rate
threshold, green the planned range table, and still violate aim latch, geometry, or enemy dodge
windows. Conversely, optimizing lead to an undefined hit-rate bar can directly weaken the native
anti-homing requirement.

**Required remediation:** delete the invented hit-rate acceptance and replace it with the exact
MAG01 M4–M6 predicates, populations and units, including the 30-trial aim-latch residual, 40+40
offline sweep reconstruction, zero RNG draws, every projectile class, and every encounter's enemy
`min_dodge_window >= 20 f@60`. Define sabotage arms against those native predicates (for example,
post-cutoff steering must breach `2 deg/s`, and a geometry perturbation must break the 40/40
reconstruction) rather than requiring a particular guidance algorithm to lose. Keep stationary and
lateral range strata as additional adversarial evidence, not a substituted bar.

No CARRIED finding is appropriate: the native comparison is fully specified before build.
