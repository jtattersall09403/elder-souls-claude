**The audit's charge, and it was correct as of the plan text it read.**
`orchestration/audits/PLAN-AUDIT-2026-08-14.md` §5 convicts this plan's headline predicate —
*"A+B ≥276 merely to avoid the fail floor and ≥460 for its target"* — as a province-wide count
carrying no obligation about what a **walk** actually crosses, with M6/M8/M9 traversal gated behind
it so the static count decides whether the walk is ever measured at all. Two hundred and seventy-six
points of interest spread over thirteen regions is a number about the province; a player walks one
route through one region for six minutes.

**But the repair is not one I have to invent, because Ruling W1 already made it — in three items
this piece owns, on 2026-08-14, and none of it is in the received plan.** All three amendments are
in the corpus at HEAD and I read them there rather than taking the audit's word:

- **`RI-WLD02` gained D14 and D15**, both weight 2 in the native aggregation. **D14:** place-type
  Jaccard **≤ 0.727 on every region pair** (Vvardenfell's own most-similar pair), fail at any pair
  > 0.85. **D15:** **≥1 POI class unique to every region, 13 of 13**, fail at any region with none.
  The amendment's own words are this plan's charge sheet: *"the whole POI apparatus above — three
  tiers, 1,060 targets, D1–D13 — is province-global … a world that scatters one uniform tier mix
  over all thirteen regions passes every D-metric in this item, and a player crossing it finds the
  same menu everywhere."*
- **`RI-WLD09` gained V10 and V11.** **V11** imports `RI-WLD15`'s W15-1 and W15-3 **unrelaxed**
  inside every declared void tract, because this item *"mandated ≥240-second POI-free walks and
  required nothing whatever of the landscape crossed during them — emptiness and sameness collected
  as one prize."* **V10** requires every tract polygon to lie in the region it names (≥50% of
  polygon area inside that region's bounds in `terrain.json`'s frame); **all five declared tracts
  currently fail it**, so their variety numbers are `unmeasurable`, not passing.
- **`RI-WLD15` is a new governing item for this piece.** It judges `world.density.handplacement`,
  which `docs/PLAN.md` gives W1-27 as its first owned subsystem path. It is the item that measures
  what a walk crosses, and it names *"fixing it by adding POIs"* as a way to lose: *"a landmark
  every 200 m does not make the ground between them change, and `RI-WLD02` already pays for
  landmarks."*

So the obligation the audit found missing now exists natively, in three places, and this round's job
was to transcribe it and un-gate the walk — not to write a new predicate over the top of `≥276`.
The `≥276` floor itself is untouched: it is native `RI-WLD02` score-zero text and no threshold in
this plan moves.
