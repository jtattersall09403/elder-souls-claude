### D. The played gate — the human read this piece did not have (Ruling W2)

A closure gate, not a change to any native threshold. Nothing below lowers a bar; a green bar matrix
without this gate is not closure. It exists because a build can satisfy every row in §§A–C and ship
a reward loop nobody can feel, and no row above would notice.

**Two of its questions are the items' own player-facing predicates, not questions I invented.**
`RI-PRG07` method 5 requires that above burden 1.00 *"movement is exactly 0 … **and that the player
is told why in prose, not a red icon**"* — a predicate about what is on the screen, which no offline
sweep can answer. `RI-UIX03` requires the burden and equip-load readouts to show correct units and
current values **and change colour/label at the item threshold**. Both are already binding; this
section is where they are actually looked at.

1. **Population, bounded, and deliberately small.** One driven session per arm, **≤15 minutes**,
   two arms, four sessions of play in total. Within the session the agent must: level up at a hearth
   **at least twice** with souls it earned; carry a pack from Unburdened across **0.60**, **0.85**
   and past **1.00**; and fight or flee once at **Light** and once at **Heavy** equip tier using the
   `RI-PRG07` §5 shipped builds (the **Fence** at Medium and the **Shell-Warden** at Heavy are the
   two the item names, so use those rather than inventing loadouts).
2. **Conditions.** Production build served by `tools/play.mjs`, shipped HUD, no debug overlay, no
   harness readout visible, no numbers from this plan. The agent receives the URL, the driver
   protocol and one sentence of premise — nothing from this plan, the items or the source.
3. **The questions, asked after playing and recorded verbatim before any reveal.**
   - *"You levelled up twice. What changed? Point at where you noticed."* (`RI-PRG01`/`RI-PRG02`)
   - *"Was levelling up worth what it cost you?"* with reasons. (`RI-PRG01`, `RI-PRG06`)
   - *"At any point you could not move. Did the game tell you why, and in what words?"* An answer
     naming only an icon or a colour is a **`RI-PRG07` method 5 failure**, already binding.
     (`RI-PRG07` §5, `RI-UIX03`)
   - *"Did you notice getting heavier before you were stopped? What told you?"* (`RI-PRG07` §3)
   - *"You fought in light gear and in heavy gear. Describe the difference in your own words."*
     (`RI-PRG07` §2, `RI-CMB01`)
   - *"Was the heavy build playable, or just worse?"* — the qualitative twin of method 7's
     ≥15% win-rate sim, which measures survivability and cannot measure whether it is any fun.
   - And the one that decides the gate: *"Was getting stronger in this game good?"* with reasons.
4. **The null arm that must come out worse.** Repeat both sessions on a copy with the **burden→world
   consumer disconnected** — the 1 / .9 / .72 / 0 world multipliers all forced to 1 so carrying
   weight has no world-side effect — everything else identical: same seeds, same builds, same
   routes, equip tier and in-fight frames untouched (touching those would be an AR-1 violation, not
   a control). This is the **plausible** wrong answer, not a broken one: it is a build where the
   numbers are all correct and nothing you carry ever matters, which is the shipped state of many
   games and is not visibly broken from a screenshot. Build it with
   `node tools/control-clone.mjs make --writable <the burden data/source file>` — hard-linked, so it
   costs kilobytes rather than a full-tree copy, and getting `--writable` right is what stops it
   corrupting the real tree.
   **Effect size, stated before the run:** the shipped arm must report a *specific* moment where
   carrying weight changed what it did — a decision to drop loot, a route taken because it was
   slower loaded, a fight avoided — where the ablated arm reports none; **and** the two arms'
   verbatim answers to question (3d) must be separable by a third fresh reader with the arm labels
   removed. If the two arms read the same, this gate is **inert and cannot pass**: it is reported as
   inert, exactly as an inert consumer is, and the piece does not close on it. This is the arm that
   stops the gate becoming a rubber stamp.
5. **Aggregation.** Worst arm, worst session — never a mean over the four (Ruling W1).
6. **Independence.** A **fresh agent plays and a fresh agent judges**, and neither is the builder;
   the builder produces the two arms and never plays or scores them. Arm identity is quarantined
   until the verbatim answers are recorded. Run `node tools/blind/leakcheck.mjs` over any packaged
   material before the judge sees it.
7. **Instruments reused, by path — no new tool is authorised for this gate.**
   `tools/play.mjs` serves the build; `tools/harness/prg-encumbrance.mjs` and the existing
   `tools/harness/w1-16-r3-live.mjs` / `w1-16-r3-reach.mjs` already instantiate the §5 builds and
   drive the tier boundaries, so the played sessions start from fixtures this piece already owns;
   `tools/control-clone.mjs` builds the ablated arm; `tools/capture/client.mjs` (`CaptureSession`,
   one connection for a whole sequence) supplies the motion sequences Directive §2 requires, since a
   burden readout is a thing that *changes* and one still cannot show it changing;
   `tools/blind/leakcheck.mjs` supplies the tell audit.
8. **Cost.** Four ≤15-minute driven sessions on a browser this piece already pays for, plus one
   hard-linked clone. That is a small fraction of one build round, and it is the only evidence
   anywhere in this piece that a person met the reward loop.

**Effect sizes on the §B/§C perturbation rows (Ruling W1).** Rows phrased as "perturb and observe a
