## 5. Independent plan criticism (2026-08-09)

**Verdict: BLOCKING / awaiting-remediation.** The proposed `20 seeds × 7 moments` save population
does not instantiate RI-JRN05's binding SV1–SV5 scenarios. The seven listed moments are engine-state
instants (idle, attack, hitstop, falling, death, money/inventory, AI transition), but the native
population deliberately includes a quest three stages in with exhausted dialogue and disposition,
a half-mutated dungeon, post-rank consequences, cross-jurisdiction crime/witness/stolen-item/hunt
state, and the post-death bloodstain/journal state. A builder can pass all seven planned moments and
the `prev_state == 0/600` headline while never testing M6–M9 or the exact five-scenario M1/M3/M5
denominators. “Every shipped state required” is not an executable replacement for those fixtures.

**Required remediation:** make SV1–SV5, with every state named in RI-JRN05 §D, the binding
round-trip/cold-reload/600-frame population; state M1/M3/M5 as exactly `5/5`, and enumerate M6
`6/6`, M7 exact bloodstain recovery, M8 `4/4 + control`, and M9 exact ordered journal checks.
Additional attack/hitstop/falling moments may remain adversarial supplements, but must not replace
the five native scenarios. The acceptance also needs the native M10–M19 and CR populations stated
or mechanically enumerated in a manifest, rather than hidden behind “all rows.”

No CARRIED finding is appropriate: the omitted native save populations are knowable before build.
