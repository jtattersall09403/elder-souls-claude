## Independent plan critique — 2026-08-09

**Verdict: BLOCKING / awaiting-remediation.**

The plan's singular “RI-VIS06 pack” can go green while violating the governing visual comparison method. RI-VIS06 mandates **two separate protocols**, one per side of the bifurcation, never judged by the same agent: Protocol A uses its exact fidelity prompt and modern reference routing; Protocol B/B1 uses the art-direction/strangeness procedure, mandatory equal degradation, and a judge who has not read the fidelity references. The plan instead describes one generic fresh judge and one generic quality question. It also makes RI-CAM07 step 5 explicitly run M1–M4 although RI-CAM07 has M5 equipment/cloth plus F2–F5 blind art-direction rows; the earlier phrase “native runnable” does not supply their populations or acceptance and the later explicit range permits omission. Thus protocol self-tests, M1–M4, and a single blind pack could all pass while the player's back still fails governing equipment and art-direction requirements.

**Required remediation:** split acceptance into RI-VIS06 Protocol A and Protocol B/B1 with separate eligible judges, exact native prompts, permitted reference sets, B1 degradation, B2 follow-up, escalation/caps, and separate quarantined keys. Expand RI-CAM07 acceptance through M5 and F2–F5 (including three authored sets per slot, mask/IoU artifacts, and native thresholds), or mark any currently impossible row fail-closed rather than omitting it.
