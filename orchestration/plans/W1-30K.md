# W1-30K — the look target: art direction written as numbers

Plan-State: awaiting-criticism
Parent: [W1-30](W1-30.md) · Contract: [Builder execution contract](BUILDER-EXECUTION-CONTRACT.md)
Evidence: [W1-30-EVIDENCE.md](W1-30-EVIDENCE.md) · Reuse: [W1-30-LIBRARY.md](W1-30-LIBRARY.md)

**Added by the parent's recriticism round under amendment R2.** The previous reviewer flagged that
W1-30E and W1-30F *"may not cohere without"* a concept pass, declined to add one because it could not
establish we can draw or license key art, and wrote that a reviewer adding it would be right.

This child resolves that by **not drawing and not licensing anything.** Art direction nobody has
written down becomes whatever each builder individually imagined, and eleven parallel children is
exactly the population that turns that into eleven different games. But the failure of the old "style
boards" was not that they were unillustrated — it was that they were **prose**. `world-art.js` gives
a region one terrain word, three flora scalars, a lean and a depth phrase. A builder cannot hit that
and a critic cannot check it.

**So the deliverable is numbers**: per region and per settlement, the ranges a frame must land in,
derived entirely from plates the corpus already holds and is already permitted to cite.

Dispatch **now**. It touches no game code, blocks nothing, and is blocked by nothing. The earlier it
lands, the more of E's and F's work it can aim rather than correct.

`docs/art-direction/**`.

## Where the "art" comes from — the objection that stopped this child last time

Nothing is drawn and nothing is acquired. Every input is already in the repository:

| source | n | what `RI-VIS09` says it is valid for | which half of the board it feeds |
|---|---|---|---|
| `refs/morrowind/REF-A*` | 89 | §3.3, explicitly: palette, silhouette, built form, flora and creature design language — and **never** texture resolution, texel density, AA or sharpness | **ART** |
| `refs/anti-generic/` | 5 | `RI-VIS07`: the thing to measure distance **from**, never a target | **ART**, as the negative anchor |
| `refs/context/` | populated | `RI-VIS09` §2: Shadowfen/Murkmire **context only**, never a target on either axis | **ART**, as context and as a named thing not to converge on |
| `refs/modern/` by profile | 131 | value range, contrast, atmospheric depth, what a shipped renderer does here | **FIDELITY** |

The one use `RI-VIS09` §3.3 affirmatively grants those 320×320 Morrowind thumbnails is exactly a look
board's content. This child is the use they were acquired for.

**The bifurcation is a hazard here and is handled explicitly.** `RI-VIS01`'s CC-3 is the cardinal sin
and cross-citation is how it happens. The board therefore ships as **two declared, disjoint halves** —
`docs/art-direction/FIDELITY.md` citing only `refs/modern/`, `docs/art-direction/ART.md` citing only
`refs/morrowind/` and `refs/anti-generic/` — each carrying `RI-VIS01`'s side declaration. A row citing
across the line is a hard fail of this child.

## The work

1. **Per-region boards, 13 of them.** For each: hue / value / chroma range as a bounded swatch set;
   key-to-fill ratio; horizon visibility distance and the fog density that produces it; the landform
   verbs from `corpus/50-world/landforms.json` this region expresses; canopy and understorey verbs;
   the wear vocabulary its materials carry. Numbers with units, plus the plate each number was read
   from.
2. **Per-settlement boards, 8 of them.** Roofline verbs, silhouette rhythm at 200 m, palette and trim
   swatches, the density and kind of street dressing, and the one thing that must be true of a street
   shot for a judge to attribute it correctly. Keyed to the grammars `world-art.js` already names.
3. **The three cross-cutting decisions**, made once instead of eleven times: the province-wide value
   structure (what is dark, what is light, and where the eye goes); the saturation ceiling, because
   an over-saturated marsh is the single most common way this subject goes wrong; and the
   "strangeness budget" — how far from a real swamp each region sits, which is `RI-VIS05`'s
   transposition question and the thing that stops Black Marsh reading as Louisiana.
4. **A machine-readable emission**, `docs/art-direction/board.json`, so a critic checks a frame
   against a range rather than against a paragraph. This is the difference between this child and the
   style boards that failed.
5. **The consumer map.** For each number, which child consumes it: A's grade recipes, B's fog profiles
   and lighting recipes, C's palette variants, E's grammars and trim selection, F's landform profiles
   and scatter families, G's room palettes.
6. **The honest gaps register.** Where the plates cannot support a number, say so and leave it unset.
   An unset row is a legitimate output; an invented row is the failure this child exists to prevent.

## The bar

| gate | acceptance, with units | null control |
|---|---|---|
| **every number is sourced** | 100% of rows in `board.json` name the plate path(s) they were read from and the statistic used. Zero unsourced numbers. | insert a row with no plate reference; the board's own validator must reject it and exit non-zero |
| **the bifurcation holds** | Zero rows in `FIDELITY.md` cite `refs/morrowind/`, `refs/anti-generic/` or `refs/context/`; zero rows in `ART.md` cite `refs/modern/`. `RI-VIS01`'s cc-scan clean on both. | cross-cite one row on a copy; the scan must fail and the verdict void |
| **it discriminates** | The 13 region boards are mutually distinguishable: no two regions' swatch+landform+canopy triples fall within the corpus swatch distance of each other. A board that describes every region equally describes none. | collapse two regions to one board; the check must go red |
| **it is reachable, not aspirational** | Every number is expressed as a **range a renderer can land in**, with the current build's measured value recorded beside it. A target the current build already meets is recorded as met, not restated as work. | — |
| **it is consumed** | ≥ 5 of the 9 game-code children cite at least one board row in their builder report; every row in `board.json` names ≥ 1 consuming child or is marked `advisory` | — |
| **it does not contaminate** | No judging pack built by W1-30V contains any board artefact; K's outputs are builder-facing only | plant a board page in a pack; V's quarantine check must reject it |
| **the gaps are declared** | Every unset row carries the reason it is unset and the plate that would fill it | — |

**Hard fails:** a number with no plate behind it; a cross-side citation; any write to `game/src/**` or
`corpus/**`; a board row that is prose where a number was possible; K authoring a judging pack.

## Builder/critic execution allocation

**Builder** — read the plates and compute the statistics with a committed, re-runnable script under
`docs/art-direction/`; write the 13 region and 8 settlement boards, the three cross-cutting decisions,
`board.json` and its validator; record the current build's measured value beside every target; write
the gaps register; run the validator and the cc-scan; demonstrate both going red on a copy; text-only
evidence, no new binaries.

**Fresh critic** — independently re-derive a sample of ≥ 20 numbers from the named plates and confirm
they reproduce; audit every citation against `RI-VIS09` §2's routing table; run the full cc-scan;
check the discrimination test across all 13 regions; verify the gaps register is honest rather than
convenient (a board with no gaps at all, from 320×320 thumbnails, is the finding); confirm no board
artefact has reached a judging pack.

## Reuse rows

**Consumed:** `refs/morrowind/REF-A*`, `refs/anti-generic/`, `refs/context/`, `refs/modern/`,
`refs/reference-metrics.json`, `corpus/50-world/landforms.json`, `world-art.js`'s existing region and
settlement records — which are the vocabulary this child gives numbers to, not a thing it replaces.
**Published:** the boards and `board.json`, consumed by A, B, C, E, F and G, and by every Wave-2
visual piece thereafter. **Variants:** a settlement board is a variant spec over its region's board —
a settlement inherits its region's value structure and overrides palette, roofline and dressing.

## Dependencies and stop conditions

None. Never blocks and is never blocked; children in flight consume the boards when they land and
proceed against `world-art.js` until then.

**Stop condition, and it is the real one.** If the plates cannot honestly support a number, leave it
unset. If **most** rows come out unset — if 89 thumbnails at 320×320 and 131 modern frames genuinely
cannot ground a look target — then this child's honest output is a short document saying so and naming
what acquisition would fix it, and the parent's R2 ruling is overturned by its own falsifier. That is a
legitimate and complete result. Do not fill the board by inventing plausible numbers: a target every
child is then measured against is the most expensive possible place to guess.

## Falsification audit

**The strongest attack** is that this child produces a document nobody reads, which is what happened to
the last set of style boards. The guard is the consumption gate — ≥ 5 children citing rows, every row
naming a consumer — and the machine-readable emission, so a critic can check a frame against a range
without reading anything. If the consumption gate fails, the boards were not useful and saying so is
the finding.

**The second** is that a board can be *correct and wrong*: every number honestly derived, and the
resulting look still dull, because "the mean value of Morrowind's Bitter Coast thumbnails" is not art
direction, it is arithmetic. This is the real risk and it is not fully guarded. The partial answer is
item 3 — the value structure, the saturation ceiling and the strangeness budget are **judgements**,
made once, in the open, by an agent that has looked at the plates, and they are the rows most worth
attacking in criticism. The rest of the board is arithmetic in support of those three.

**Least certain**: whether 320×320 crops with `pixel_metrics_valid: false` on all 89 can ground even
the palette and silhouette numbers `RI-VIS09` §3.3 says they support. §3.3 grants the *use*; it does
not promise the precision. If the derived swatches turn out too wide to discriminate 13 regions, the
discrimination gate above catches it, and the honest response is fewer, wider, more confident
statements rather than 13 precise ones that are noise.
