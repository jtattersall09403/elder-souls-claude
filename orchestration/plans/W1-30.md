## Production route: cheap, shared and wide before polish


### Current Three.js baseline and permitted implementation envelope

This is not a greenfield renderer. The game vendors **Three.js r180 core only** in
`game/vendor/three/`; there is no installed examples/addons tree or asset-build package.
`game/src/render/renderer.js` owns the single `WebGLRenderer`, ACES filmic tone mapping, sRGB output,
`PCFSoftShadowMap`, world/UI pass order and render statistics. World construction is split across
`scene.js`, `exterior.js`, `interior.js`, `places.js`, `actor.js`, `sky.js`, `spell-vfx.js` and
`world/province.js`. It already uses `MeshStandardMaterial` and instancing, but many surfaces are
constant-colour procedural primitives, many emissives/decals/windows use `MeshBasicMaterial`, and
world presentation is a direct `renderer.render(scene, camera)` with no post chain. The builder must
evolve those shipping seams rather than create a parallel beauty-shot demo.

The viable envelope is **WebGLRenderer + Three r180**, with additive vendoring of only the specific
same-version official Three addons needed (including licences), or small project-owned passes where
an addon does not meet the item. Do not upgrade Three, adopt WebGPU, replace the engine or import a
large framework on the critical path unless a cheap compatibility spike proves the r180 route cannot
meet a governing predicate. Avoid scattered `onBeforeCompile` patching: a necessary custom effect
gets one named shader/material module, a program-cache key, fallback and sabotage switch.

### Production-standard Three.js implementation recipe

The builder implements the smallest coherent subset below that reaches every native bar; none of
these techniques counts until shipping pixels and the named consumer prove it.

1. **One colour-managed HDR world pipeline.** Preserve `SRGBColorSpace` at final output. Mark
   authored base-colour/emissive textures sRGB and normal/roughness/metalness/AO/height/masks as
   non-colour data. Render the world to a half-float HDR target where supported, then apply exposure,
   ACES and output conversion exactly once. Composite existing UI/title surfaces after world tone
   mapping so UI is neither bloomed nor converted twice. Keep an observable direct-render fallback
   for unsupported/low-tier devices.
2. **A bounded compositor, not filter stacking.** Introduce one renderer-owned world composer
   (same-r180 official `EffectComposer`, `RenderPass` and `OutputPass` may be vendored). Add only
   evidenced passes: depth/normal prepass when required; GTAO/SSAO before transparencies;
   thresholded multi-resolution bloom; and SMAA as the WebGL floor. Prefer native MSAA where valid.
   TAA is optional and may replace SMAA only with motion/history invalidation for cuts, teleports,
   resize, cell/seed changes and animated foliage/VFX. Every pass owns enable, resize, dispose,
   ordering, sabotage and low-tier fallback behaviour.
3. **Physically coherent lighting and IBL.** Use one authoritative sun/moon direction for sky, fog,
   lighting and shadows. Fit and texel-snap a `DirectionalLight` shadow camera around the playable
   camera neighbourhood with tuned normal bias; if one map cannot span the required range, use a
   small cascade set rather than a huge blurry map. Generate a `PMREMGenerator` environment from the
   procedural sky/weather or a legal authored HDR environment and assign `scene.environment`.
   `LightProbe`/hemisphere is ambient support, not an IBL substitute. Use bounded authored practical
   lights and interior probes/environments instead of uncontrolled per-prop lights.
4. **A material library, not a colour dictionary.** Add one data-driven family factory/registry
   consumed by exterior, interior, places, actors, province and VFX. Each family declares texture
   set, physical scale/UV policy, base colour, roughness/metalness, normal strength, AO, emissive,
   alpha mode, palette variants and fallback. Use `MeshStandardMaterial` for ordinary opaque
   surfaces and `MeshPhysicalMaterial` only for governed clearcoat/transmission/sheen/iridescence.
   Restrict `MeshBasicMaterial` to UI/debug and bounded emissive/decal uses. Reuse materials and use
   vertex/instance colour or atlases for variation rather than one shader program per prop.
5. **Production asset ingestion.** Admit legal authored glTF/GLB only through a manifest recording
   licence/source, real-world scale, bounds, LODs, material families and consumers. Vendor matching
   r180 `GLTFLoader` and `KTX2Loader`/Basis only if used. Prefer compressed mipmapped KTX2 textures,
   bounded anisotropy, atlases for repeated small props and stable texel density. Keep source assets
   and conversion reproducible; generated outputs and binary evidence are not planning artifacts.
6. **Terrain that holds up near and far.** Replace single vertex-colour expanses with
   palette-controlled multi-scale blending driven by height, slope, region and authored masks:
   macro colour/roughness breakup, tiled detail normals, wetness and contact transitions. Break
   silhouettes with bounded rocks, roots, litter and decals, not noise alone. Preserve the province
   height/collision source of truth. Consume W1-03 water; do not fork its shader.
7. **Vegetation as layered geometry.** Continue `InstancedMesh`/batched families for trunks, root
   cages, reeds, fungal/aquatic/xeric layers, deterministically scattered by region. Use alpha-tested
   mipmapped foliage rather than broad blended cards, per-instance hue/scale/bend variation and
   stable seeded shader wind. Supply near geometry, simplified mid mesh/card and far cluster/cull
   bands with hysteresis or fade so M11 sees no pop ring. Each exterior family still needs an
   inexplicable Black-Marsh hybrid; density is not art direction.
8. **LOD, streaming and memory as visible behaviour.** Give costly families explicit distance bands
   through `THREE.LOD` or the existing province chunks; cull/stream chunks beyond the active horizon;
   pool geometry/material/texture resources; dispose only after the last live reference; and prewarm
   the bounded shader set outside combat. Cross-fade/dither or overlap silhouette-changing bands and
   use hysteresis to prevent thrash. Renderer draw/triangle/program/geometry/texture figures and VFX
   budgets remain binding.
9. **Characters and animation through W1-06.** Upgrade existing rigs or load legal skinned glTF into
   one skeleton/material vocabulary. Use `AnimationMixer` clips with authored crossfades, additive
   upper-body layers where justified, root-motion agreement with simulation, attachment sockets and
   deterministic secondary motion. Add contact shadows/AO, normal/roughness detail and equipment
   variation before expensive skin effects. Preserve camera-readable attack/recovery silhouettes and
   prove locomotion, turn, roll, attack, swap, slope/IK and near-plane cases through the landed seam.
10. **Atmosphere, sky and weather as one depth system.** Couple the procedural sky's sun, horizon and
    region palette to fog colour/exposure and height-dependent atmosphere. A lightweight depth-based
    height-fog/scattering pass is valid where `FogExp2` cannot produce the required height tell; it
    reconstructs world depth, excludes sky and survives resize/teleport. Blend weather/time
    parameters instead of rebuilding materials, and give dry highlands, wet marsh and emissive
    interiors materially different responses rather than recoloured fog.
11. **VFX integrated with depth, light and materials.** Extend existing pooled/instanced systems:
    soft particles read scene depth; mesh spells use lit materials; emissives feed thresholded bloom
    without flattening the frame; residues use bounded decal/projection pools; distortion, if used,
    samples one opaque-scene copy rather than per-effect copies. Stable seeds, transparency order,
    overdraw, day/night/interior response and native per-spell/whole-frame budgets choose what ships.
12. **Art direction authored into kits and composition.** Translate RI-VIS05 into the shared
    registries: region swatches/forbidden-colour distance; wet/chitin/resin/bone/root/clay/salt
    families; asymmetric modules; architecture grammar; flora/creature tags; landmark/skyline rules;
    and deliberate Imperial exceptions. For every 13 regions and eight settlements, add a compact
    text/data style board naming dominant/contrast materials, silhouette motif, inexplicable element,
    atmosphere response and forbidden generic forms. Build breadth through modular kits plus authored
    hero silhouettes, not one showcase or undirected asset-library intake.

   current gameplay contracts. New content must enter through a validated family and declare art
