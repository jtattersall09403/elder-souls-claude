## Production route: cheap, shared and wide before polish

1. **Freeze the authority and census the live graph.** At the dependency landing commit, regenerate
   path→item applicability and inventory every render/material/asset/VFX/animation/stream consumer.
   Perturb each legacy entry point to establish it actually runs. Record missing, duplicate and
   Basic/fallback paths. Do not refactor dead code as if it were the game.
2. **Make measurement valid before tuning pixels.** Reconcile W1-24 outputs and literal RI-VIS03;
   repair metric/capture/declaration/reference tools; run disable-one red tests, empty-support and
   anti-reference controls. Run RI-VIS09 manifest/routing. Stop numeric tuning on any statistic
   that is not yet the defined statistic.
3. **Establish one shared production foundation.** Prefer an additive, incremental seam over an
   engine rewrite: a canonical material/palette/kit/effect/animation registry and renderer feature
   configuration consumed by existing exterior, interior, actor, place, province and VFX builders.
   Centralise quality defaults and explicit exceptions; preserve deterministic simulation and
   current gameplay contracts. New content must enter through a validated family and declare art
   vocabulary, PBR response, shadow/AO/IBL participation, LOD/stream policy and motion/VFX needs.
4. **Close high-leverage fidelity fundamentals first.** Following RI-VIS04's order, ensure physical
   material response and authored variation; stable directional/contact shadows; ambient/IBL and
   AO separation; exposure/tonemap/colour-space/AA; atmospheric depth and sky; then vegetation
   depth/motion, animation/attachments, streaming/LOD and VFX integration. A feature counts only
   when a screenshot/live sabotage can reveal its absence.
5. **Apply the Black-Marsh language through the same foundation.** Encode palette/value/chroma,
   wet/chitin/resin/bone/root/clay/salt material families, asymmetrical silhouettes, region-specific
   flora/architecture/creatures, settlement composition and mood. Preserve Gideon/Imperial forms as
   deliberate contrast, never the default. Spread WLD05's vocabulary across regions and time rather
   than concentrating it in one showcase.
6. **Sweep the whole existing game once, by family.** Convert/fix shared families before individual
   instances; then inspect every census row and explicitly record each exception. Reuse a single
   capture pool across native metrics and blind-pack construction. Fix systemic gaps before hero
   assets, and avoid speculative breadth that does not close a governing predicate.
7. **Run builder proof and hand off.** Cheap aggregate first, then one bounded live session and
   targeted controls. The critic subsequently pays for full populations, repeated/fresh judgement
   and final aggregation. This ordering allows an invalid tool, dead consumer or failed shared
   family to stop expensive capture work early.

## Falsification audit

The easiest false pass is a beautiful fixed-eight pack backed by a modern renderer while most of the
province still uses legacy flat families; the strongest critic move is therefore to choose unannounced
ordinary surfaces from the census, disable the claimed shared family, and verify both the image metric
and fresh art read worsen. A second high-value attack is to compare every reported RI-VIS03 field to
the item equation and feed it sky-only, resampled, noisy and default-render inputs. If any goes green,
the numerical visual verdict is invalid regardless of how the images look.

The plan's least certain joint is the legal modern reference population: canonical metadata records
large gaps and historically non-equivalent metric statistics. The plan deliberately does not fill
that gap with an invented bar. A reviewer should attack whether each affected item has an exact
fail-closed cap/blocked-score route and whether the allocation still gives the builder enough live
proof without making it pay for the critic's complete populations.
