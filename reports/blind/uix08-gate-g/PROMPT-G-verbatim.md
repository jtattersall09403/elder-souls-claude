THE FIVE QUESTIONS.

You have just played one of the builds. Before you play the other one, answer these
five, in this order, in these words. Answer them about the build you just played.

Record your answers verbatim — what you actually thought, in your own words, before
you have compared anything to anything. Do not tidy them. Do not try to be fair.

1. Did you find out something you were not told to look for? What was it?

2. When you clicked a coloured word, did it feel like following something, or like
   pressing a button?

3. Is there anything in this town you now want to ask someone about? Name it.

4. Would you want to read another one of these conversations?

5. What was the worst thing about it?

There are five questions and there is no sixth. If one of them does not apply to
what you played, say so in your answer to it — that is an answer, and it is a real
one. Do not substitute a question you would rather have been asked.
