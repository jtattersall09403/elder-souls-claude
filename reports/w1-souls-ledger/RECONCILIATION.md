# W1-SOULS-LEDGER — the two soul ledgers, reconciled

Measured at **`9288ebf`**. Every number here is a soul count, a body count, a level, a row count or a percentage — no wall-clock figure appears anywhere.

This closes `GAP-W1-souls-world-copy-of-the-values-is-53pc-stale`, named as the single biggest remaining gap by `corpus/90-verdicts/wave1/W1-SOULS-r3.md` §9.

## The two ledgers

* **TRUTH** — game/data/combat/enemies/*.json .souls — read by game/src/sim/souls.js awardFor() on the alive->dead transition. The only producer of souls in the build.
* **CACHE** — game/data/world/population-posts.json — post.souls plus the report roll-ups, generated by tools/world/build-population.mjs, read at runtime only by game/src/world/population.js report() -> souls_total.

## How the truth was established, rather than assumed

1. READ, not assumed. game/src/sim/souls.js:320 adds awardFor(this.d[e.id], awardHour).souls to sim.progression.soulsHeld, where this.d IS Engine.data.enemies (engine.js:498) = game/data/combat/enemies/*.json. A grep for readers of the cached field over game/src finds exactly one — game/src/world/population.js:294, a report(), not a payer.
1. DEMONSTRATED in the running engine. tools/souls-consumption-ledger.mjs arm E materialises a real population post through the shipping streamer, sets that post cached souls to 3x+7 of its real worth, kills its bodies through the shipping kill path, and the player is paid the statblock sum to the soul (73 claimed, 22 paid, 22 owed).
1. PROVENANCE corroborates. population-posts.json last written at e54a18a (2026-08-07 18:50); the seven re-anchored statblocks written at 0c8acfa (22:36) and e97347f (00:50), i.e. after it. The cache is the older document.
