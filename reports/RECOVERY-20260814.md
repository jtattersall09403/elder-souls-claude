# Recovering the work the banks deleted — 2026-08-14

**What this is.** A forensic sweep of every commit on `codex/wave1-build-experiment` for the signature
`HAZARDS` §2f names — *a commit whose tree does not contain files its own parents contain* — followed
by the recovery of everything that sweep proved was lost by accident rather than removed on purpose.

**Headline.** The famous incident, `06dafd04` (18 files, 22,209 lines), **had already been repaired**
three minutes later by the agent it robbed. It was not the only one, and it was not the worst still
outstanding. **Seventy-four files were still missing from the branch tip when this pass started; all
seventy-four are back.** The largest single group is 39 pieces of visual-truth evidence — including
every one of the 24 `-shipping` frames of the close-orbit see-through study, i.e. exactly the pictures
the owner's "play the game, don't trust a still" directive rests on.

And the disease is **eight days old, not one**. The 08-06/08-10/08-11 restore commits were recorded as
*believed* to be regenerated-index churn. They are not. They restored `game/src/engine.js` twice
(+11,573 and +11,583 lines), nine quest routes, three round-2 verdicts, a dialogue topic, and two
composition matrices of 3,387 and 3,688 lines. That is engine source and measurements, on 08-06.

## 4. What was deliberately removed, and is staying removed

The whole defect is that nothing in a tree distinguishes *deliberately deleted* from *never written on
this disk*. So intent was read from the authoring commit's message, from the surviving successor, or
from the tool that writes the file. Everything here has one of those.

- **`docs/data/cost-ledger.error.json` — 15 sightings, zero restorations.** `tools/cost-refresh.mjs`
  writes it when the cost ledger fails to regenerate and removes it when the run succeeds. Its absence
  *is* the success signal. It has been added and deleted 4 times each. Restoring it would plant a
  false failure on the dashboard. **This is the eighteenth file of `06dafd04`.**
- **`docs/play/**` — 715 files.** `c8414d7f`: *"Pages serves the root now, so the mirror is deleted and
  there is one copy of the game."* Deliberate, and stated.
- **The old W1-30 material library — 35 files** (`*_diff_1k`, `*_nor_gl_1k`, `*_rough_1k`,
  `*_detail_1k`, and `materials/generated/*_256.jpg`). Superseded by `27bdf0a6`'s twenty authored sets
  in the `_albedo_1k` / `_normal_1k` / `_rough_512` convention, with the conversion pipeline written
  into `materials/manifest.json`. `node tools/assets/check-material-coverage.mjs` → **20/20 families,
  281 MB high / 70 MB medium — PASS.**
- **`plastered_stone_wall` and `white_plaster_rough_01`, 6 files** — deleted *after* the rebuild, which
  looked like a loss until `tools/assets/material-library.json` answered it in writing:
  *"plastered_stone_wall, clay_plaster and white_plaster_rough_01 are all plaster: the close-up rig
  showed clay, stone and salt reading as one material, which fails the distinguishability gate before
  a judge ever sees it."* Replaced by `coral_stone_wall`. Deliberate.
- **`corpus/90-verdicts/wave1/artifacts/W1-07/*/trace.jsonl` — 4 files.** Superseded by `.jsonl.gz` of
  the same traces, all present.
- **`corpus/10-combat/RI-CMB07-exemplar-frames-1196-1330.jsonl`.** `RI-CMB07`'s own table names exactly
  two per-frame excerpts, `150-265` and `990-1125`, and both are present. The third was cut.
- **Scratch and sabotage copies** — `.critic-tmp/*` (20), `tmp/probe*`, `tools/harness/_*.mjs`,
  `tools/analysis/.r4probe/*`, `tools/world/w1-04-r4-live.mjs.next`,
  `game/data/camera/rig.json.notloaded`, `reports/run-all-*.json`, `reports/s39/tree-deleted-fix/*`,
  `corpus/70-visual/refs/_provenance-local.json`, `_provenance.json.bak-temporal`, and
  `tools/_vv-vacuous.mjs` — the last being a deliberately-vacuous validator, which `HAZARDS` §3 says

## 6. Method, and how to redo it

```sh
# the signature, per (commit, parent) pair, over every commit reachable from HEAD
for c in $(git rev-list HEAD); do
  for p in $(git rev-list --parents -n1 $c | cut -d' ' -f2-); do
    git diff --diff-filter=D --name-only $p $c        # paths the child's tree lacks
  done
done
# for a merge, the damning subset: those absent from merge-base(parent1, parentN)
# then, decisively: which of them is still absent from HEAD today
```

Restoration was `git cat-file blob <rev>:<path> > <path>` per file, never `git checkout` — the shared
index is not this pass's to touch while a dozen agents hold staged work in it (`HAZARDS` §1). Every
restored file was re-hashed against its source blob afterwards; 74/74 identical, and the two
superseded HUD stills were correctly skipped rather than reverted.

Gates after the restore: `node tools/boot-check.mjs` **PASS**, `node tools/check-data.mjs` **PASS**
(408 NPCs, 310 soul rows agree), `node tools/check-content.mjs` **PASS** (551 quest resolutions),
`node tools/assets/check-material-coverage.mjs` **PASS** (20/20). Every restored JSON parses.

## 7. What this pass could not do

- **It could not prove intent for two files, and says so rather than guessing.**
  `game/src/ui/screens/bindings.js` is imported by nothing and `game/data/npcs/quest-witnesses.json`
  is absent from `game/data/index.json`. Both are restored and flagged; either could legitimately be
  deleted again by its author, and that costs one line.
- **Restoring three builder status files re-registers their path claims.** `land.mjs` reads
  `files_claimed` / `files_touched` from every non-abandoned status file to arbitrate conflicts, so
  W1-11-builder's moveset claims are live again. That is the correct historical record but it does
  nudge conflict resolution. Reversible in one line by marking those records `superseded`; not done
  here, because rewriting another agent's status file to suit a tool is the wrong direction.
- **The 39 recovered visual-truth frames have not been re-verified as depicting what their report
  says.** They are byte-identical to the blobs the capture produced, which is the correct standard for
  a recovery, but nobody has re-run `vt-seethrough.mjs` against the current engine. If the renderer has
  moved since 08-14, the pictures are still the right *evidence of that run* and the wrong *evidence of
  today*.
- **The sweep covers `HEAD`'s ancestry only.** Twenty-odd `worktree-agent-*` branches exist locally and
  were not swept; work that never reached this branch would not appear.
- **`06dafd04`'s first-parent side deleted 15 paths** — the entire HUD pack. 13 are recovered here; the
  other two were regenerated afterwards and their later versions are kept. No further hand audit of the
  08-14 banks was attempted beyond the automated sweep.
- **The HUD pack is 15 images, not 16.** `phone-portrait-390x844-night-minimal` was never captured, so
  its absence is not a loss and nothing was invented to fill the gap.
