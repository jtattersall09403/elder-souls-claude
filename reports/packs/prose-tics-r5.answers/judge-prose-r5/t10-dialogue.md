- B is a set of NPC self-descriptions in which the job explains the person: "In the strict sense my duty is the sequence: what arrived, in what order, and from whom, which is a smaller question than it sounds and a much larger one than the [employer] believes it is paying for."
- B carries a whole caste system in its grammar — the third-person humble "This one buys. This one sells", the honorifics aimed downward at the player ("provincial", "supplicant", "citizen"), and the archivist's flatly contemptuous "a chapel school … and twenty-one years of other people's paperwork, which is a background in the sense that a wall is a view."
- B's throwaway lines are load-bearing: "I was hatched at the third well and I was given to it before I was named. It is done less now than it was done then. The not-doing of it is one of the reasons the sap runs thin" — a demographic decline, a religious practice and a world-state change in three sentences.

WEAKEST POINT: every B speaker lands the same kind of epigram in the same rhythm ("a background in the sense that a wall is a view", "Do you want the cart or the story?"), so B's superiority may be one writer's wit rather than several characters' voices.
